## Escalation Math

**A's clause (Pretend Falls Realty, PFR-ESC 2026):** "$1,000 more than the highest purchase price offered in any other written offer for the Property that Seller receives before accepting this offer, up to a maximum Purchase Price of $440,000." Proof: "a copy of the other offer's price page."

**B's clause (Sample Ridge Brokers, SRB-EA 2025):** "$1,500 more than the highest purchase price in any such offer, up to a maximum Purchase Price of $438,000." Proof: "a copy of the competing offer, with the buyer's names removed." The $8,000 in concessions doesn't change.

Both clauses measure against the other offer's **purchase price** (not a net), and neither excludes any offer. Neither says whether "the highest purchase price" in an offer that escalates means its stated price or its escalated price.

**Reading 1: stated prices only.**
- A: highest other stated price is C's $432,000; $432,000 plus $1,000 = **$433,000** (under $440,000).
- B: highest other stated price is C's $432,000; $432,000 plus $1,500 = **$433,500** (under $438,000 and under its $435,000 letter maximum).
- Result: B $433,500, A $433,000, C $432,000.

**Reading 2: each escalated price counts as the other's competing price.** The two clauses leapfrog: B goes to $433,500, A to $434,500, B to $436,000, A to $437,000, B to its $438,000 cap, then A to $438,000 plus $1,000 = **$439,000** (under $440,000). B can't go higher.
- Result: A $439,000, B $438,000, C $432,000.
- B's $438,000 is **$3,000 above its letter's $435,000 maximum**. A's $439,000 is inside its letter ($440,000 price; at 10% down the loan would be $395,100 against a $396,000 maximum), and A's $440,000 cap equals its letter's maximum, so A can't escalate past what its lender approved.

**What to do with it:** I'm not choosing a reading. Ask your broker how the brokerage treats escalation clauses that point at each other, whether a clause can count another offer's escalated price, and how to share each buyer's proof (A wants the other offer's price page; B wants the whole competing offer, names removed). The sellers aren't obliged to accept an escalating offer; the final price should be written up the way your broker directs. I'm not rewriting either clause.
