# Utah: MLS Remarks and Advertising Rules

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Sources at the end. Use this file only in Utah mode (the property is in Utah). It states only what the sources below say. Anything they don't cover, such as the Public Remarks character limit, is "confirm with your MLS." This is not legal advice; questions go to the broker.

## UtahRealEstate.com: Public Remarks and Agent Remarks

UtahRealEstate.com (the Wasatch Front MLS) explains its rules in its Rules and Regulations Reference Guide (revised February 11, 2026). On remarks:

- **Public Remarks** "is intended for marketing the property to consumers, and may include information such as a description of the property's features, a description of the property's physical characteristics, information about the neighborhood, etc." It is public: it shows on UtahRealEstate.com and on brokers' and agents' IDX websites.
- **Agent Remarks** is "a member-only field," for "contact information, showing instructions, information about the occupancy status of the property, any required disclosures about the sale or the property, etc."
- **Never in Public Remarks** (Agent Remarks only):
  - Any contact information, which "includes, but is not limited to": any name, "such as the name of the broker, agent, office assistant, brokerage, office, team, homebuilder, homeowner, etc."; a telephone number; an email address; a broker, brokerage or agent website address (Rule 1.24)
  - "Website addresses, including links to virtual tours"
  - "Showing instructions, which includes lockbox information and alarm, gate, or other security codes"
  - "Property occupancy status (i.e. vacant or tenant occupied)"
- **Never in any remarks:** anything "which could be considered disparaging to other MLS members or brokerages" (Rule 1.24).
- **Fair housing:** every listing must comply with the federal Fair Housing Act, and "any remarks which may violate Fair Housing Guidelines must not be included on any listing" (Rule 20.5).
- **Compensation:** offers of compensation are not allowed on the MLS (Rule 5.0.1), including a link to a site that shows them (Rules 5.0.1 and 5.0.2). **Seller concessions to the buyer** (closing costs, repair or update allowances) may be advertised in Agent Remarks and/or Public Remarks.
- **Accuracy:** "all information included on a listing must be accurate and cannot be misleading," and the rules prohibit entering information the listing broker or agent knows to be inaccurate (Rules 1.2.1 and 20.4(e)). A member who learns of an inaccuracy has to fix it (Rules 1.2.1 and 8.2).

What this means for the drafts in Utah mode:
- The Public Remarks draft names no person, brokerage, team, builder or owner, and holds no phone number, email, website or virtual tour link.
- No showing, lockbox or occupancy wording, even rephrased ("easy to show," "vacant and ready").
- A seller concession goes in only when the inputs state it, exactly as stated. Compensation never does.
- **Character limit:** the reference guide doesn't give one. Use the limit the agent gives, or the one their listing input screen shows; otherwise flag it: "Confirm the Public Remarks limit in UtahRealEstate.com's listing input screen, or ask Member Services at 801-676-5400, Ext. 2."

## UtahRealEstate.com: Public Marketing Starts a Clock

- Under the Clear Cooperation and Public Marketing Rule (Rule 1.0.2), "if a property is publicly marketed (via websites, a yard sign, social media, email blasts, etc.), a listing for that property must be added to the MLS within one (1) business day of the first public marketing of that property." This overrides the usual five business days to enter a listing.
- If the seller filed an MLS Listing Exclusion Notice, "the listing cannot be publicly marketed in any way (i.e. a sign in the yard, on social media, via a flyer, etc.) unless or until the listing is published on the MLS."
- The rule doesn't apply to "To Be Built" or "Under Construction" listings, but does apply to a built new construction home that is ready to be sold, when listed with an MLS member brokerage.
- **For the drafts:** when the inputs show the listing isn't on the MLS yet (a go-live date in the future, a "coming soon" sign, a private or office-exclusive plan), add a flag: posting the caption, or putting up a sign, is public marketing, so the listing has to be on the MLS within one business day after it. The agent confirms the timing with the broker.

## UtahRealEstate.com: Another Agent's Listing on Social Media

If the agent asks for a caption for a listing that isn't theirs, the reference guide says an ad for another agent's listing on social media must display "minimal information," be "200 characters or less," and link to UtahRealEstate.com or a compliant IDX website with the required disclosures (Rule 18). Keep that caption to 200 characters and leave a [Link to the listing on UtahRealEstate.com or your IDX site] placeholder. A mailer with another agent's listing needs the listing broker's permission (Rule 2.7).

## Utah Law: Advertising by Licensees

Utah Code 61-2f-401(12) (version effective October 1, 2026) makes these grounds for discipline, "at the time of placing an advertisement":
- "(a) advertising the availability of real estate or the services of a licensee in a false, misleading, or deceptive manner"
- "(b) failing to include within the advertisement the brokerage name with which a person who is licensed is affiliated or, if the advertisement is placed online or in a digital format, a link to a website or media platform that identifies the brokerage name within one click"

**For the drafts:** the social caption ends with the agent's name and brokerage name ([Agent Name], [Brokerage Name] when the inputs don't give them), so the brokerage name is in the post itself. Public Remarks still name no brokerage (the MLS rule above). Team names and logos: confirm with the broker.

## Utah Fair Housing Classes

The Utah Fair Housing Act (Utah Code 57-21-5(2), effective May 6, 2026) makes it a discriminatory housing practice to make, print, circulate, publish or post "any notice, statement, or advertisement ... that directly or indirectly expresses any preference, limitation, or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, source of income, disability, sexual orientation, or gender identity." That is the federal list plus source of income, sexual orientation and gender identity. Review every draft and every piece of supplied wording against all ten.
