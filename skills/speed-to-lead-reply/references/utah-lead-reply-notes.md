# Utah Notes for New Lead Replies

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Utah only: read this when the property is in Utah. Outside Utah, skip it and say "State rules were not checked." Not legal advice; questions go to the broker.

## Touring: The Written Buyer Agreement

- **NAR's practice change:** since August 17, 2024, NAR's MLS policy requires an MLS participant "working with" a buyer to sign a written agreement with that buyer "prior to touring a home," "including both in-person and live virtual tours" (NAR, Written Buyer Agreements 101; MLS Policy Statement 8.13). Speaking with a buyer or marketing services is not "working with" (NAR, Consumer Guide to Open Houses and Written Agreements).
- **Utah's rule:** a licensee must "define the scope of the individual's agency" by executing "a written agency agreement" with each principal, including "buyers the individual represents" (Utah Admin. Code R162-2f-401a(2), amended effective May 25, 2022). The rule does not set a time for signing. The before-touring timing comes from NAR's MLS policy.
- **The form:** Utah has no state-approved buyer agreement. The Utah Association of REALTORS says having its Exclusive Buyer-Broker Agreement and Agency Disclosure signed before showing a property satisfies the written agreement requirement. Use the version in the brokerage's forms library.
- **What the skill does:** no draft promises a showing, a day or a time. A draft that mentions a showing says the agreement comes first, in plain words, with no compensation, term or form name. Check before sending carries one line: "Before any showing, sign your brokerage's current buyer agreement (in Utah, usually UAR's Exclusive Buyer-Broker Agreement and Agency Disclosure); confirm with your broker."

## A Lead Who Has an Agent

- A licensee may not "enter or attempt to enter into a concurrent agency representation when the licensee knows or should know that the principal has an existing agency representation agreement with another licensee" (Utah Admin. Code R162-2f-401b(1)(k)).
- A lead who says they're working with an agent is a reason to know. So the reply is polite and offers nothing: no help, showing, call, meeting, listings or search. Ethics questions go to the broker.

## Calls and Texts on Sundays, Legal Holidays and at Night

- Utah's Telephone and Facsimile Solicitation Act: a person "may not make a telephone solicitation to a residential telephone or cellular telephone without prior express consent" between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. local time, "on a Sunday," or "on a legal holiday" (Utah Code 13-25a-103(3), effective May 4, 2022).
- A "telephone solicitation" is "the initiation of a telephone call or message for a commercial purpose," including calls "encouraging the purchase or rental of, or investment in, property" or "encouraging the person called to sell real or personal property" (13-25a-102(8), effective May 1, 2024).
- The act does not define "prior express consent." Whether a lead's own inquiry counts as consent to a call on a Sunday or a holiday was not confirmed for this file. Ask the broker.
- A caller making an "unsolicited telephone call" must identify themselves, the business, and the purpose of the call promptly (13-25a-103(5)). A call "in response to an express request of the person called," or to someone with an established business relationship (one based on an inquiry, among other things), is not an "unsolicited telephone call" (13-25a-102(4) and (10)). The call opener names the agent, the brokerage and the reason in its first sentence either way.
- The same section requires following the federal TCPA (13-25a-103(4)); see `outreach-rules.md`.
- **Licensee exemption.** Sections 13-25a-103 and 13-25a-108 "do not apply to a person who holds and acts within the scope of a license" issued by the Utah Division of Real Estate (Utah Code 13-25a-111, effective May 1, 2024). So for a licensed agent acting as one, Utah's own evening, Sunday and holiday limits may not apply. The skill keeps the Sunday and holiday care in this file anyway, as house practice. The federal TCPA still applies (`outreach-rules.md`), and whether a message is within the license is the broker's call.

What the skill does (house practice for a licensed agent, since 13-25a-111 exempts a licensee acting within the license from 13-25a-103):
- Texts and calls go between 8 AM and 8 PM anyway, inside Utah's window.
- On a Sunday or a Utah legal holiday, a text to a TEXT OK lead keeps its day (the lead's written consent covers texts), and so does a call when the same consent line covers calls.
- Any other call or voicemail that would land on a Sunday or a Utah legal holiday moves to the next day that is neither, and the output says why in one line. Emails keep their day.
- When today itself is a Sunday or a Utah legal holiday, Flags says so first: the email now, the text if TEXT OK, and the call on the next weekday unless the broker says the inquiry counts as consent.

## Utah Legal Holidays

The solicitation act doesn't define "legal holiday." Utah's list is in Utah Code 63G-1-301 (version effective May 6, 2026):
- Every Sunday.
- January 1; the third Monday of January; the third Monday of February; Good Friday; Easter Sunday; the last Monday of May; June 19; July 4; July 24; the first Monday of September; the second Monday of October; November 11; the fourth Thursday of November; December 25; and any day of fasting or thanksgiving proclaimed by the president or the governor.
- A fixed-date holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed the Friday before, and one on a Sunday the Monday after. (June 19 has its own shift rules in the 2026 version; from January 1, 2027 it follows the same rule as the others.)
- The next ones: Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (Veterans Day); Thursday, November 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving); Friday, December 25, 2026; Friday, January 1, 2027; Monday, January 18, 2027; Monday, February 15, 2027.

## Schools

- For a schools question, point to the school district's own boundary map and to the Utah School Report Card from the Utah State Board of Education (reportcard.schools.utah.gov), with no opinion, no school names and no ratings.

## Fair Housing

Utah's protected classes add source of income, sexual orientation and gender identity to the federal list (Utah Code 57-21-5). See "Fair Housing" in `lead-reply-guide.md`.
