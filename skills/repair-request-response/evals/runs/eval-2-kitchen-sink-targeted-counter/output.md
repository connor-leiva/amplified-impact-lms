How the deadline works on this form:
- **There is no Response Period.** Under Section 12.1(b), by the Due Diligence Deadline the buyer must either cancel in writing (the $4,000 Earnest Money Deposit goes back to the buyer) or "resolve in writing with Seller" the objections. Under Section 12.1(c), if neither happens, the Due Diligence Condition is waived.
- The deadline for a signed written resolution and the end of the buyer's cancellation window are the same moment: 5:00 PM Mountain Time on November 10, 2026.
- If the buyer's addendum lapses Monday, the buyer still has until Tuesday at 5:00 PM to resolve or cancel. The sellers' counter needs its own acceptance time early enough for the buyer to sign before then. Acceptance happens when the signed counter is Received by the other side (Sections 22 and 23).
- If a signed agreement isn't possible by Tuesday at 5:00 PM, more time needs a written extension signed by all parties before the deadline (Section 25). Talk to your broker before offering one.
- The Section 25 weekend rule moves only earnest money dates and the Settlement Deadline, not the Due Diligence Deadline.
