- **There is no Response Period.** By the Due Diligence Deadline the buyer must either cancel in writing (earnest money back to the buyer) or "resolve in writing with Seller" the objections; otherwise the Due Diligence Condition is waived.
- So the deadline for a signed written resolution and the end of the buyer's cancellation window are the same: 5:00 PM Mountain Time on [Due Diligence Deadline]. There is no seven-day period plus three days.
