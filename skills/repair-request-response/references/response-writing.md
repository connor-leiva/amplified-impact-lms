# Writing the Response

Last verified: 2026-11-08

Four drafts: the response terms, the seller email, the note to the buyer's agent and the disclosure note. Then "Check before sending."

## Response Terms for the Addendum

The agent puts these on the approved form the brokerage uses. In Utah that is a state-approved addendum (see `utah-repair-response.md` and `utah-forms-and-counters.md`). These are standard terms for repairs, credits, price, evaluations, declines and deadline extensions. Anything else, such as new obligations, releases, indemnities or conditions on other deadlines, goes to the broker. This is not legal advice.

First decide the frame:
- **The seller agrees to everything as written:** no new terms are needed. The seller accepts the buyer's addendum before its acceptance deadline. Say so, and give that deadline.
- **Anything else:** a counter addendum. It lists what the seller agrees to, says the remaining items are declined, and gives the buyer an acceptance deadline that leaves time to sign before 5:00 PM Mountain Time on the Due Diligence Deadline (in Utah).

Rules for each term:
- **One item, one remedy, one reference.** Name the finding, cite the report page and item, and state one remedy.
- **Licensed work, with proof.** Repairs by a licensed contractor in the relevant trade, with paid invoices delivered to the buyer. Radon by a certified radon mitigation professional, with a post-mitigation test.
- **A completion date the transactions team can track.** Tie it to the Settlement Deadline ("no later than five calendar days before the Settlement Deadline") instead of "before closing."
- **Real numbers only.** A credit or price amount comes from a bid or a share of a bid, with the arithmetic shown in the analysis. Without a bid, write "[credit amount from bid]."
- **Neutral language.** No adjectives, no blame, no reasons in the terms themselves.
- **Name what's declined** by the buyer's item numbers, so nothing is left ambiguous.

Example language. Replace every bracket with what the documents say.

1. Seller agrees to have a licensed HVAC contractor repair the furnace condition noted in item [7.2] on page [7] of the [inspection company] report dated [date] so the furnace operates safely, and to provide Buyer the contractor's written report and paid invoices no later than five calendar days before the Settlement Deadline.
2. Seller agrees to have a certified radon mitigation professional install a radon mitigation system and to provide Buyer a post-mitigation test showing a radon level below 4.0 pCi/L no later than five calendar days before the Settlement Deadline.
3. Seller agrees to have a licensed [trade] contractor evaluate [condition] noted in item [X] on page [X] of the inspection report and to complete the repairs the contractor identifies as needed for safe operation, with the contractor's written findings and paid invoices provided to Buyer no later than five calendar days before the Settlement Deadline.
4. Seller agrees to contribute $[amount from bid] at Settlement toward Buyer's closing costs, in place of Buyer's items [numbers] in Addendum No. [X]. (On the November 1, 2026 Utah REPC, write it as a change to the Section 2.2 amount, from $[current] to $[new].)
5. The Purchase Price is reduced from $[current] to $[new].
6. Seller declines Buyer's items [numbers] in Addendum No. [X].
7. The Due Diligence Deadline is extended to [date]. (Only if the seller chooses it with the broker; all parties must sign before the current deadline.)

## Seller Email

Written as the agent, in first person, to the seller. Warm, plain and direct. Follow the user's voice profile if one is in the conversation.
- Subject in Title Case, such as "The Buyer's Repair Request On 123 Main St: My Recommendation"
- Open with a bolded bottom line: the recommendation, what it costs from bids, and the decision needed and by when (a time before the buyer's request lapses or the Due Diligence Deadline, whichever is first)
- **What They Asked For:** one or two sentences (how many items, what kinds)
- **What I Recommend:** the items to agree to and the items to push back on, one sentence each, with the reason in property terms
- **What It Costs:** bid amounts only; name the items that still need bids
- **Your Options:** two or three, one line of tradeoff each
- **One More Thing:** one plain sentence that what the inspection found is now known, so if this sale doesn't close, it goes on the disclosure for the next buyer
- **Next Step:** what you need from the seller and by when
- Under 300 words, short paragraphs, no em dashes. Count the words before you finish; if it runs long, cut What I Recommend down to the decisions and drop any reason the bottom line already gives
- Never characterize the buyers ("picky," "unreasonable," "first-timers"). Describe the request and the house.
- None of "stunning," "dream home," "don't hesitate to reach out"
- Sign with the user's name if known; otherwise [Agent Name]

## Note to the Buyer's Agent

A short cover note that goes with the response. 80 to 150 words, Title Case subject.
- The response in one or two sentences, its acceptance deadline, and that the addendum is attached
- The facts behind any declined item, from the documents only: the report's own recommendation, the disclosure page and date, "the report notes the system was not tested"
- Any documentation you need (the full report pages, where an item was documented)
- Never include the seller's priorities, timing, finances, motivation or bottom line, and never hint at what else the seller might accept
- Professional and neutral, no editorializing, signed [Agent Name]

## Disclosure Note

For the agent and the seller's file.
- List every condition the seller now knows from the report and the request, including findings the buyer didn't request, with page references.
- Where the disclosure says "no known" and the report shows evidence, note both facts neutrally.
- State: if this sale doesn't close, the seller's disclosures to the next buyer must reflect any material defect the seller now knows about. Repairs finished with paid invoices can be disclosed as repaired, with the invoices.
- Utah: the duty and its sources are in `utah-repair-response.md`. Outside Utah: "State disclosure rules weren't checked; confirm with your broker."
- Which findings count as material defects is the broker's call; legal questions go to a real estate attorney.

## Check Before Sending

List every date, time, dollar amount, page and item number, quoted finding and quoted disclosure that appears in the response terms, the seller email and the note to the buyer's agent. Give the source of each (the document and page, the bid line, or the agent's message) so the agent can verify it. List every placeholder still open.
