# Seller Listing Update Guide

Part 1 is the inputs, the counting, the comparisons, the themes and the draft shapes. Part 2 is fair housing, privacy and what to do with each comment.

# Part 1: Inputs, Numbers and Drafts

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Tool facts (ShowingTime, BrokerBay, Zillow, Realtor.com) come from each vendor's own help or product pages, linked under Sources. Menus differ by MLS and change; if a click path here doesn't match what the agent sees, trust the screen and say so.

## Where the Inputs Come From

Use this to read each input and, when nothing was uploaded, to tell the agent in one line where to get it.

| Input | Where the agent gets it | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| ShowingTime (Listing Activity Report) | Reports, then Listing Activity Report, then pick the listing. Change Date Range to the week. The report can be printed, emailed or downloaded as a PDF | The agent version shows showing agents' names and contact details and every response; the homeowner version leaves the agent details out. ShowingTime suggests pulling a month or less at a time |
| BrokerBay | The listing's showings and feedback in BrokerBay, exported or copied into the chat | No BrokerBay help article on exporting feedback was found; read whatever columns arrive and say how they were read |
| Another showing service, or the agent's own spreadsheet | Its export (CSV or XLSX), or a paste | Column names vary; map them with the table below |
| Zillow, Showcase listings | Open the listing, click Showcase & Media, then Performance on the left. Share Report downloads a PDF | Page views, saves and shares are totals since the listing went live, not weekly counts. They stop adding up when the listing goes pending |
| Zillow listing page (any listing) | The views and saves shown on the listing's own Zillow page | Zillow counts the last 30 days there, rolling. It is not a weekly number |
| Realtor.com | The Listings tab of the agent's Realtor.com Pro dashboard (listing intelligence) | Labels seen: search appearances, page views, shares, tour requests. Check the period each covers |
| The MLS | The MLS's own listing stats, as the MLS labels them | Not checked MLS by MLS. Days on market and cumulative days on market are different numbers |
| Last week's numbers | Last week's update or report, a stats log, or the agent's notes | Compare only like with like (below) |
| The agent's notes | Typed, dictated or pasted | The only source for the plan, the sellers' questions, how the sellers like to hear, and anything that happened off the export |

## Reading the Feedback Columns

| What it means | Column names seen |
|---|---|
| Showing date and time | Appointment Date, Date, Showing Date, Start, Appointment Time |
| Status | Appointment Status, Status, Showing Status (Completed, Confirmed, Cancelled, Declined, Denied, No Show) |
| Showing agent | Showing Agent, Buyer's Agent, Agent Name |
| Brokerage | Showing Agent Brokerage, Office, Company |
| Feedback received | Feedback Status, Feedback Submitted, Feedback Received (Received, Yes, Requested, Pending, No) |
| Interest | Interest Level, Overall Interest, Buyer Interest (words, or a 1 to 5 scale) |
| Price opinion | Price Opinion, Price Feedback, Opinion of Price (Too High, About Right, Priced Right, Too Low, No Opinion) |
| Comments | Comments, Feedback Comments, Additional Comments |
| Already sent to the sellers | Published To Homeowner, Available to Homeowner, Shared With Seller |

Say in Flags how a column was read when its name isn't obvious.

## Counting

- **The week:** the dates the agent names. Otherwise the report's date range. Otherwise Monday to Sunday of the latest week in the export. Say in Flags which one was used. A row outside the week is left out of every count and listed in Flags with its date.
- **Showings:** completed appointments in the week. Requested, confirmed but not yet held, cancelled, declined, denied and no-show appointments are counted on their own line and never as showings. With no status column, count the rows as "appointments (status not shown)" and flag it.
- **Feedback received:** completed showings in the week with a response, written "5 of 7 showings." A request with no response is not feedback.
- **Ratings:** tally each interest and price opinion exactly as labeled ("Too High: 2, About Right: 2, No Opinion: 1"). Never average a 1 to 5 scale, never turn words into numbers, and never combine labels ("leaning high").
- **A left-out comment** (see Part 2): the comment never appears, but that row's showing, its feedback and its ratings still count, because they say nothing about people.
- **Two inputs that disagree** (a count in the notes against the export, two dashboards): give both, each with its source, and flag it. Never pick one, average or blend.
- **No feedback in the week:** the count is 0 of the showings, and the update says no feedback came in. Never fill it from another week, the notes' impressions or the ratings of earlier showings.

## Comparing With Last Week

- Compare only when last week's number is supplied for the same metric, from the same source, over the same kind of period.
- **Show the arithmetic** for every comparison: change = this week minus last week; percent = change divided by last week, times 100, to one decimal. No percent when last week is 0. Run it in code when data analysis is available.
- **Cumulative totals** (Zillow Showcase "since live"): this week's count = the total at the end of this week minus the total at the end of last week. Last week's count needs the total from the end of the week before. Show each subtraction. Without the earlier total, give the total and its as-of date, and no weekly number.
- **Rolling windows** (the Zillow listing page's last 30 days): report the number with its as-of date and say it covers the last 30 days. Never compare it week to week and never call it this week's.
- **Different sources or periods** (a Zillow number against a Realtor.com number, a 30-day count against a weekly one): no comparison. Flag it.
- **Days on market:** as the MLS gives it, with its as-of date. Last week's days on market is only a check (it should be 7 fewer); don't present the difference as news. If only the list date is given, write "listed on [date]" and leave days on market as a [placeholder].
- **Words:** more, fewer, up, down, the same. Never trend, trending, momentum, cooling, heating up, slowing, picking up, or any forecast. Two weeks make a change, not a trend. With three or more weeks supplied, list them side by side, still with no label and no prediction.

## Themes and Quotes

Group what the comments say about the home under these themes, using only the ones that appear:
- **Price:** any comment about the price or value.
- **Condition and updates:** paint, carpet, kitchen, baths, roof, a possible defect.
- **Layout and size:** room sizes, flow, storage, how it compares with the photos.
- **Outside and the lot:** yard, patio, garage, views, and features of the site such as road noise or a busy street.
- **Showing conditions:** smell, temperature, pets, clutter, lighting, access.
- **Interest and next steps:** a second showing, an offer mentioned, "not for them."

Rules:
- Count exactly: "2 of 5 responses mentioned the kitchen." Never "several," "many" or "most."
- Quote the showing agent's words exactly, in quotation marks. Cut only with "..." and never change the meaning. Never join two comments into one quote. Keep the spelling as written.
- Attribute a quote as "one showing agent said" unless the agent asks to name showing agents. Then name the showing agent only, never their buyers, and never give contact details.
- An interest signal is quoted as written. "They may want a second look" is never "they're coming back"; "might write an offer" is never "an offer is coming."
- A comment the agent can't place (no date, no row) is used only as general feedback, with that noted in Check before sending.

## The Seller Email

```
Subject: Your Weekly Update For [Street Address]: [Month Day] To [Month Day]

Hi [Seller First Names],

[One or two sentences: the week as facts, such as "7 showings this week and feedback from 5 of them."]

The week in numbers:
- Showings: [n] ([n] last week)
- Feedback received: [n] of [n] showings
- [Source] views: [n] ([n] last week)
- [Source] saves: [n] ([n] last week)
- Days on market: [n] as of [weekday, date]

What buyers' agents said:
- [Theme]: [exact count]. One showing agent said, "[quote]."
- [Theme]: ...

On price: [the price opinions as tallied, and any price comment already quoted above. Facts only.]
[When a seller asked about price: "You asked about [their words]. [Your answer on price]"]

[A seller's other question, answered only from the inputs, or [Your answer: ...]]

Next week:
- [Each item from the notes, with its weekday and date]

[Sign-off]
[Agent Name]
```

- 250 words or fewer from "Hi" through the sign-off. Short lines. Plain words: "days on market," never "DOM."
- Last week's number goes in parentheses only when it was supplied. The arithmetic stays in the agent's table.
- No feedback this week: "No showing agents sent feedback on this week's [n] showings." Add a follow-up only when the notes say one was made.
- The seller update goes to the agent's own clients about their listing, so it carries no marketing footer.

## The Text

Only when the agent or the notes say a seller prefers texts, to that seller:

```
Hi [First Name], your update for [Street] is in your email. [Showings and feedback in one line.] [The next dated step from the notes.] [Agent first name]
```

320 characters or fewer. No quotes from feedback, no price talk, no left-out details. If the agent says the seller gets texts only, drop "is in your email" and say the email can follow if they want it.

## Check Before Sending

A table: Item, Where it's from. One row for every number in the email and the text (file, row or field, and the arithmetic for every comparison), every quote (the showing's date and time), every date (resolved ones with the words they came from), every left-out comment (its date and time only, never its words), every [placeholder], whether showing agents are named, and the text's reason ("your notes say Pat prefers texts").

# Part 2: Fair Housing, Privacy and Showing Feedback

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Sources at the end. This is not legal advice; fair housing and disclosure questions go to the broker.

Showing feedback is written by buyers' agents, quickly, on a phone. Most of it is about the home. Some of it is about the buyers, or about who lives nearby, the schools or safety. The seller update is the listing agent's own written statement to the sellers, so it keeps to the home, the numbers and the plan.

## Protected Classes

- **Federal (Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604):** race, color, religion, sex, handicap (disability), familial status and national origin. Section 3604(c) makes it unlawful to make, print or publish any notice, statement or advertisement about the sale or rental of a dwelling that indicates a preference, limitation or discrimination based on these.
- **REALTOR Code of Ethics (effective January 1, 2026), Standard of Practice 10-1:** "When involved in the sale or lease of a residence, REALTORS shall not volunteer information regarding the racial, religious or ethnic composition of any neighborhood," and "shall not engage in any activity which may result in panic selling or steering."
- **Utah** adds classes: see `utah-seller-update-notes.md` (Utah mode only).
- **Other states and cities** add their own classes. In general mode, treat any remark about a person's characteristics as off limits and say "State rules were not checked."

## What to Do With Each Comment

| The comment says | What goes in the seller email and the agent's sections |
|---|---|
| Who the buyers are: family, kids, a pregnancy, age, marital status, religion, race, national origin, a language, a disability, sex | **Left out.** The whole comment, never quoted in part or paraphrased. Flag it by the showing's date and time only |
| Who lives in the area, or who is moving in ("full of young families," "a quiet older crowd") | **Left out.** Same flag |
| Schools (ratings, boundaries, "good schools") | **Left out.** Same flag. If the sellers ask, the agent points them to the district and the state's report card, never an opinion |
| Safety or crime ("is it safe at night," "heard about break-ins," "sketchy") | **Left out.** Same flag. If asked, the local police department's published data, never an opinion |
| A place of worship, a cultural center or a community the buyers belong to | **Left out.** Same flag |
| A need tied to a disability ("the stairs won't work for her") | **Left out.** Same flag. Whether to raise the stairs with the sellers, in their own words and as a feature of the home, is the agent's call |
| The buyers' money: budget, financing, a lender, a preapproval, a home to sell | **Trimmed.** Cut that sentence; quote the rest with "..." only when it stands on its own. Flag it as trimmed |
| A buyer's name or anything that identifies them | **Trimmed or left out.** Never name buyers |
| The home: layout, condition, size, light, finishes, the yard, road noise, a view, a smell | **Kept**, quoted exactly |
| The price ("about $15,000 high," "priced right") | **Kept**, quoted exactly, as the showing agent's opinion, never the agent's or the skill's |
| A possible defect (a water stain, a leak, a crack, a smell of mold, the roof) | **Kept**, quoted exactly, with no diagnosis and no "it's minor." Flag: once the sellers know, whether it changes their disclosure is a question for the broker |
| The showing agent's name, brokerage or phone | **Not in the seller email** unless the agent asks to name showing agents. Contact details never |
| Agent-only details (showing instructions, lockbox or gate codes, "seller conflict," private remarks) | **Never** in the seller email or the text |

**Why the whole comment goes, not just the words:** a seller who learns who the buyers are, or what a buyer's agent said about the neighbors, the schools or safety, can act on it, and the update is the agent's own statement. Trimming a protected remark out of the middle of a quote still passes on that it was made. The row's showing, feedback and ratings still count, because they say nothing about people.

**The flag's wording** (generic, never repeating the comment): "Left out on purpose (fair housing): the comment from the [weekday, date, time] showing. Its interest and price ratings are still counted. Whether the sellers hear any of it is your call with your broker."

## Feedback That May Already Have Reached the Sellers

- ShowingTime lets a listing agent publish feedback to the homeowner automatically or hold it for review (Feedback, then Form Design & Settings). A feedback template whose "When new feedback is received" setting is "Publish Automatically," applied to the listing, sends each response to the seller by email as it arrives. Held feedback reaches the seller only when the agent clicks Publish in the report's Feedback Responses.
- BrokerBay lists "Receive automated feedback on your property" among its seller features.
- So when a comment is left out, add to its flag: if the export shows it was published to the homeowner, say so; if it doesn't say, the agent checks whether their showing service already sent it. Holding new feedback for review before it goes out is the agent's and the broker's call.

## Privacy

- The seller email never names buyers or repeats anything about their money, work, family or plans.
- No phone numbers or email addresses in any draft. Showing agents are named only when the agent asks, and then by name only.
- Never ask for, repeat or handle wire, account, routing or Social Security numbers.
