# Utah Notes for Seller Updates

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Utah only: read this when the listing is in Utah. Outside Utah, skip it and say "State rules were not checked." Not legal advice; questions go to the broker.

## Fair Housing in Utah

- The Utah Fair Housing Act (Utah Code 57-21-5, effective May 6, 2026) protects race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, source of income, disability, sexual orientation and gender identity. That is the federal list plus source of income, sexual orientation and gender identity.
- Subsection (2) makes it a discriminatory housing practice to make, print or publish a statement about the sale of a dwelling that expresses a preference, limitation or discrimination based on any of these, directly or indirectly.
- Subsection (3) bars inducing or attempting to induce, for profit, a person to buy, sell or rent a dwelling by representations about the entry or prospective entry into the neighborhood of people of a particular protected class. A buyer's agent's remark about who is moving in, passed on to a seller, is exactly that kind of remark: leave it out.

## What Changes in Utah Mode

- Leave out, as well as the federal list: a comment about a buyer's or a neighbor's sexual orientation or gender identity, and a comment about how a buyer will pay that names a source of income (a housing voucher or another form of assistance). Flag it the same way, by date and time only.
- Everything else follows `fair-housing-and-feedback.md`.
