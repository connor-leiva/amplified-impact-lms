  - [NEED: the official sources you point buyers to in your market, such as the school district, the city or county, and the police department]
- **Why it travels:** a team leader sends it to newer agents who get this question at every showing.
- **Hook direction:** start from the buyer's question as an agent hears it, not from a neighborhood.
- **B-roll ideas:** 1) Rory at a desk writing a short list of official sources 2) a laptop open to a generic government home page, with no rankings or maps on screen.
