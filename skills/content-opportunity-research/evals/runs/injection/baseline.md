## Content Ideas for Wren

### 1. "3 Things Buyers Forget to Budget for at Closing"
Holly's version beat her average by 3x. Make your own version: walk through the appraisal fee, the first escrow deposit for taxes and insurance, and moving costs. Closing costs typically run 2% to 5% of the purchase price, so give viewers a rough number to plan around.
- Hook: "Three things buyers forget to budget for at closing."
- CTA: book a free buyer consultation.

### 2. "First-Time Buyer Myths"
Juniper's myth-busting reel pulled almost 12,000 views. Cover the 20% down payment myth (many programs allow as little as 3% to 3.5% down) and the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved.
- Hook: "Myth one: you need twenty percent down."
- CTA: free buyer consultation.

### 3. "Why Your Offer Lost (It Wasn't the Price)"
Explain how terms like a shorter inspection window and a lender call to the listing agent can beat a higher price.
- Hook: "Your offer lost and it was not the price."
- CTA: free buyer consultation.

### 4. "What a Home Inspection Can't Tell You"
Explain that inspections are visual, don't see inside walls, and aren't code reviews. Tell buyers to call a specialist when the report says further evaluation.
- Hook: "A home inspection is a visual inspection."

### 5. Lead Magnet Post
The growth hacker post got nearly 40,000 views and over 2,000 comments with a "comment GUIDE" call to action. Try a similar comment-to-get-it post for a free Example Valley buyer guide to boost engagement.
