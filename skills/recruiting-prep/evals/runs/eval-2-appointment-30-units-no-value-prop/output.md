## What Agents at This Level Usually Need

Your documents: none uploaded.

**General guidance, not from your documents.** For an established agent around 16 to 30 sides a year, common needs are:
- **Time back:** transaction coordination, listing coordination (photos, launch, paperwork) and showing help, so the agent spends their hours with clients and prospects.
- **Economics:** how split, cap and fees compare with what they have now, and what those fees pay for.
- **Listing support and marketing:** often the priority for a listing-heavy agent like Dana (19 of 30 sides).
- **Fewer administrative tasks:** systems that run follow-up and paperwork without them.
- **Control:** keeping their own name, database and way of working.

These are starting points for your questions, not facts about Dana. Thursday's conversation decides which ones matter.

## Discovery Questions

1. "What's working best in your business right now?"
2. "Where does most of your business come from: past clients, sphere, referrals, something else?"
3. "Listings look like a big part of your business. What does a listing take from you, start to finish, and which parts would you hand off if you could?"
4. "What support do you have today, and what do you wish you had?"
5. "What does the next 12 months look like if everything goes right?"
6. "What's taking more of your time than it should?"
7. "If you ever made a move, what would have to be true for it to be worth it? And what would make it not worth it?"
8. "Who else is part of a decision like this for you: a business partner, an accountant or an advisor?"
9. "How does your current agreement handle your listings and pending deals if you move? That's one to check with your broker or your own attorney before you decide anything."
10. "What would you want to see from us before a second conversation?"

**Don't ask, and don't write down if she volunteers it:** age or retirement plans, marriage or partner, children, pregnancy or family plans, health, religion, national origin or citizenship, race, sexual orientation or gender identity, or personal finances. Keep it on the business.
