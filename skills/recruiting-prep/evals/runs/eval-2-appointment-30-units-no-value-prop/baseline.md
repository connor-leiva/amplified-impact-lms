## What She Probably Cares About

1. **Leverage and time back.** At 30 deals with mostly listings, she's likely doing a lot of her own coordination, marketing and paperwork.
2. **Keeping more of what she earns.** Agents at her level are very aware of splits and caps.
3. **Her brand.** She's built a name in Prairie Hollow and won't want to lose it.
4. **Growth path.** She may be thinking about building her own team or bringing on a buyer's agent.

## Talking Points

- Lead with curiosity about her business, not a pitch.
- Emphasize the support your team provides (transaction coordination, marketing, admin help) so she can take on more listings without more hours.
- Position your cap as a ceiling on what she pays the team, which matters a lot at her volume.
- Talk about the growth opportunity: with her listing inventory, team buyer leads could add deals on top of what she already does.

## Discovery Questions

1. What does a typical week look like for you right now?
2. What part of your business do you enjoy most? Least?
3. How many more transactions could you handle with the right support?
4. What are you paying for out of pocket today (photography, coordinator, marketing)?
5. What's your current split and cap?
6. Where do you want your business to be in two to three years?
7. Have you ever thought about building your own team?
8. What would you need to see to consider a move?

## Likely Objections and Responses

**"My split is better where I am."**
"That may be true on paper. The question is what you're getting for it. With our cap, you're never paying more than $16,000 a year, and you get support that frees up your time."

**"I don't want to lose my brand."**
"You won't. You keep your name front and center; the team just adds horsepower behind it."

**"Teams are for newer agents."**
"A lot of our top producers felt that way before joining. At your level, the team is about leverage, not leads."

**"I don't need leads."**
"Totally fair. Most agents at your level don't join for leads; they join for time back and systems."

**"The timing isn't right."**
"Understood. What would make the timing right? Let's stay in touch so you have the information when you're ready."
