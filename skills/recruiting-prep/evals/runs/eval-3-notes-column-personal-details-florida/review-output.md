### Florida Notes for These Drafts

From the outreach rules reference (Florida Statutes 501.059): texts count as telephonic sales calls; prior express written consent is required for a sales text sent with "an automated system for the selection and dialing of telephone numbers," which is broader than the federal definition; once someone says they don't want calls or texts, don't contact them again; and before suing over texts, a recipient must reply STOP, after which the sender has 15 days to stop. Send texts between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in the recipient's local time. Whether Florida's 501.616(6) limits (no calls before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m., and no more than three on the same subject in 24 hours) reach a licensee's recruiting texts is not clear: confirm with your broker or an attorney.
