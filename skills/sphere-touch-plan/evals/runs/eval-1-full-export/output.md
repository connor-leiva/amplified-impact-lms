3. **Home anniversaries.** Your Home Anniversary field is filled only for Ben. Your Account Owner checks it's a Date field with Occurs Every Year (Admin > Custom Fields); you fill it for the other past clients from Deal Close Date (the dates are in the export and in Check Before Sending). An admin makes an action plan whose first step is a task, "Home anniversary: send a personal note," and an automation (Admin > Automations): Calendar Date trigger, Home Anniversary, "is in 0 days," start that action plan, "Run automation multiple times per person." It starts at 8 AM in your company time zone. An automation-started action plan doesn't send its first text, so the task reminds you and you send by hand.
