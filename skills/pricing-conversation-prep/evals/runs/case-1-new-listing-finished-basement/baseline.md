Great question, and this is a common situation with homes that have basements in neighborhoods where most don't. Here's how I'd approach it.

## Recommended List Price: $559,900 to $569,900

**My pick: $564,900**

## The Comps at a Glance

**Sold (Sample Orchard Estates, no basements):**
- 1204 W Sample Orchard Ln: $489,000 (1,480 sf rambler)
- 1157 W Sample Orchard Ln: $476,500 (1,420 sf rambler)
- 1175 W Pretend Plum Ct: $497,500 (1,510 sf rambler)

**Sold (Makebelieve Meadows, finished basements):**

- 905 W Makebelieve Meadow Dr: $538,000 (2,690 sf total)

**Average sold price:** $535,714. **Average price per square foot:** $279.

## How I Got There

The subject has 2,850 finished square feet, but 1,400 of that is basement. Appraisers and buyers typically value finished basement space at about 50% to 60% of above-grade space. Using the rambler comps at roughly $332 per above-grade square foot:

- Above grade: 1,450 x $332 = $481,400
- Basement: 1,400 x $166 (50%) = $232,400
- Indicated value: about $713,800

That's clearly too high compared with the actual basement sales, which tells us the market isn't paying full value for basement space here. The two Makebelieve Meadows sales ($538,000 and $552,000) are the best evidence. Adjusting them up for time (prices have generally risen this year) and for the subject's 2022 kitchen remodel and newer furnace (roughly $10,000 to $15,000), I land around $560,000 to $570,000.

The active at 877 W Makebelieve Meadow Dr at $564,900 is your direct competition, so pricing right at it makes sense since the subject has the better kitchen.

## Handling the Square Footage Argument

Your sellers are making the classic total-square-footage mistake. Here's a script:

> "I totally understand why you'd look at it that way. But when buyers and appraisers look at a home, they treat the upstairs and the basement differently. The Fictional Fox home has 2,050 square feet above ground, and yours has 1,450. Basement space adds value, but typically about half as much per foot as main-level space. The best comparisons for your home are the two ramblers with finished basements in Makebelieve Meadows, which sold for $538,000 and $552,000. Your updated kitchen puts you above those."

## Why Not $640,000?

- No comp supports it. The highest sale is $612,000, a much bigger two-story.
- 921 W Makebelieve Meadow Dr tried $619,900 with a finished basement and expired after 120 days.
- You'd risk an appraisal gap: if it appraises at $565,000, the buyer may ask for a reduction or walk.

## Other Things to Mention

- The pending at 1222 W Sample Orchard Ln went under contract with a cash buyer in 12 days, so demand is there at the right price.
- The seller at 1204 W was relocating, which may have pushed that price down a bit.
- Months of supply is very low, so it's a seller's market.

## Tips for the Appointment

1. Bring printed comps with the above-grade and basement numbers side by side.
2. Show them the expired listing at $619,900. Nothing convinces a seller like a home that didn't sell.
3. Offer a 2-week price review if they insist on starting higher.

Good luck Thursday! You've got the data on your side.
