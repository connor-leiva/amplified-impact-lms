## 4. Recommendation

- **Supported range: $545,000 to $560,000.** The bottom sits above 905 W Makebelieve Meadow Dr ($538,000), because the subject has an updated kitchen and slightly more space. The top stays under the $564,900 asked for 877 W Makebelieve Meadow Dr, which buyers have passed on for 52 days, even after a cut.
- **Recommended price: $549,900.** It lines up with the closest sale (842 W Makebelieve Meadow Dr, $552,000, or $546,000 after its $6,000 concessions) and stays under $550,000, so it shows up in searches capped at $550,000. Listing above $550,000 would drop it out of those searches for a few thousand dollars the comps don't clearly support.
- **Confidence: medium.** Strong evidence for the floor, but only two finished-basement sales, both older than six months and outside the subdivision.
- **The sellers' $640,000:** above every sale in this CMA, above both expired listings ($629,900 and $619,900), and $75,100 above the active basement rambler at $564,900. Nothing in this comp set supports it.
- **Appraisal check (Utah):** a financed buyer's appraiser will lean on closed sales like these and will count only the 1,450 above-grade square feet as living area, with the basement on its own line. Under the state-approved REPC, if the appraisal condition box is checked and the home appraises below the price, the buyer can cancel by the Financing & Appraisal Deadline and get the earnest money back. It's a right to cancel, not an automatic price cut, but it usually reopens the price conversation after the listing has lost its newness. No appraisal outcome is certain either way.
- **What would change this:** a finished-basement sale closer to Sample Orchard Estates or newer than March 2026; the sale price of the pending at 1222 W Sample Orchard Ln once it closes; and the basement's condition and finish at your walkthrough.

"Online estimates are a good starting point, but in Utah sale prices aren't public record, so those sites may not have the closed prices I'm showing you. These are what buyers actually paid for the homes most like yours, and they're what an appraiser will use."

**"We redid the kitchen and the furnace."**
