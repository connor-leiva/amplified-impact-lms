## Objections and Answers

**1. "Zillow says $607,000."**
"That's a model's estimate. In Utah, sale prices aren't public record, so estimates work without the closed prices the MLS has. Here are the actual sales: the highest two-story sale was $604,500, with a 2023 kitchen and three-car garage. A buyer's appraiser will use sales like these, not the estimate."

**2. "We'd like to start at $615,000. We can always come down."**
"I understand not wanting to leave money on the table. In this set, the three homes that priced right went under contract in 5 to 14 days and closed at 99.48% to 100.77% of their first price. The two that started high and cut took 36 and 44 days and closed at about 96.6% of where they started. And 1340 Cedar Sample started at $649,900 and expired. Starting high here has meant a longer wait for less."

**3. "We have 3,160 finished square feet. That should be worth more."**
"Your basement adds real value. Appraisers, though, count the above-grade space as living area and treat the basement on its own line. Above grade, your home is 2,180 square feet, right in line with these sales (2,060 to 2,300), and nearly all of them have finished basements too. At $599,900 you're at $275.18 per above-grade square foot, inside what these homes sold for."

**4. "We put a lot into the basement, the roof and the counters."**
"You did, and it shows in the price: those updates are why I put you above 1510 Juniper Example, which has an original kitchen. Where the sales stop is 1376 Cedar Sample, with a 2023 kitchen remodel and a third garage stall, at $604,500."

**5. "1477 Juniper Example is listed at $609,000."**
"It is, and it came on just three days ago, so it hasn't been tested yet. It has a kitchen updated in 2024 and a covered patio. At $599,900, buyers comparing the two will see a $9,100 difference. If it sells, we will look at its price together."
