## 6. Objections and Answers

**"Zillow says $607,000."**
"Zillow's a fair place to start. In Utah, sale prices aren't public record, so sites like that don't have the actual closed prices I'm showing you. What buyers really paid for homes like yours runs from $572,000 to $604,500, and the buyer's appraiser will use those sales too."

**"We were hoping for $615,000. Can't we start there and come down?"**
"I understand not wanting to leave money on the table. Here's what this neighborhood shows: the homes that sold at their first price went under contract in 5 to 14 days. The two that started high and cut took 36 and 44 days and sold at 96.6% of their first price. The home your size that started at $649,900 expired after 120 days."

**"We finished the basement and replaced the roof."**
"Those count, and they're why I'm putting you above 1510 down the street, which sold for $589,000 with its original kitchen. The top sale had a remodeled kitchen and a third garage stall, so that's the step above you."

**"The rambler on Maple Mock sold for $612,000, and it's about our size."**
"It has about the same total space, but 1,600 of its square feet are in the basement and only 1,650 are upstairs. Yours has 2,180 upstairs. It's a different kind of home, and appraisers compare upstairs and basement space separately, so it isn't a good guide for yours."

**"Another agent said they could get more."**
"That's a fair thing to check. I'd ask which closed sales support their number. Mine comes from these sales, and they're the ones a buyer's appraiser will look at."
