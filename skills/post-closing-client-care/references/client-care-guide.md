# Client Care Guide: The First Year After Closing

The house rules for turning one closed deal into a first-year plan and its drafts: where the facts come from, how the dates are worked out, what a draft may mention, the shape of each draft, the Follow Up Boss setup and the items every Check Before Sending lists. The plan's milestones are Amplified Impact defaults, not law. The review and vendor rules are in `reviews-and-vendors.md`, the texting and email rules in `outreach-rules.md`, and the Utah property tax facts in `utah-client-care.md`.

Last verified: 2026-11-10. The Follow Up Boss facts were checked against its help center on 2026-11-10 (as gathered for Amplified Impact's FUB Automation Audit and Sphere Touch Plan, with each article's own "updated" date under Sources). Follow Up Boss changes its screens often: when a click path here and the agent's screen disagree, trust the screen and say so.

## Where the Deal Comes From

Take only what the plan needs. Leave the rest where it is.

| Source | Take | Never carry over |
|---|---|---|
| A TC's closing email | Each client's name and email, the side, the property address, the closed, funded or recorded date, the county (the recorder or title company line) | Price, commission, disbursement, wire or trust account lines, loan and lender details, file numbers |
| A Sisu transaction (copied text or a screenshot) | Client names, Type (Buyer or Seller), Status, Close Date, the property | Sale price, GCI, commission, lead source |
| A Follow Up Boss person or deal (copied text or a screenshot) | Name, email, phone, tags (Do Not Text, Do Not Call, Unsubscribed), a Text Consent field, the background, the deal's stage and close date | Deal price, commission, Last Activity, website activity |
| The agent's notes | What the client loved, planned or asked for, the gift, who agreed to texts | Anything in "Left out on purpose" below |

- **The closing date:** "Recorded," "Closed," "Funded," "Close Date" or "Actual Close Date." When signing and recording fall on different days, use the recording date (the day ownership changed) and flag it. A Follow Up Boss deal whose stage isn't Closed, or a date marked projected or scheduled: plan from it and flag "not closed yet."
- **The county:** the county recorder or the title company's county in the closing email. Otherwise look it up from the property's city (Utah: `utah-client-care.md`, Where to Find the County). Never guess a county for a town you don't know: [County], flagged.
- **Two clients:** a couple or co-buyers are two people. Each has their own text consent and email. Address them both by first name in emails and the card.
- **Not closed by this agent's side:** a referral or a teammate's deal still works; sign as the agent who asked.

## The Plan Dates

Work the dates out from the closing date, in code when you can, and write each one with its weekday ("Tuesday, November 17, 2026").

| Touch | Date | What it is (default) | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closing day | the closing date | The handwritten card (with the gift, if the notes name one) and the thank-you email. The vendor introductions too, when the client asked for a name | Card; email |
| Week 1 | closing date plus 7 days | The review request, and a line asking how the move went | Email to every client; the same ask by text to each client who agreed to texts |
| Day 30 | plus 30 days | A check-in tied to a note. Buyers: the property tax reminder. Vendor introductions not asked for but planned (the notes say they plan the work) | Email (buyers, for the reminder); a text check-in to a client who agreed, when there is no reminder |
| Day 90 | plus 90 days | A call, with a talking point from the notes | Call; a text if the client agreed and has asked for texts over calls |
| Month 6 | plus 182 days | A check-in tied to a note | Text to a client who agreed; otherwise email |
| First anniversary | the same month and day, one year later | Handed to sphere-touch-plan: the client joins the agent's sphere plan as a past client | None here |

- **Why 182:** Follow Up Boss counts delays in days, so a fixed day count keeps the plan and the Follow Up Boss tasks on the same dates. February 29 anniversaries become February 28.
- **Already past:** a touch whose date is before today moves up. The closing-day touches go today; each later past touch goes two days after the one before it. Touches still ahead keep their dates. Flag each moved touch with its original date ("Week 1 was Friday, November 6; the review request now goes Thursday, November 12").
- **Talking points:** day 90 and month 6 get one line each from the notes (the garden they planned for spring, the fence they plan to replace). Nothing left in the notes: "Ask how the house is treating them," and say the notes had nothing more.
- **No call on a Sunday or a legal holiday** (a conservative default in `outreach-rules.md`): a call or voicemail that would land on one, the day 90 call included, moves to the next day that is neither, and the plan says why in one line. Texts, emails and the card keep their day.
- **Which holidays:** in Utah mode, the Utah legal holidays in `utah-client-care.md`. In general mode, Sundays and the federal legal holidays (5 U.S.C. 6103): New Year's Day (January 1), the Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. (third Monday in January), Washington's Birthday (third Monday in February), Memorial Day (last Monday in May), Juneteenth (June 19), Independence Day (July 4), Labor Day (first Monday in September), Columbus Day (second Monday in October), Veterans Day (November 11), Thanksgiving Day (fourth Thursday in November) and Christmas Day (December 25). One that falls on a Saturday is observed the Friday before, and one on a Sunday the Monday after: skip that day too. Say that state holidays were not checked.
- **The next federal ones:** Wednesday, November 11, 2026; Thursday, November 26, 2026; Friday, December 25, 2026; Friday, January 1, 2027; Monday, January 18, 2027; Monday, February 15, 2027; Monday, May 31, 2027; Friday, June 18, and Saturday, June 19, 2027; Monday, July 5, 2027 (July 4 is a Sunday); Monday, September 6, 2027; Monday, October 11, 2027; Thursday, November 11, 2027; Thursday, November 25, 2027.
- **Check Before Sending** writes every date as "closing date plus N days."

## Channels

| Channel | Open when | Closed when |
|---|---|---|
| Text | The agent says this client agreed to texts, or the record shows a written yes (a Text Consent field or a signed form) | A phone number alone, a past deal, a verbal-only yes, a blank, No, STOP or Revoked, Do Not Text, Do Not Call, no phone |
| Email | An email address | Unsubscribed, Do Not Email, Do Not Contact, no email |
| Call | A phone number | Do Not Call, Do Not Contact, no phone |
| Card | Always (hand-delivered, or mailed to the new address; [Mailing Address], flagged, when the deal shows none) | The agent says no card |

- A Do Not Call request covers texts too: the FCC treats a text as a call.
- A yes the agent gives in chat: draft the text, and Check Before Sending says to keep the written record with its date.
- Texts are drafted one per client, sent by hand from the agent's own phone or Follow Up Boss, one at a time, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in the client's time zone.

## What a Draft May Mention

Every personal line comes from the notes or the deal, and Check Before Sending names its source.

- **May use:** what the client loved about the home (a room, a tree, the light, the yard), a project or plan the notes name, something they asked the agent for, a pet the notes name, the gift the notes name, the street name, and the closing itself.
- **Never invent:** no children, pets, plans, hobbies, trips, jobs, events or favors the notes don't state. Never join two notes into a fact neither one says ("loves the maple" and "has a dog" is not "the dog under the maple").
- **Left out on purpose, even when the notes say it:** health and medical details; money (price, loan, rate, payment, appraisal, equity, commission, savings, a job change); family status and changes in it (pregnancy, marriage, engagement, divorce, children, grandchildren, a parent moving in, a death); religion, national origin, disability and age; anything told in confidence. Leave the whole note out. One agent-only flag: "Left out on purpose: N notes with health, money or family details. Read them yourself before you decide what to say in person."
- **Fair housing:** describe the home, never the people in it or near it. No comments on the neighborhood, schools, safety or who lives nearby. Never call the clients "a family" or the home "perfect for a family"; say "the next person" or "other buyers and sellers."
- **Sellers:** a sale is not a "home anniversary." Thank them for the sale and name what they loved about the home they sold, from the notes. Their next home gets a mention only when the notes name it.

## Draft Shapes

Follow the user's voice profile QUICK CARD when one is in the conversation, including its sign-off and the words it bans. Without one: warm, plain, direct, short paragraphs. Sign with the user's name and brokerage, or [Agent Name]. None of "stunning," "dream home," "don't hesitate to reach out," "touch base," "circle back," "just checking in" or "I hope this email finds you well."

- **Card text:** two to four sentences in the agent's hand, to the clients by first name, signed with the agent's first name. One or two things from the notes, the gift if the notes name it, and thanks. No links, no asks, no footer.
- **Thank-you email:** a Title Case subject; 150 words or fewer in the body; thanks tied to one or two things from the notes; the next touch with its weekday and date ("I'll check in next Tuesday, November 17"). No review, referral or vendor ask. Then the footer, like every email.
- **Review request email:** a Title Case subject; 120 words or fewer in the body; "I ask every client the same thing"; an honest review of their experience; every link from the agent's list, each on its own line with the site's name; "a few sentences is plenty." No stars, ratings, "five-star," sample wording or reward. Then the footer.
- **Review request text:** for each client who agreed to texts: "Hi [First Name], it's [Agent Full Name] with [Brokerage]." The same ask in one or two sentences, the first link only, "Reply STOP to opt out." 300 characters or fewer, links counted.
- **Vendor introductions email:** a Title Case subject naming what they asked about; 150 words or fewer in the body; for each vendor: the trade, the company, the contact name and phone exactly as the list shows them, and why (the client's need, from the notes); each relationship the list discloses, in plain words ("Full disclosure: Dale is my brother-in-law"); "You're free to use anyone you like." Add "I don't receive anything for referring them" only when the list says so. Then the footer.
- **Day 30 email (buyers):** a Title Case subject; 150 words or fewer in the body; one check-in line from the notes; the property tax reminder (Utah: `utah-client-care.md`; general mode: the county assessor, and nothing about the state's rules); any vendor introductions that wait for day 30. Then the footer.
- **Talking points** (day 90, month 6): one line each, from the notes, with the channel.

**The email footer,** from `outreach-rules.md`, after the signature on every email, the thank-you and the vendor introductions included: "To stop these emails, reply UNSUBSCRIBE or use this link: [Unsubscribe Link]. [Sender Postal Address]. This is a marketing email from [Agent Name], [Brokerage Name]." Never drop a sentence, even on an email the client asked for: the full footer on every one-to-one client email is a conservative default. Check Before Sending says to confirm the footer with the broker.

## Set It Up in Follow Up Boss (the Agent's Instructions)

Follow Up Boss can already remind the agent when each touch is due. Give these steps as instructions; the agent or an admin does them. Never set anything up or write to the CRM. Texts stay manual: Automations 2.0 has no text action, and a classic plan's initial text is not sent when an automation starts the plan.

**Which kind of account:** Admin > Automations shows a canvas with triggers, controls and actions on an Automations 2.0 account. After an account switches to 2.0, action plans can't be created, edited or started (they can only be paused, resumed or deleted). Only the account owner can switch, and it can't be undone.

**Classic account (Admin > Action Plans):**
1. An admin creates a plan named "First Year After Closing" with five Create Task steps (tasks, not automatic emails, so each touch stays personal):
   - Run 0 days after: "Closing day: card and thank-you email (Post-Closing Client Care drafts)"
   - Run 7 days after the previous step: "Week 1: review request, the same ask for every client"
   - Run 23 days after the previous step: "Day 30: check-in; buyers get the property tax reminder"
   - Run 60 days after the previous step: "Day 90: call"
   - Run 92 days after the previous step: "Month 6: check-in"
   That puts the tasks on days 0, 7, 30 (7 + 23), 90 (30 + 60) and 182 (90 + 92). Follow Up Boss counts each step from the one before it.
2. On closing day, start it by hand on the client's profile. Two clients on two contacts (a couple): start it on one, so the tasks don't double. Task steps post early in the morning in the user's time zone.

**Automations 2.0 account (Admin > Automations):**
1. An admin creates an automation with the Manual trigger (an automation needs it to be applied by hand), then Create Task actions with Time Delay controls of 7, 23, 60 and 92 days between them, the same five tasks as above.
2. On closing day, apply it by hand on the client's profile (on one contact for a couple, as above).

**The first anniversary:** if the account has a Home Anniversary date field set to occur every year, fill it with the closing date on each client's profile so the sphere plan's yearly reminder picks them up (sphere-touch-plan sets that up). If it doesn't, say the first anniversary date and that sphere-touch-plan covers the setup.

**Closing day already past:** don't start the plan for this client, or every task lands late. Add each touch still ahead as a task on the profile, due on its date from the plan.

**Another CRM or none:** the same five dated touches, as tasks or calendar reminders, in plain words.

## Check Before Sending (Items Every Run Lists)

- Every date: the closing date and its source line, then each touch as "closing date plus N days," and any moved touch with its original date.
- Every fact a draft mentions, with the note or document line it came from.
- Each vendor exactly as the list shows it (company, contact, phone), each relationship the list discloses, and "confirm you receive nothing of value for this referral." Affiliated businesses: the disclosure form first.
- Each review link, opened once to check it lands on the right profile; the same ask going to every client.
- Texts: each client's consent record (the agent's word in chat, or the field or form with its date), sent by hand, one at a time, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. their time. Clients without consent get no text.
- Calls and texts: the Do Not Call check. A past client within 18 months of the closing is inside the established business relationship window in `outreach-rules.md`, but any do-not-call request ends it.
- Calls: none on a Sunday or a legal holiday; a moved call with its original date and why.
- Emails: the unsubscribe link works, the postal address is real, every email carries the full footer (the thank-you and the vendor introductions too), and the footer wording is confirmed with the broker.
- The county assessor's link and name (Utah: the 2027 change in `utah-client-care.md`).
- The gift, as the notes name it.
- State rules: Utah mode, or "State rules were not checked."
