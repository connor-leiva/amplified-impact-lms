# Utah REPC Effective November 1, 2026: Section Map and What Changed

Last verified: 2026-11-08

Use this file only when the uploaded REPC's footer reads "EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 1, 2026." For the form with the footer "EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 4, 2024," use `utah-repc.md`. The signed contract always controls: read every term and section number from the upload, and use this file to explain them.

## Which Form Is This, and Is It in Use?

- The footer of the revised form reads: "THIS FORM APPROVED BY THE UTAH REAL ESTATE COMMISSION AND THE OFFICE OF THE UTAH ATTORNEY GENERAL, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 1, 2026. AS OF JANUARY 1, 2027, IT WILL REPLACE AND SUPERSEDE THE PREVIOUSLY APPROVED VERSION OF THIS FORM."
- The Utah Association of REALTORS (UAR) says: "The Utah Attorney General's Office has approved a new version of the Utah Real Estate Purchase Contract (REPC). The updated REPC will be effective and available for use on Nov. 1, 2026. You may no longer use the old version of the REPC starting Jan. 1, 2027." UAR posts a watermarked "Final Clean Copy" of the form dated October 14, 2026. The section map below comes from that copy.
- On 2026-11-08 the Division of Real Estate's State Approved Forms page still posted the December 4, 2024 REPC. So both versions can show up in a file through December 31, 2026. Confirm with your broker which version your brokerage uses.
- The same UAR page says the FHA/VA Loan Addendum, Seller Financing Addendum and Deposit of Earnest Money with Title Insurance Company Addendum were also updated, and that the FHA/VA update removes its closing cost language. The Division's Blank Addendum (the form used for counteroffers, footer "EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2020") is not on UAR's list of updated forms, and the Division still posted that version on 2026-11-08. Its acceptance line still points to "Section 23 of the REPC," which is Acceptance in both REPC versions.

## What Changed, and How It Reads

Compared with the December 4, 2024 form:

**Deadlines are in Section 4, and there are two financing deadlines.** The December 2024 form has one Financing & Appraisal Deadline (24(c)) and the Settlement Deadline at 24(d). The November 2026 form has a separate Appraisal Deadline (4(c)) and Financing Deadline (4(d)), and the Settlement Deadline is 4(e). A counter that says "the date in Section 4(b)" is changing the Due Diligence Deadline.

**Seller money toward the buyer's costs is a line in the contract.** Section 2.2: "Seller agrees to contribute at Settlement the amount of $___ to be applied at Buyer's discretion toward Buyer's Closing Costs as defined in Section 8.3(c), if applicable." Section 8.3(c) lets the buyer apply it to "(a) a permanent reduction, or temporary reduction, in the mortgage loan interest rate; (b) mortgage financing costs; (c) closing costs; and (d) Buyer's prepaid escrow funds," and says "Any unused portion of Seller's contribution may, at Buyer's option, be applied to any costs or expenses permitted by Buyer's lender (if applicable), including a reduction in the Purchase Price." A counter that changes seller money should be read against Section 2.2. The buyer's lender confirms how much the loan program allows.

**Home warranty** is Section 2.3: "[ ] WILL [ ] WILL NOT," with "Seller agrees to pay for a home warranty plan selected and ordered by Buyer not to exceed $___ at Settlement."

**Earnest money on a financing cancellation is a percentage.** Section 3(c)(i): "[ ] 50% of the Earnest Money Deposit; or [ ] ___ % of the Earnest Money Deposit described below shall be released to Seller if Buyer cancels the REPC in accordance with Section 12.3(a). If no box is checked, then the percentage shall be 50%." The December 2024 form uses a dollar blank in 8.3(b)(i). Show the dollar result of the percentage, with the math.

**Earnest money delivery.** Section 5: the buyer delivers the deposit to the Buyer's Brokerage "No later than four (4) calendar days after Acceptance," and the brokerage has four calendar days to deposit it in its trust account or with a title insurance company. "If the Earnest Money Deposit is not delivered or deposited within the timeframe described above, Seller may, in Seller's sole discretion, cancel the REPC." There is no Additional Earnest Money section in this form.

**Possession and holdover.** Possession is checked in 4(f): "Upon Recording," a number of hours after Recording, or a number of calendar days after Recording. Section 9.3 says a rental between the parties "shall be by separate written agreement or addendum to the REPC," and sets a holdover fee if the seller doesn't deliver possession: "$ ___ or $300 per day, whichever is greater," described as "liquidated damages."

**Brokerage compensation.** Section 2.1 sets any Seller's Compensation Contribution. Section 8.3(b): "If Seller's Compensation Contribution exceeds the written buyer-broker agreement, the excess amount shall be returned to Seller." Don't compute commissions without a net sheet.

**Acceptance requires receipt.** Section 23: Acceptance occurs only when "(a) Buyer or Seller has signed the offer or counteroffer where noted to indicate acceptance; and (b) the offer or counteroffer has been Received by the other party, or the other party's respective agent, or by the other party's branch broker or principal broker, no later than the applicable date referenced in Section 30 or as noted in the counteroffer." Section 22 says electronic delivery is "Received" when it "enters an information processing system (e.g., email server) that the recipient has designated or uses" for that purpose. The December 2024 form instead requires that the signing be "communicated."

**Weekends and holidays.** Section 25: "if any dates for the Earnest Money delivery, Earnest Money Deposit or the Settlement Deadline referenced in this REPC fall on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, performance shall be required on the next business day." That rule covers only those dates. The December 2024 form has no such rule.

**Deadlines and time of day.** Section 25 keeps "5:00 PM Mountain Time on the stated date" and calendar days "counted beginning on the day following the event which triggers the timing requirement (e.g., Acceptance)." Extensions "must be agreed to in writing by all parties."

**The offer's own deadline.** Section 30: "If Seller does not accept this offer by: ___ [ ] AM [ ] PM Mountain Time on ___ (Date), this offer shall lapse." A seller's counteroffer is checked on the COUNTEROFFER box under Section 30 and refers to "the attached ADDENDUM NO.___."

**Due diligence waiver.** Section 12.1(c): if the buyer doesn't cancel or resolve objections in writing by the Due Diligence Deadline in 4(b), the buyer "shall be deemed to have waived the Due Diligence Condition, and, except as provided in Sections 12.2(a) and 12.3(a), the Earnest Money Deposit shall become non-refundable." Moving 4(b) earlier moves that point earlier.

**Appraisal cancellation.** Section 12.2(a): the buyer may cancel with written notice "along with a complete copy of the appraisal, no later than the Appraisal Deadline referenced in Section 4(c)."

## What Stays the Same

- Changes still go on an approved addendum, and a licensee may not alter the REPC's printed or filled-in language to make a counteroffer (see `utah-forms-and-counters.md`).
- Prorations are made as of the Settlement Deadline (8.1), so a counter that moves Settlement moves the proration date.
- Closing still means Settlement plus loan funding and Recording, with funding and Recording "no later than four calendar days after Settlement" (9.2).
