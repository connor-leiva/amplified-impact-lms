# Fair Housing and Privacy in Follow-Ups

Last verified: 2026-11-08. Sources at the end. This is not legal advice; fair housing questions go to the broker.

An appointment transcript holds the client's exact words, including things that should never be written down by the agent. The recap email and the CRM note are the agent's own written statements, and a CRM note is a lasting record other team members can read. Keep both to property features, facts and next steps.

## Protected Classes

- **Federal (Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604):** race, color, religion, sex, handicap (disability), familial status, and national origin. Section 3604(c) makes it unlawful to make or publish any statement, with respect to the sale or rental of a dwelling, that indicates a preference, limitation or discrimination based on these.
- **Utah (Utah Fair Housing Act, Utah Code 57-21-5):** race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, source of income, disability, sexual orientation, and gender identity. Subsection (2) covers oral or written representations and statements that directly or indirectly express a preference. Subsection (3) bars inducing a sale or rental by talking about who is moving into a neighborhood.
- **Other states and cities** add their own classes. In general mode, treat any remark about a person's characteristics as off limits.

## What to Do With What the Client Said

| The client said | What goes in the email and CRM note |
|---|---|
| A remark about who lives in a neighborhood (race, ethnicity, language, religion, families, kids, age of residents, "people like us") | Nothing. Not the remark, not a paraphrase, and not the area as an exclusion. One agent-only flag: "A remark about who lives in an area was left out on purpose (fair housing). If it comes up again, steer back to property features." |
| An area ruled out for reasons about the people there | Leave the area out of the criteria. Keep only areas the client wants for property or commute reasons. |
| An area ruled out for a property reason (busy road, HOA rules, commute, lot sizes) | Record the reason as a property criterion ("no homes backing onto a major road"). |
| A need tied to a disability or health ("Mom had her hip replaced," "my dad uses a walker") | The feature only: "main-floor bedroom with a full bath on the main," "no-step or one-step entry." Never the condition or the person's health. |
| Household size or a baby on the way | The bedroom count or space they asked for. |
| A place of worship as a reason for an area | The area or drive time only if the client also named it as an area they want; never the place of worship. |
| "Are the schools good?" | No opinion and no ranking. Point to the school district's boundary lookup and the state's school report cards. In the CRM note: "Asked about schools; pointed to official sources." |
| "Is it safe?" | No opinion. Point to the local police department's published crime data. In the CRM note: "Asked about safety; pointed to official sources." |
| "Who lives there?" or a question about demographics | No answer. Same agent-only flag; suggest the client visit the area themselves. |

When the agent in the transcript already redirected the client well, don't praise or quote the exchange; just leave it out.

## Leave Out of Everything

- Loan balances and payoff figures ("we still owe about...")
- Credit scores, income, debts (student loans, car payments), savings, down payment sources and amounts, sale proceeds
- Account, loan and Social Security numbers, and any wire or banking detail
- Health details and diagnoses
- Anything a client asked to keep private
- Agent-only MLS fields (private remarks, showing instructions, lockbox and access details) in anything client-facing

## Allowed, and Where

| Detail | Email | CRM note |
|---|---|---|
| Price range the buyer gave | Yes, as stated | Yes |
| Preapproval status, lender, loan officer, loan type, letter date | Lender name only if useful ("once Pat has your pay stubs") | Yes |
| Preapproval amount | No | Yes, as stated |
| Prices discussed at a listing appointment | Yes, each as stated and by whom | Yes |
| Monthly payment target the client gave | No | Yes, as a criterion |
| A home the client must sell first, and its status | Yes | Yes |
| Personal details for rapport (a pet, a trip, a hobby) | Only through the agent's own personal note | No |
