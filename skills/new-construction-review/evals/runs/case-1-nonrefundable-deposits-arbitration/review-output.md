## Lot, HOA and Special District

- HOA: $140 per month, 0.5% reinvestment fee at Closing ($3,169.50 on $633,900, computed). Utah caps reinvestment fees at 0.5% of value for covenants recorded on or after May 6, 2026, with exceptions (`utah-property-notes.md`). Ask the title company to confirm the fee and how it applies; I am not concluding anything about it.
- CC&Rs, HOA budget and when the builder's control of the board ends: not uploaded. Ask for them before signing.
- Special district: the PSA is silent. Ask the title company whether Lot 112 sits inside a public infrastructure district (a recorded notice would appear in the title commitment). If it does, the expected annual cost must be disclosed at or before closing under Utah Code 57-1-49; ask for it now.
- No plat, soils report or easement information was uploaded. Ask for the recorded plat.
- Utah notes to raise with the title company and an attorney: whether a notice of completion will be filed and whether the owner's policy will cover construction liens recorded after Closing (liens can be recorded up to 180 days after final completion, or 90 days after a notice of completion, Utah Code 38-1a-502); new-home property tax is based on January 1 value, so ask the title company how the proration is figured and the county assessor about the residential exemption declaration. Look up Notreal Ridge Builders, Inc. on DOPL's License Lookup (https://secure.utah.gov/llv/search/index.html).

## For a Real Estate Attorney

Not legal advice. These are the clauses a real estate attorney should review with the buyer before signing.

1. **Deposits (PSA 2; Deposit Schedule):** all payments go to the builder, may be used for construction and are nonrefundable at signing or when paid. How is the money protected if the builder fails to finish?
2. **Default (PSA 11):** the buyer's "sole remedy is the return of deposits paid, without interest," while 11.1 lists "deposits, Structural Option payments and Design Center payments" separately. Would the $22,400 options payment and Design Center payments come back on a builder default?
3. **Arbitration (PSA 8):** binding arbitration before "a single arbitrator selected by Seller," Buyer pays the filing fee, jury and class action waivers. What does this mean for Avery?
4. **Warranty (PSA 7.2):** the limited warranty is "IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES" and she "WAIVES ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES." In Utah, defect claims run mostly through contract and warranty (Utah Code 78B-4-513), so what does this waiver leave her?
5. **Claim deadline (PSA 7.3):** "within one (1) year after Closing, regardless of when it is discovered." Utah's six-year contract and warranty period yields to a period the contract sets (Utah Code 78B-2-225(3)(c)). How does this interact with the ten-year structural coverage in PSA 7.1?
6. **Completion (PSA 3.2):** extensions "without limit" and "NO LIABILITY TO BUYER FOR ANY DELAY," with no outside date. For a home not yet built, how does the federal Interstate Land Sales Full Disclosure Act's two-year exemption (15 U.S.C. 1702(a)(2)) apply to this contract?
7. **Price increase (PSA 1.2):** up to 4% of the Base Price after 270 days, for any reason other than Buyer's delay.
8. **Missing terms:** the PSA has no title, deed, title insurance, risk of loss or attorney fee terms.
9. **Liens:** whether a notice of completion will be filed and how Utah's lien statutes and the owner's title policy would apply to this purchase.
