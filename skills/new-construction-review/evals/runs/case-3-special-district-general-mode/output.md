## What This Form Lacks Next to the Standard Protections

Compared with the standard buyer protections list (not a state form):
- **Inspection with a right to cancel:** present, but narrow. Sam can cancel only if the builder refuses to correct a building code item (Section 5), not for any reason.
- **Financing and appraisal conditions:** present (6.1, 6.2), with the earnest money returned.
- **Earnest money held by a neutral party:** the $12,000 is in escrow with the title company (2.1); the $15,000 options deposit is not (2.2).
- **Seller disclosures and a title commitment by a deadline:** not addressed.
- **Default remedies as strong for the buyer as for the seller:** Sam gets deposits back or specific performance; the builder keeps up to $27,000 (Section 12).
- **Final walk-through before closing:** not addressed.
- **Mediation and access to court:** mediation, then binding arbitration with a jury waiver (Section 8).
- **Risk of loss with the seller until closing:** not addressed.
- **Written deadlines with a time of day:** deadlines count from an undated Contract Date, with no time of day.
- **Completion:** a 24-month outside date with a refund (Section 3). For a home not yet built, the federal ILSA exemption for a contract "obligating the seller ... to erect such a building thereon within a period of two years" (15 U.S.C. 1702(a)(2)) is consistent with this kind of clause; whether it applies is the attorney's call.

## Lot, HOA and Special District

**Imaginary Creek Metropolitan District No. 2, as the disclosure states it** (Exhibit D p. 1):
- Maximum authorized mill levy: 55.000 mills debt service; 10.000 mills operations.
- 2026 certified mill levy: 50.000 mills debt service + 8.000 mills operations = 58.000 mills.
- Total debt authorized by voters: $45,000,000. Bonds outstanding: $28,400,000, final maturity December 1, 2056.
- Facilities fee: $1,500 per lot, "paid at the first sale of each lot to a homeowner." The contract doesn't say who pays it on this sale.
- District services fee: $45 per month (landscaping and covenant enforcement).
- The disclosure's own example: "A home with an assessed value of $40,000 would pay $2,320 per year" at the 2026 levy, and it says the example "is for illustration only" and the levy "may change each year." This review doesn't estimate Sam's tax; the home's assessed value isn't known yet.
- District taxes are on the county tax bill, in addition to the county, city, school district and other taxing entities' taxes.

Ask the title company whether the district's documents are recorded against the lot and appear in the title commitment, the Example County Treasurer or Assessor what the home's first full-year bill may be based on, and the district manager (Example District Management) for current figures. HOA: $60 per month; ask for the governing documents and who controls the association board now.
