# Sample Crest Flex Incentive -- What It Means for the Evalsons

## Quick Summary

Sample Crest is offering a **$20,000 Flex Incentive** if the Evalsons use the builder's in-house lender (Sample Crest Mortgage) and title company (Make-Believe Title), versus a **$5,000 Standard Incentive** if they stay with Pretend Valley Credit Union. That's a **$15,000 difference** on the table.

Whether switching makes sense depends on how the two loans compare. Builder-affiliated lenders sometimes price their rates or fees a bit higher to offset incentives, so the key is to compare the full cost, not just the headline number.

## The Incentive Terms

**Flex Incentive ($20,000)** -- applied at closing toward closing costs, prepaids, or a rate buydown. Conditions:
1. Finance with Sample Crest Mortgage, LLC
2. Apply within 3 days of the contract date
3. Close on or before December 18, 2026
4. Use Make-Believe Title for title and escrow

**Standard Incentive ($5,000)** -- if they use any other lender or pay cash, still requires closing by December 18, 2026.

**Important fine print:**
- Any part of the incentive their loan program doesn't allow is forfeited (no cash back, can't reduce the price).
- If closing slips past December 18 for any reason other than the seller's delay, **both incentives are void.**
- The addendum states they are not required to use Sample Crest Mortgage to buy the home.

## Is It Legal for the Builder to Do This?

Yes, generally. Under RESPA, builders can offer incentives for using an affiliated lender as long as the buyer isn't required to use them and the relationship is disclosed. Sample Crest provided an Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure showing they own 100% of the mortgage company and 51% of the title company, which is what's required.

## How to Compare

Have both lenders provide an official **Loan Estimate** on the same day, for the same loan type, amount and lock period. Then compare:
- Interest rate and APR
- Origination charges and discount points (Sample Crest discloses 0% to 1% origination)
- Total closing costs (Section A + B on the Loan Estimate)
- Cash to close after the incentive is applied
- Monthly payment

**Rule of thumb:** if Sample Crest's rate and fees are within about 0.125% to 0.25% of the credit union's, the extra $15,000 will almost always come out ahead. If their rate is noticeably higher, run the numbers over how long the Evalsons plan to keep the loan.

Also consider using part of the $20,000 for a permanent rate buydown -- that can make Sample Crest's offer even stronger.

## Other Contract Items to Note

- **Earnest money:** $5,000, held by Make-Believe Title.
- **Review period:** only 5 days after the contract date to inspect and cancel for any reason. Schedule the inspection right away.
- **Loan approval deadline:** December 4, 2026.
- **Appraisal:** they can cancel if it appraises low, but the seller can match the appraised value.
- **Disputes:** mediation then binding arbitration.
- **HOA:** $78/month plus a $350 transfer fee.

## Questions to Ask

1. Can we see a Loan Estimate from Sample Crest Mortgage today?
2. Will the incentive cover a permanent buydown, and how many points does $20,000 buy?
3. Is there any flexibility on the December 18 closing date?
4. Will the builder honor part of the Flex Incentive if we use our credit union but use Make-Believe Title?
5. What is the builder's warranty and who handles claims?
