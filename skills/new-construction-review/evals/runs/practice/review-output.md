## Incentive and Lender Conditions

- **Amount and use:** "up to $17,500 at Closing ... toward Buyer's closing costs, prepaid items, and/or the cost of a permanent or temporary interest rate buydown" (Addendum 1).
- **Every condition (Addendum 2):** (a) the loan comes from Sample Summit Home Loans, LLC; (b) complete application to it within 5 days after the Contract Date; (c) no change of lender after applying; (d) Closing within 10 days after the Notice of Completion; (e) escrow and closing by Pretend Peak Title Agency. And the buyers must sign on or before November 13, 2026 (Addendum 6).
- **If a condition isn't met:** "the Incentive is $0" (Addendum 3). Difference: $17,500 - $0 = $17,500 (computed).
- **Loan program limits:** any part the loan program won't allow "is forfeited and will not be paid to Buyer in cash, credited to the Purchase Price, or applied to options or upgrades" (Addendum 4). The buyers' lender confirms how much of it the loan can use.
- **Relationships:** "Preferred Lender and Pretend Peak Title Agency are affiliates of Seller. See the Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure Statement provided by Seller. Buyer is not required to use Preferred Lender or Pretend Peak Title Agency to purchase the Home." (Addendum 5)

RESPA checks, as questions (not conclusions):
- The Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure Statement is referred to but not in the upload. Ask for it, with its date, the ownership it describes, the estimated charges and whether the buyers signed it.
- The Addendum says the buyers are "not required" to use the affiliated lender or title agency. The PA says "Closing will be held at Pretend Peak Title Agency, Seller's designated closing agent" and "Buyer pays for the lender's policy of title insurance" (PA 12.2). Read together with RESPA Section 9 (12 U.S.C. 2608(a): a seller may not require "directly or indirectly, as a condition to selling the property, that title insurance covering the property be purchased by the buyer from any particular title company"), this is an attorney question.
- PA 5.1 says "Buyer's lender must be acceptable to Seller," and PA 5.4 lets Seller keep all Deposits if loan approval isn't delivered in 30 days. Ask how Seller decides whether the credit union is acceptable, and put it on the attorney list next to the Addendum's "not required" line.

The buyers want to use their credit union. That choice is theirs. With the credit union the incentive is $0 under these documents; their lenders can lay out the full cost of each option side by side. The CFPB's homebuying guide says: "Even if a real estate agent or homebuilder has a preferred lender, still look for a better deal."

## Lot, HOA and Special District

- **Public infrastructure district:** "All lots in this plat are within the boundaries of the Make-Believe Meadows Public Infrastructure District No. 1. The District may levy a property tax ... at a rate not to exceed 0.015 per dollar of taxable value." Notice recorded February 2, 2026, Entry No. 900012-2026 (Plat note 4; PA 11.1). No current levy or annual amount was uploaded; I am not estimating one. Under Utah Code 57-1-49 the seller must disclose the expected annual cost at or before closing. Ask for that number now, before signing, and confirm with the title company and the county.
- **Soils:** "Lots 45 through 49 contain collapsible soils and require foundations designed according to" the Imaginary Soils Engineering report dated January 9, 2026 (Plat note 3). Lot 47 is in that range. Ask the builder for the report and how the foundation follows it; questions about the soils go to a licensed geotechnical engineer.
- **Canal easement:** Lots 44 through 49 back onto the Example Irrigation Company canal, with "a 20-foot canal maintenance easement" along the rear and no fence, structure or blocking landscaping in it (Plat note 2). The PA requires rear yard landscaping within 12 months after Closing (PA 10.4). Ask how the CC&R landscaping requirement applies to the easement area, and who maintains it.
- **Drainage swale:** Lots 46 through 48 have a 5-foot swale along the east side lot line; owners must keep it clear and may not regrade it (Plat note 6).
- **Easements:** public utility easements of 10 feet along the front and 7 feet along the side and rear lot lines (Plat note 1).
- **Water:** culinary water and metered secondary water from the City of Sampleton (Plat note 5).
- **Lot size:** Lot 47 is 0.18 acres (Plat note 8).
- **HOA:** $95 per month; $600 Working Capital Contribution; declarant control until 90% of lots are conveyed or December 31, 2032 (PA 10). Ask for the CC&Rs, budget and rental rules. A declaration recorded before the developer's first sale may restrict rentals without the usual statutory exemptions (`utah-property-notes.md`).
- **Utah notes for the title company and an attorney:** construction lien timing after completion (Utah Code 38-1a-502) and whether the owner's policy covers liens recorded after Closing; property tax is based on January 1 value, so PA 12.2's proration "based on the most recent tax notice" may reflect a lot, not a finished home (ask the title company and the county assessor, including the residential exemption declaration). Look up Placeholder Peak Homes, LLC on DOPL's License Lookup.

## For a Real Estate Attorney

Not legal advice. These are the clauses a real estate attorney should review with the buyer before signing.

1. **Deposits (PA 2.4, 2.5):** paid to Seller, unsegregated, usable for any purpose; Earnest Money nonrefundable after 3 days; Option Deposit and change orders nonrefundable when paid.
2. **Loan approval and lender approval (PA 5.1, 5.4):** "Buyer's lender must be acceptable to Seller"; Seller may keep all Deposits if approval isn't delivered in 30 days. Read next to the Addendum's "Buyer is not required to use Preferred Lender" (Addendum 5) and RESPA Section 8's condition that a referred buyer "is not required to use any particular provider of settlement services" (12 U.S.C. 2607(c)(4)(B)).
3. **Title (PA 12.2):** Closing at "Seller's designated closing agent," with the buyers paying for the lender's policy, next to the Addendum's "not required" line and RESPA Section 9 (12 U.S.C. 2608).
4. **Arbitration (PA 9):** binding, jury and class waivers, each side pays its own fees, one year from when the buyers knew or should have known.
5. **Warranty (PA 8.2):** limited warranty "in lieu of all other warranties," implied warranties waived. Utah defect claims run mostly through contract and warranty (Utah Code 78B-4-513).
6. **Default (PA 14.2):** refund only; specific performance and damages waived.
7. **Appraisal (PA 5.5):** any gap paid in cash.
8. **Liens and title:** special warranty deed; how Utah's lien statutes and the owner's policy apply.
