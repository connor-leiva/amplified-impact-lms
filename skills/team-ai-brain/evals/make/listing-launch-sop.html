SOP: Listing Launch

Kettle Creek Example Team. Owner: Marlo Fakerson, Operations Manager. Effective: June 3, 2026
  1. The signed listing agreement goes in the transaction folder before anything else happens.
  2. Ops books photos within 2 business days of the signed listing agreement. Follow the Photo Day Checklist.
  3. Wren writes the MLS remarks. Ops checks them against the brand guide before the listing goes live. Remarks describe the home, never people.
  4. New listings go live on a Thursday by 10 a.m.
  5. Social post the day the listing goes live. Every listing post is approved before it goes up.
  6. First open house: the first Saturday after the listing goes live, 11 a.m. to 1 p.m.
  7. Seller update every Monday by noon: showings, feedback and online views.
