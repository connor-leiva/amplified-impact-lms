# Utah Notes for a Listing Appointment

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Utah only: read this when the property is in Utah. Outside Utah, skip it and say "State rules were not checked for [state]." Not legal advice. Use the version of each form in your brokerage's forms library, and take questions to your broker or the UAR Legal Hotline, (801) 676-5211.

## The Listing Agreement

- Utah's licensing rules require a licensee to define the scope of the agency "by executing a written agency agreement" with each principal, including "sellers the individual represents" (Utah Admin. Code R162-2f-401a(2)).
- The Utah Association of REALTORS form is the **Exclusive Right to Sell Listing Agreement and Agency Disclosure**. UAR revised it for the NAR settlement. Per UAR's forms update, the revised form:
  - separates the listing brokerage's fee from any buyer-brokerage compensation, instead of rolling them into one;
  - "Specifies in Section 2.1 the amount of compensation for the listing brokerage only";
  - "Authorizes in Section 2.2 the listing broker to advertise and pay compensation up to a certain amount" to a buyer's brokerage, with Section 2.3 covering compensation "to be added to the brokerage fee if a written brokerage compensation agreement is established";
  - "Emphasizes that brokerage fees are fully negotiable" and allows "flexibility for listing brokerage and seller to negotiate terms";
  - "Adds an end date to clearly state when the agreement ends."
- A temporary **Addendum to Exclusive Right to Sell Listing Agreement** brought agreements signed before the settlement into line; it isn't needed with the revised form.
- UAR's November 2024 forms update revised Section 2 again "to facilitate additional options and to give additional disclosures to consumers," and updated the **Real Estate Brokerage Compensation Agreement** (an agreement between the two brokerages) "to add flexibility with net and gross payments," with a new blank addendum to modify it.
- Section numbers can move between versions. In the prep, name the topic ("the section that sets your fee," "the section on offering a buyer's brokerage"), not a section number, unless the agent's own form is uploaded.

## Compensation in Utah After the Settlement

From UAR's settlement FAQ:
- The listing agreement "explains that compensation is not set by law and is fully negotiable," and compensation "should always be negotiated between agents and the consumers they serve."
- Sellers "must also approve in writing the sharing of the listing agent's compensation," and UAR says "this already takes place in the Exclusive Right to Sell Listing Agreement & Agency Disclosure form."
- The settlement "does not prohibit offers of compensation to buyer brokers or other buyer representatives outside the multiple listing service." A brokerage may show offers of compensation on its own website, "only on listings from their own brokerage."
- Seller concessions "cannot be conditioned on retaining or paying a buyer representative."

Also from Utah's rules:
- A licensee may not "take a net listing" (R162-2f-401b(1)(h)). The fee is agreed as the agent states it, never "whatever the price brings above the seller's number." A net sheet is an estimate for the seller, not a net listing.
- A licensee must "disclose in writing to each party to a transaction any compensation in addition to any real estate commission" and see that it is paid to the principal broker (R162-2f-401a(16)).

## Square Footage and Marketing Facts

- Before offering a residential property for sale, a licensee must disclose the source of any square footage used in marketing, "in the written agreement, executed with the seller, through which the licensee acquires the right to offer the property for sale," and in a written disclosure to the buyer (R162-2f-401a(8)).
- Before offering any property, a licensee must "make reasonable efforts to verify the accuracy and content of the information and data to be used in the marketing of the property" (R162-2f-401a(7)).
- What the skill does: when the county record, the prior MLS and the seller's questionnaire give different sizes, it lists each with its source and flags that the listing agreement names the source used.

## Another Agent

- A licensee may not "enter or attempt to enter into a concurrent agency representation when the licensee knows or should know that the principal has an existing agency representation agreement with another licensee" (R162-2f-401b(1)(k)). If the seller has signed with a relative or friend who is an agent, stop and talk to the broker.

## Fair Housing in Utah

- Utah's Fair Housing Act protects "race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, source of income, disability, sexual orientation, or gender identity" (Utah Code 57-21-5(1), effective May 6, 2026).
- It is a discriminatory housing practice to make any statement or advertisement that "directly or indirectly expresses any preference, limitation, or discrimination" based on those classes (57-21-5(2)), and, for profit, to induce someone to sell "by making a representation about the entry or prospective entry into the neighborhood" of people of a protected class (57-21-5(3)).
