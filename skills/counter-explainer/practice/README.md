# Counter Explainer Practice Files

Three made-up documents from one made-up home sale in Utah. Every person, brokerage, street and city is fictional. Nothing here is a real client document, and the two forms are shortened copies in the layout of Utah's state forms, not the forms themselves.

- `01 REPC 1428 Sample Ridge Lane.pdf`: the buyers' offer, dated October 28, 2026. The seller answered with a counteroffer.
- `02 Addendum No. 1 Seller Counteroffer.pdf`: the seller's counteroffer, which the buyers accepted on October 31, 2026.
- `03 Addendum No. 2 Home Warranty and Washer Dryer.pdf`: a new addendum from the seller, waiting on the buyers. The listing agent described it as "just the home warranty and the washer and dryer."

## What to Say

Attach all three files and say:

"I represent the buyers on 1428 Sample Ridge Lane. The listing agent sent Addendum No. 2 and says it's just a home warranty and the washer and dryer. What does it change, and can you draft a message to my buyers? This is a practice run, so treat today as November 9, 2026."

Then read the whole result before you look at the documents again, and check whether it caught everything Addendum No. 2 does.
