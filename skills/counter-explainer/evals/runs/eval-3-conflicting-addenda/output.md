## Conflicts, Gaps and Questions

**Conflict 1: seller money.** Addendum #2 (page 1, item 1) says the seller "will credit Buyer $2,500 at closing. This credit is in addition to Seller's closing cost contribution in Counteroffer #1." Addendum #3 (page 1, item 1) says "Seller's total contribution toward Buyer's closing costs, prepaid items and credits of any kind shall not exceed $4,000."
- If Addendum #2's reading holds: up to $6,500 in total ($4,000 toward costs plus the $2,500 credit).
- If Addendum #3's reading holds: $4,000 in total, with the $2,500 credit counted inside it.
- Addendum #3 says it was signed "At the request of Buyer's lender." If the loan allows no more than $4,000, what happens to the other $2,500 is a negotiation between the parties, in writing. Don't assume either outcome.
- Who settles it: your broker. Tell title and the lender once it's settled.

**Conflict 2: Closing Date.** Addendum #2 (page 1, item 2) says "The Closing Date is changed to December 22, 2026." Addendum #3 (page 1, item 2) says "The Closing Date remains December 18, 2026."
- If Addendum #2's date holds: closing December 22, 2026.
- If Addendum #3's date holds: closing December 18, 2026. "Remains" suggests whoever drafted it didn't account for Addendum #2, which is why this is a conflict and not a clean change.
- Who settles it: your broker. Tell title and the lender once it's settled.

The usual fix for both is one new addendum, signed by both sides, that states the total seller money (and whether the $2,500 credit is part of it) and the Closing Date once.
