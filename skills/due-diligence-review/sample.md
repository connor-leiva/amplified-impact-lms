## Issue List

**Tier 1: Safety and Health**
1. **Water heater venting (page 7, rated Safety).** "The vent connector is separated at the draft hood and slopes downward toward the chimney," and "exhaust spillage was observed at the draft hood" during a 5-minute draft test. The inspector notes this "can allow carbon monoxide into the home" and says to have a licensed plumber or HVAC contractor correct it before use. Action: ask the seller. Cost: needs bid.
2. **Radon at 6.8 pCi/L (radon report).** A 48-hour continuous monitor test in the basement family room averaged 6.8 pCi/L. EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or more. Action: ask the seller for mitigation by a certified radon mitigation professional and a post-mitigation test. Cost: needs bid. Health questions go to a certified radon professional.

**Tier 2: Major System or Structure**
3. **Roof (page 3, rated Further Evaluation).** "Widespread granule loss on all slopes," about a dozen cracked or lifted shingles on the south slope, and exposed nail heads. "The roof appears to be near the end of its service life." The inspector recommends a licensed roofing contractor evaluate repair or replacement. The disclosure gives the roof's age as "Approx. 2004." Action: get the roofer's evaluation and bid this week, then ask for a credit or price reduction from that bid. Cost: needs bid.
4. **Bath fan venting into the attic, with stained roof sheathing (page 9). Not in the inspector's summary.** The upstairs hall bath fan duct "ends in the attic insulation instead of venting to the exterior" (Repair), and there is "dark staining on the underside of the roof sheathing over an area about 4 feet by 6 feet around the bath fan location" (Further Evaluation). The sheathing was dry at the time of inspection, and "the inspector cannot determine the nature of the staining." I've rated it Tier 2 because it involves the roof structure and an unknown cause; the roofer evaluating item 3 can look at it on the same visit. Cost: needs bid.
5. **Grading at the northeast corner, with efflorescence on the basement wall (pages 4 and 10).** Soil slopes toward the foundation for about 8 feet, the downspout discharges at the foundation, and there is white efflorescence on the foundation wall in the unfinished storage area at that corner, which "shows that water has moved through the concrete at some time." It was dry during the inspection. This is the unfinished part of the basement the buyers want to finish. Action: ask the seller to regrade and extend the downspout. Cost: needs bid.

**Tier 3: Repair and Maintenance**

7. Basement bathroom toilet loose at the floor (page 10, Repair)
8. Furnace filter dirty; have the furnace serviced. It produced heat (page 6, Maintenance)

## Negotiating Timeline

*For strategy; confirm dates with your transactions team.*

- **Due Diligence Deadline:** 5:00 PM Mountain Time, Friday, November 13, 2026 (Sections 21 and 24(b)). 5 days from today.
- **By then (Section 8.1(b)):** cancel by written notice, and the $8,000 Earnest Money Deposit is released to the buyers; or resolve objections in writing with the seller.
- **No response period.** This REPC gives the seller no set number of days to answer and gives the buyers no cancellation window afterward. If the objection goes out Monday, November 9, the seller has November 10 through 5:00 PM Friday, November 13 to sign a resolution. Each day spent waiting for a roofing bid is a day less for the seller.
- **If neither happens (Section 8.1(c)):** the Due Diligence Condition is waived, and the Earnest Money Deposit becomes non-refundable except under the appraisal and financing conditions.
- **More time:** an extension has to be agreed in writing by all parties (Section 21) on the approved addendum, signed before 5:00 PM on November 13. Confirm with your broker.
- **This week, if the buyers want them:** the roofer's evaluation and bid; a sewer scope (the inspection didn't cover the sewer lateral); the irrigation company's transfer steps; a call to the city about setbacks and permits for a shop.
- **Later dates, for reference:** Financing & Appraisal Deadline November 24, 2026; Settlement Deadline December 4, 2026.
