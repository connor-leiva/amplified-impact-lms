## HOA Findings

1. **Proposed special assessment.** On October 21, 2026 the board voted to put a special assessment of $4,200 per unit to an owner vote at the annual meeting on December 15, 2026, to fund roof replacement for Buildings A to E. If approved, it is payable in two equal installments due February 1, 2027 and May 1, 2027 (p. 7). The resale certificate shows no special assessment currently levied (p. 1). Section 8.2 has the Seller box checked, but it covers only an assessment "approved, prior to the Settlement Deadline." The vote falls after the December 11, 2026 Settlement Deadline, so as written Morgan would pay it. This belongs in the objection now.
2. **Rental cap is full.** Article 12 (recorded April 12, 2023) caps leasing at 6 of 30 units (20%). All six slots are filled and four owners are on the waitlist (minutes p. 6 and p. 7). Leases need an initial term of at least 12 months, no rentals under 30 consecutive days, and an HOA lease addendum (p. 3). An owner who wants to lease notifies the board in writing and waits in order (12.2). The unit is owner occupied (p. 1), and under Utah law a buyer does not inherit a seller's grandfathered rental status anyway. Morgan's plan to rent in about two years depends on the waitlist. This is a decision for Morgan, not a repair. How the rules apply to a specific plan: confirm with a real estate attorney.
3. **Reserves.** The 2026 budget contributes $21,500 to reserves (p. 4) against a recommended $58,000 a year (p. 5). The study, dated March 14, 2024, reports 31% funded, a reserve balance of $148,200 at December 31, 2025, roofs at 0 to 2 years of remaining life, and a roof replacement estimate in the study of $310,000 (p. 5). The September minutes say reserves are not enough for the roofs (p. 6). Ask the HOA how it plans to close the gap; this is a question, not a verdict.
4. **Insurance.** The 2026 master premium is $31,400, up from $22,750 in 2025, which the October minutes call 38% higher (pp. 4 and 7). The master policy covers the building exteriors and common area; the owner insures the interior, improvements, personal property, liability, and should ask about the master deductible (Section 11.1, p. 2). Send Morgan to an insurance agent for a quote before November 17.
5. **Fees at closing.** The reinvestment fee is $2,490, which is 0.6% of the $415,000 price (resale certificate p. 1; Declaration Section 9.6). That is above 0.5%. Flag it for the title company to confirm; it is not for us to call it unlawful, and the covenant was recorded August 9, 2016, so how the current cap applies is a title or attorney question. Under Section 8.3(a), the Buyer box is checked, so Morgan pays it. The $350 buyer setup fee is Morgan's too; 8.3(a) says setup fees are not change of ownership fees.
6. **Dues.** $285 a month, covering the master policy, water and sewer, trash, snow removal, landscaping, exterior maintenance and reserves (p. 1). Paid through November 30, 2026. No pending litigation known (p. 1).
7. **Use rules.** Two pets per unit, two parking spaces, no RV, boat or trailer storage, and board approval for exterior changes (p. 3).

## Negotiating Timeline

For strategy; confirm dates with your transactions team.

- **Today:** Sunday, November 8, 2026.
- **Due Diligence Deadline:** Tuesday, November 17, 2026, 5:00 PM Mountain Time (4(b)). 9 calendar days.
- **No response period.** Under Section 12.1(b), by that time Morgan must either cancel in writing (the $4,000 Earnest Money Deposit goes back to Morgan) or have a written resolution signed with the seller. Sending an objection does not extend anything.
- **Silence:** under 12.1(c), the Due Diligence Condition is waived and the Earnest Money Deposit becomes non-refundable, except under the appraisal (12.2(a)) and financing (12.3(a)) conditions.
- **If the objection goes out Monday, November 9:** the seller's side has November 10 through 5:00 PM on November 17 to agree in writing. Each day of delay takes a day from that.
- **More time** takes a written extension signed by all parties (Section 25) before 5:00 PM on November 17. Confirm with your broker before relying on one.
- **Fits before the deadline:** an insurance quote; a radon test if Morgan wants one (EPA says a minimum of 48 hours, so it has to start by 5:00 PM on November 15 to finish by the deadline, and earlier to leave time to negotiate); a call to the HOA manager about the waitlist and the assessment.
- **After the deadline (for context only):** Appraisal Deadline November 24, Financing Deadline December 1, Settlement Deadline December 11, owner vote on the assessment December 15.
