## HOA Findings

- **Proposed special assessment (minutes, October 21, 2026, page 7).** The Board voted to put a special assessment of $4,200 per Unit to an owner vote at the annual meeting on December 15, 2026, to fund roof replacement for Buildings A to E together with reserves. If approved, it is payable in two equal installments due February 1, 2027 and May 1, 2027. The resale certificate shows no assessment currently levied and points to these minutes (page 1).
- **Who pays under the REPC.** Section 8.2 is checked "Seller," but it covers only "any special assessment as approved, prior to the Settlement Deadline." The Settlement Deadline is December 11, 2026; the vote is December 15. As written, if the assessment passes after closing, Morgan would owe it as the owner. This is the main item for the objection.
- **Roofs and reserves (pages 4 and 5).** The reserve study (March 14, 2024) gives the roofs 0 to 2 years of remaining life and recommends $58,000 a year in reserve contributions; the 2026 budget contributes $21,500. The reserve balance was $148,200 on December 31, 2025, and the study calls the reserves 31% funded. These are questions to ask, not a verdict, but they explain why an assessment is on the table.
- **Rentals (Third Amendment, Article 12, page 3; minutes, pages 6 and 7).** No more than 6 of the 30 Units (20%) may be leased at a time. All 6 slots are filled and 4 owners are on the waitlist (September 16 and October 21 minutes). An owner who wants to lease joins the waitlist in order and may lease only when a slot opens. Leases need an initial term of at least 12 months, nothing under 30 consecutive days, and the HOA's lease addendum. Section 12.6's right to keep renting ends when a unit is conveyed, and the unit is owner occupied now, so Morgan would start with no rental status. Morgan's plan to rent in about two years depends on a slot opening; nothing in the packet says when that might be. Utah law lets an association restrict rentals this way in its recorded declaration and requires certain exemptions (Section 12.5 lists them); whether any would apply to Morgan is a question for a real estate attorney.
- **Insurance (Declaration 11.1, page 2; budget, page 4; minutes, page 7).** The master policy covers the building exteriors and common areas; each owner insures the interior, improvements, personal property and liability, and the Declaration says to ask an insurance agent about coverage for the master policy deductible. The 2026 premium is $31,400, up from $22,750 in 2025; the minutes call it 38% higher. Morgan should get a quote from an insurance agent with these terms in hand.
- **Dues.** $285 a month, covering the master policy, water and sewer, trash, snow removal, landscaping, exterior maintenance and reserves (page 1).
- **Fees at closing (page 1).** Reinvestment fee $2,490, which is 0.6% of the $415,000 price, per Declaration Section 9.6 (added by the 2016 amendment). Utah Code 57-1-46 caps reinvestment fees at 0.5% of the property's value for covenants recorded on or after May 6, 2026 (0.25% in some low-amenity associations); before that change, the same 0.5% cap applied to covenants recorded on or after March 16, 2010. How it applies today to this 2016 covenant is a question for the title company. Since the fee is above 0.5% of the price, flag it for the title company to confirm. Under Section 8.3(a) the buyer pays change of ownership fees. The $350 buyer setup fee is also the buyer's (Section 8.3(a) says setup fees are not change of ownership fees).
- **Rules.** No more than two pets; two parking spaces per unit; no RV, boat or trailer storage.
- **Litigation.** None known (page 1).

## Negotiating Timeline

*For strategy; confirm dates with your transactions team.*

- **Due Diligence Deadline:** 5:00 PM Mountain Time, Tuesday, November 17, 2026 (Sections 4(b) and 25). 9 days from today.
- **By then (Section 12.1(b)):** cancel by written notice, and the $4,000 Earnest Money Deposit is released to Morgan; or resolve objections in writing with the seller.
- **No response period.** The seller has no set number of days to answer, and there's no cancellation window after the deadline. If the objection goes out Monday, November 9, the seller has November 10 through 5:00 PM on November 17 to sign a resolution. Sending it early is the only way to buy negotiating time; more time takes a written extension signed by all parties (Section 25).
- **If neither happens (Section 12.1(c)):** the Due Diligence Condition is waived, and the Earnest Money Deposit becomes non-refundable except under the appraisal and financing conditions (Sections 12.2(a) and 12.3(a)).
- **Radon:** EPA says a test should run at least 48 hours, so there's time to test this week if Morgan wants to. Utah DEQ says 1 in 3 Utah homes have elevated radon levels.
- **Other dates, for reference:** Appraisal Deadline November 24, 2026; Financing Deadline December 1, 2026; Settlement Deadline December 11, 2026; HOA annual meeting and assessment vote December 15, 2026.
