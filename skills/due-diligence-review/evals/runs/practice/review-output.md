## Issue List

### Tier 1: Safety and Health

1. **Water heater exhaust spillage** (inspection p. 7, Safety; summary item 1). "The vent connector is separated at the draft hood and slopes downward toward the chimney. During a 5-minute draft test, exhaust spillage was observed at the draft hood. This can allow carbon monoxide into the home. Have a licensed plumber or HVAC contractor correct before use." Action: ask the seller to repair. Cost: needs bid.
2. **Radon at 6.8 pCi/L** (radon report p. 1). A 48-hour continuous monitor test in the basement family room, November 4 to 6, 2026, averaged 6.8 pCi/L, above the EPA action level of 4 pCi/L. The disclosure says the home was never tested and has no mitigation system. Action: ask for mitigation by a certified radon mitigation professional and a post-mitigation test. Cost: needs bid. Health questions go to a certified radon professional.

### Tier 2: Major System or Structure

3. **Bath exhaust duct ends in the attic, with staining on the roof sheathing** (inspection p. 9). **Not in the inspector's summary.** The upstairs hall bath fan duct "ends in the attic insulation instead of venting to the exterior" (Repair). "Dark staining on the underside of the roof sheathing over an area about 4 feet by 6 feet around the bath fan location. The sheathing was dry by moisture meter at the time of inspection. The inspector cannot determine the nature of the staining. Have a qualified contractor evaluate the sheathing and extend the fan duct to the exterior" (Further Evaluation). Tiered 2 because it is moisture in the roof structure with an evaluation pending. What the staining is: for the evaluating contractor, not this review. Cost: needs bid.
4. **Roof near the end of its service life** (inspection p. 3, Further Evaluation; summary item 2). Widespread granule loss on all slopes, about a dozen cracked or lifted shingles on the south slope, exposed nail heads; "The roof appears to be near the end of its service life. Have a licensed roofing contractor evaluate." The inspector did not determine the age. Action: get the roofer's evaluation and bid inside the window. Cost: needs bid.
5. **Grading toward the foundation, with efflorescence at the same corner** (inspection pp. 4 and 10; summary item 3, Repair). Soil slopes toward the foundation for about 8 feet at the northeast corner, and the downspout discharges at the foundation (p. 4). White efflorescence on the foundation wall in the unfinished storage area at that corner; "Efflorescence shows that water has moved through the concrete at some time" (p. 10, Monitor). This is the part of the basement Avery and Jordan would finish. Action: ask the seller to regrade and extend the downspout. Cost: needs bid.

## Negotiating Timeline

For strategy; confirm dates with your transactions team.

- **Today:** Sunday, November 8, 2026.
- **Due Diligence Deadline:** Friday, November 13, 2026, 5:00 PM Mountain Time (24(b)). 5 calendar days.
- **No response period.** Under Section 8.1(b), by that time Avery and Jordan must either cancel in writing (the $8,000 Earnest Money Deposit goes back to them) or have a written resolution signed with the seller. Sending an objection does not extend the deadline.
- **Silence:** under 8.1(c), the Due Diligence Condition is waived and the Earnest Money Deposit becomes non-refundable, except under 8.2(a) (appraisal) and 8.3(b)(i) (financing).
- **If the objection goes out Monday, November 9:** the seller's side has November 10 through 5:00 PM on November 13 to agree in writing.
- **Evaluations:** the roofer, the sheathing evaluation and any sewer scope have to report before November 13 to support the objection. If they can't, the choices are to ask for the repair by a licensed contractor of that trade, ask for more time in writing, or decide without them.
- **More time** takes a written extension signed by all parties (Section 21) before 5:00 PM on November 13. Confirm with your broker before relying on one.
- **After the deadline (for context only):** Financing & Appraisal Deadline November 24, 2026; Settlement Deadline December 4, 2026; final walk-through no earlier than seven days before Settlement (Section 11).
