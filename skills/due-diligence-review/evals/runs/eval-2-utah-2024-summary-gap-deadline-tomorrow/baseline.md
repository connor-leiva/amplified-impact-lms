## The big one: the electrical panel (page 9)

The summary page doesn't mention it, but the Electrical section on page 9 says the home has a **Federal Pacific Electric (FPE) Stab-Lok panel**, with **scorching on the 20-amp kitchen breaker and the adjacent bus bar**, and several breakers that don't seat firmly. The inspector calls it a fire hazard and recommends immediate evaluation by a licensed electrician.

FPE Stab-Lok panels are widely known in the industry for breakers that may fail to trip, and many insurers and electricians recommend replacing them outright. Replacement typically runs **$2,000[EN DASH]$4,500** depending on the service and any wiring updates, so this is worth negotiating.
