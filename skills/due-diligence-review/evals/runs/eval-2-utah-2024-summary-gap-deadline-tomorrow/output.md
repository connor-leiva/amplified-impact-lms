## Negotiating Timeline

*For strategy; confirm dates with your transactions team.*

- **Due Diligence Deadline:** 5:00 PM Mountain Time, Monday, November 9, 2026 (Sections 21 and 24(b)). 1 day from today.
- **What the buyers can do by then (Section 8.1(b)):** cancel by written notice, and the $5,000 Earnest Money Deposit is released to them; or resolve their objections in writing with the seller.
- **No response period.** This REPC gives the seller no set number of days to answer an objection and gives the buyers no cancellation window afterward. Sending the objection does not extend anything. If the request goes out Monday morning, the seller has until 5:00 PM that same day to sign a resolution.
- **If the buyers do neither (Section 8.1(c)):** they are deemed to have waived the Due Diligence Condition, and the Earnest Money Deposit becomes non-refundable except under the appraisal and financing conditions (Sections 8.2(a) and 8.3(b)(i)).
- **More time:** an extension has to be agreed in writing by all parties (Section 21), on your brokerage's approved addendum, and signed before 5:00 PM Monday. Confirm with your broker before relying on it.
- **Delivery:** notices count when received by the other party, their agent or their brokerage (Section 18). Send tonight or first thing Monday and confirm receipt.
- **Radon:** EPA says a radon test should run at least 48 hours, so one can't finish before the deadline without an extension. Utah DEQ says 1 in 3 Utah homes have elevated radon levels.
- **Later deadlines, for reference:** Financing & Appraisal Deadline November 20, 2026; Settlement Deadline December 1, 2026.
