# Ethics, Fair Housing and Privacy for Expired and FSBO Outreach

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Sources at the end. This is not legal advice; ethics and fair housing questions go to the broker.

A first touch to an owner is the agent's own written statement, and a solicitation. Keep it to the property, the listing's own dates and the agent's offer. Never describe the owner, and never explain why the home didn't sell.

## Another Brokerage's Listing (NAR Code of Ethics, Article 16)

The NAR Code of Ethics (2026 edition, effective January 1, 2026) binds REALTORS; many brokerages hold every agent to it.
- **Article 16:** REALTORS shall not take any action inconsistent with exclusive representation or exclusive brokerage relationship agreements that other REALTORS have with clients.
- **Standard of Practice 16-2:** general mailings and canvassing of a whole area are allowed. Article 16 treats two kinds of solicitation as unethical: telephone or personal solicitation of owners identified by a sign, an MLS compilation or another information service as having exclusively listed their property with another REALTOR; and mail or other written solicitation aimed specifically at owners identified that way.
- **Standard of Practice 16-4:** REALTORS shall not solicit a listing that is currently listed exclusively with another broker. If the listing broker, when asked, refuses to disclose the expiration date and the kind of listing, the REALTOR may contact the owner to get that information, and may discuss a future listing or take one that starts when the existing listing expires.
- **Standard of Practice 16-13:** dealings about exclusively listed property are carried on with the client's broker, not the client, unless that broker consents or the client starts them.

What the skill does with that:
- Expired listings (the agreement has ended) may be solicited. Withdrawn, Temporarily Off Market, Active - No Show and similar statuses may still be under an agreement, so they get no drafts. Canceled rows wait for the agent's confirmation (see the guide's status table).
- An address that shows up again as Active, Coming Soon or Under Contract has relisted: no drafts.
- A FSBO ad that says it is listed with a flat-fee or limited-service brokerage: no drafts.
- Every expired letter says "If you've already made other plans, or you're working with an agent again, please set this aside," because a listing can relist between the export and the mail.
- Check before sending asks for a fresh MLS search of every address before anything goes out.

## The Prior Agent and Brokerage (Article 15)

- **Article 15:** REALTORS shall not knowingly or recklessly make false or misleading statements about other real estate professionals, their businesses or their business practices. **Standard of Practice 15-2:** that includes not publishing, repeating or republishing false or misleading statements made by others.
- The list never says why a listing expired or why a FSBO hasn't sold. So the skill says nothing at all about the prior agent, the brokerage, the price, the photos, the marketing or the reason. The listing office stays in the agent-only list.
- If the agent asks for that angle ("they were overpriced," "their agent did nothing"), leave it out of every draft and flag it in one line: "Left out: what you said about the prior agents. The list doesn't say why these expired, and the Code of Ethics (Article 15) bars misleading statements about other agents."

## Honest Offers (Article 12)

- **Article 12:** REALTORS shall be honest and truthful in their real estate communications and present a true picture in their advertising, marketing and other representations.
- No draft claims a buyer, a waiting list, a sale "in 30 days" or a price. If the agent asks to say they have buyers, leave it out and flag it: "Left out: a buyer claim. If you have a specific buyer client for a specific home, that's a different conversation, and this skill doesn't write it."
- No price, price change, value or net figure in any draft. The pricing conversation is the agent's (Pricing Conversation Prep, `pricing-conversation-prep`, prepares it once an appointment is booked).

## Fair Housing

- **Federal (Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604):** race, color, religion, sex, handicap (disability), familial status and national origin. Section 3604(c) bars any notice, statement or advertisement about the sale of a dwelling that indicates a preference, limitation or discrimination based on these. Section 3604(e) bars inducing or trying to induce, for profit, anyone to sell or rent a dwelling by representations about the entry or prospective entry into the neighborhood of persons of a particular protected class (blockbusting).
- **Utah (Utah Code 57-21-5):** race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, source of income, disability, sexual orientation and gender identity. Subsection (2) covers written statements and notices that express a preference; subsection (3) covers inducing a sale through representations about who is moving into the neighborhood.
- **Other states and cities** add their own classes. In general mode, treat any remark about a person as off limits.

| The list or the agent's message says | What goes in the drafts |
|---|---|
| A FSBO ad's words about people or the area ("great family neighborhood," "ideal for retirees," "walk to the church," "near the elementary school") | Nothing. Use only the ad's property features ("new carpet, central air, large corner lot") |
| Who lives in the area, who is moving in, schools, safety, crime | Nothing, in any draft or opener. Never a reason to sell |
| The owner's situation (relocating, divorce, death, estate, a trust, tenants, health, money, "motivated," "must sell fast") | Nothing. One generic agent-only flag counts what was left out |
| The owner's name | The greeting and the mail-to line only. Never a guess from a name about anything |

## Privacy and Agent-Only Fields

- **Agent-only MLS fields** (Private Remarks, showing instructions, lockbox and access details, tenant details) never appear in any draft, and are never quoted, even in Flags. One line says they were set aside.
- **Phone numbers:** no draft repeats any phone number, and the agent-only list doesn't either: it points to the row ("row 3"). [Agent Phone] is the agent's own number.
- **Emails:** an owner's email address appears only as the "To" line of that owner's own email draft, never elsewhere.
- **Money:** no loan, equity, mortgage, payoff or net figures, and never ask for, repeat or handle wire, account, routing or Social Security numbers.

## Door-Knocks and Mailboxes

- Federal law makes it an offense to knowingly and willfully put mailable matter with no postage in a mailbox approved by the Postal Service, with intent to avoid postage (18 U.S.C. 1725). So a note left at the door goes on or by the door, never in the mailbox.
- City rules on door-to-door solicitation (hours, permits) vary and were not checked. A No Soliciting or No Trespassing sign means no knock.
