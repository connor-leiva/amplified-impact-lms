# Utah Notes for Expired and FSBO Outreach

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Utah only: read this when the properties are in Utah. Outside Utah, skip it and say "State rules were not checked." Sources at the end. Not legal advice; questions go to the broker or an attorney.

## Utah's Telephone Solicitation Law (Utah Code Title 13, Chapter 25a)

What it says:
- **A call to list a home is a telephone solicitation.** The definition is "the initiation of a telephone call or message for a commercial purpose," including calls "encouraging the person called to sell real or personal property" (13-25a-102, effective May 1, 2024).
- **Hours, Sundays and holidays.** Without prior express consent, a telephone solicitation may not be made to a residential or cell phone between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. local time, on a Sunday, or on a legal holiday (13-25a-103(3), effective May 4, 2022).
- **Identify and stop.** An unsolicited call must identify the caller and the business, state its purpose promptly, and stop if the person gives a negative response (13-25a-103(5)). The same section requires following the federal TCPA (13-25a-103(4)).
- **Utah numbers on the national Registry.** No unsolicited call to a Utah number on the National Do Not Call Registry (13-25a-108).
- **Asked not to be called.** A telephone solicitor may not call a person who has told them, in writing or orally, that they don't want calls from them (13-25a-107.2).
- **Penalties.** Administrative fines of $100 to $2,500 per violation (13-25a-105), and a private suit for $500 or the loss, more for a knowing and willful violation (13-25a-107).

**The real estate exemption.** Sections 13-25a-103 and 13-25a-108 "do not apply to a person who holds and acts within the scope of a license" issued by the Utah Division of Real Estate (13-25a-111, effective May 1, 2024). So for a licensed agent calling about listing a home, Utah's own hour, Sunday, holiday and state Registry rules may not apply. What still applies:
- The federal rules, which apply in every state: the 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. window, the National Do Not Call Registry and the team's internal list (`outreach-rules.md`).
- 13-25a-107.2 (not exempted): never call someone who said they don't want your calls.
- The exemption is for the licensee acting within the license. A teammate, assistant or calling service who isn't licensed is not covered.

**What the skill does (house practice):** in Utah mode the plan schedules no calls on a Sunday or a Utah legal holiday and keeps every call between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m., even for licensed callers. That keeps one rule for the whole team, licensed or not. Whether a licensee may call on those days is a question for the broker. Each Utah run says this once, in Check before sending.

**Texts.** The definition says "call or message." Whether Utah's section reaches text messages was not confirmed for this file. The skill drafts no texts without prior express written consent anyway.

## Utah Legal Holidays (Utah Code 63G-1-301)

The version in effect from May 6, 2026 through December 31, 2026 lists: January 1; the third Monday of January; the third Monday of February; Good Friday; Easter Sunday; the last Monday of May; June 19 (observed on a Monday); July 4; July 24; the first Monday of September; the second Monday of October; November 11; the fourth Thursday of November; December 25; any day of fasting or thanksgiving the President or the Governor proclaims; and every Sunday. A fixed-date holiday that falls on a Saturday moves to the Friday before; on a Sunday, to the Monday after. The version effective January 1, 2027 keeps the list and observes June 19 on its own date (moved the same way as the others).

Dates that matter for plans made in late 2026 and early 2027 (worked out from the rules above, check against a calendar):
- Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (Veterans Day)
- Thursday, November 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving)
- Friday, December 25, 2026 (Christmas)
- Friday, January 1, 2027 (New Year's Day)
- Monday, January 18, 2027 (Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Day)
- Monday, February 15, 2027 (Presidents' Day)

The Governor can proclaim more; if one is announced, add it.

## UtahRealEstate.com Statuses

From UtahRealEstate.com's "Quick Guide to MLS Listing Statuses":
- **Expired:** "The termination date of the listing agreement has passed, and a valid listing agreement for this listing is no longer in effect."
- **Withdrawn:** "A valid listing agreement is in effect; however, the seller and listing broker have agreed to withdraw the listing from being marketed through the MLS."
- **Canceled:** "The listing agreement has been terminated prior to the expiration date specified in the listing agreement." Its rules guide adds that a listing should only be canceled when the agreement has been canceled and terminated, and that a new MLS listing needs a new agreement or an extension.
- **Active - No Show (formerly Off Market):** temporarily not available for showings at the seller's request; a valid listing agreement is in effect.

Park City, Washington County and Iron County MLSs set their own definitions; not checked here.

## The Brokerage Name (Utah Administrative Code R162-2f-401h)

A licensee may not advertise real estate services without "clearly and conspicuously identifying in the advertisement the name of the brokerage," as on the Division of Real Estate's records. A solicitation letter or email offers the agent's services, so every draft signs with the brokerage name ([Brokerage Name] when not given, flagged).
