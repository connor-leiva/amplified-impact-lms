# Expired and FSBO Outreach Guide

Part 1 is the list: where it comes from, the columns, who may be solicited, the channels, the sort, the draft shapes and the touch plan. Part 2 is ethics, fair housing and privacy.

# Part 1: The List, the Channels and the Drafts

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Vendor and MLS facts come from each one's own pages, linked under Sources. Menus change; if a click path here doesn't match what the agent sees, trust the screen and say so. Calling, texting and email law is in `outreach-rules.md` (federal) and `utah-calling-rules.md` (Utah). Ethics and fair housing are in Part 2 below.

## Where Lists Come From

Use this to read the columns and, when nothing was uploaded, to tell the agent in one line where to get the list.

| Source | Where the agent gets it | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| MLS expired search | Search the MLS for Expired listings in the area and date range, then use the MLS's export to CSV or Excel. The click path depends on the MLS; UtahRealEstate.com's listing export path was not confirmed in its help center | MLS exports carry agent-only fields (Private Remarks, showing and lockbox details). Owner names, mailing addresses and phones are often added from tax records or a data service, with a Do Not Call status column |
| Landvoice | Open one of your lists, then Print/Export in the top menu, then CSV | Landvoice says it marks every phone number on the National Do Not Call Registry. Read its DNC marking as the DNC status |
| REDX (Vortex) | Leads arrive in Vortex, REDX's lead manager. Use its export for the list. The click path was not confirmed in REDX's help pages | REDX says its numbers are checked against the Do Not Call Registry. Read its marking as the DNC status |
| Vulcan7 | Vulcan7 says agents can export leads. The click path was not confirmed | Vulcan7 says DNC filtering is built in. Read its marking as the DNC status |
| The agent's own list | A spreadsheet or a pasted list (yard signs, classified ads, FSBO websites) | Usually no Do Not Call column at all, so no calls until the agent scrubs |

A vendor's DNC marking is the vendor's statement, as of the date it checked. The agent owns the scrub (Check before sending asks for the date).

## Status: Who May Be Solicited

| Status or sign in the list | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Expired, expiration date on or before today | EXPIRED: may be solicited | The listing agreement has ended. UtahRealEstate.com: "a valid listing agreement for this listing is no longer in effect" |
| Expired, but the expiration date is after today | HELD OUT: confirm first | The dates disagree. Check the listing in the MLS before any contact |
| Expired, and the same address appears again as Active, Coming Soon or Under Contract | HELD OUT: relisted | It may be listed again, with any brokerage (NAR Article 16) |
| Withdrawn | HELD OUT: may be under a listing agreement | UtahRealEstate.com: "A valid listing agreement is in effect." Article 16 and Standard of Practice 16-4 |
| Canceled | HELD OUT until confirmed | UtahRealEstate.com uses Canceled only when the agreement was terminated, but a canceled listing is often re-entered by the same brokerage under a new agreement, and other MLSs use the word differently. Drafts only after the agent confirms with a fresh MLS search or the listing brokerage that no agreement is in effect (16-4 lets them ask the listing broker) |
| Temporarily Off Market, Active - No Show, Off Market, Hold | HELD OUT: may be under a listing agreement | UtahRealEstate.com: Active - No Show (formerly Off Market) means "a valid listing agreement is in effect" |
| Active, Coming Soon, Under Contract, Pending, Backup, Sold, Closed | HELD OUT: not a prospect | Listed, sold or under contract |
| Blank or a status not in this table | HELD OUT: unknown status | Ask the agent |
| FSBO ad | FSBO: may be solicited | Unless a row below applies |
| FSBO ad that says no agents, no realtors, no brokers, no solicitations, no solicitors, principals only, or agents need not call | SKIPPED: no agents | Respect the owner's ad. No letter, email, call or knock. Flag it with the ad's words |
| FSBO ad that says it is listed with a brokerage (flat fee, MLS-only, limited service) | HELD OUT: may be under a listing agreement | A flat-fee MLS listing is still a listing with a brokerage. Article 16 |
| FSBO ad that says "agents welcome" or "will work with buyer's agents" | FSBO: may be solicited | That invites buyers' agents. It is not a request to be solicited, so the first touch is still a solicitation, written the same way |

Held-out and skipped rows get no drafts. Each is listed with its reason. Never draft for them because the agent asks; say what would change the answer (for Canceled: the agent's confirmation).

## Channels, Row by Row

Mark every solicitable row with each channel: open or closed, and why.

| Channel | Open when | Closed when |
|---|---|---|
| LETTER | The row has a mailing address (the owner's mailing address; otherwise the property address, flagged) | No address at all |
| EMAIL | The list gives an email address. The email replaces the letter as the first touch | No email address |
| CALL | There is a phone number, and either (a) the list marks it not on the Registry ("Clean," "N," "No," "Not DNC," "Not on DNC") and, when the list gives a check date, it is 31 days old or less; or (b) the agent says in this conversation they scrubbed the numbers against the National Do Not Call Registry and the team's internal do-not-call list, and the row isn't marked DNC | No phone. Marked DNC ("DNC," "Y," "Yes," "On DNC"). DNC status blank or unreadable. A check date more than 31 days before today ("rescrub first"). No DNC column and no scrub statement from the agent. The owner asked not to be called, or is on the team's internal list |
| TEXT | The list shows this owner's prior express written consent to get marketing texts from the agent or the brokerage, with the number (a consent column with a yes) | Every other row. A phone number, a "Mobile" tag, a vendor lead or an ad that says "call or text" is not consent |
| KNOCK | Expired: the owner's mailing address is the property, or no separate mailing address is given. FSBO: always (the ad points to the property) | The owner mails elsewhere (an absentee owner, an LLC or a trust with an office address): the property may be someone else's home, so no knock |

Why the call rule: the Do Not Call rules cover calls to expired and FSBO owners when the purpose is to list their home (Florida Realtors, "Do Not Call," updated February 9, 2026; NAR's cold calling page). The Registry safe harbor counts on a Registry copy no more than 31 days old (`outreach-rules.md`). The skill never claims a buyer, so every call it drafts is a listing solicitation.

Why texts are closed: a marketing text needs prior express written consent under the federal rules, and the Registry covers texts (`outreach-rules.md`). Lists of expired and FSBO owners almost never carry that consent. Say so once, in Flags.

## Sorting

Work it out in code, from the list's own facts. The order is a work order, not a judgment about any owner.

- **Expired:** expiration date, newest first (those owners have had the least time to relist), then days on market, longest first.
- **FSBO:** the ad or lead date, oldest first, then asking price as listed. Check each ad is still up.
- **Price change:** original price minus the final price, worked out in code and marked "worked out" in the table. It stays in the agent-only list.
- **Old expirations:** an expiration more than 30 days before today gets a line: "check the MLS for a relist before sending."
- **Held out and skipped:** their own table, in file order, with the reason.

## Draft Shapes

One section per owner, in sort order, headed with the owner's name as the list gives it, the address, the decision and the open channels in capitals.

### First-Touch Letter (Expired)

```
Mail to: [Owner name as on the tax record], [mailing address from the list]

Hi [First names],

I'm [Agent Name] with [Brokerage Name]. I saw that the listing for your home at [street address] ended on [weekday, month day], after [N] days on the market.

[One or two facts from the list, plainly: "It's a 4-bedroom, 3-bath home with 2,840 square feet."]

If you still plan to sell, I'd like to hear what matters most to you and show you how I would market it. If you've already made other plans, or you're working with an agent again, please set this aside.

You can reach me at [Agent Phone] or [Agent Email].

[Agent Name]
[Brokerage Name]
```

150 words or fewer. Owner name for an LLC, trust or estate: "Hello," as the greeting, the name exactly as the list gives it on the mail-to line, and nothing said about it.

### First-Touch Letter (FSBO)

```
Mail to: [Owner name], [property address]

Hi [First name],

I'm [Agent Name] with [Brokerage Name]. I saw your [source as listed: "ad on a FSBO website," "yard sign"] for [street address].

[One or two features the ad itself lists, plainly: "New roof in 2024, a fenced yard and RV parking."]

If you'd like a second set of hands with marketing, showings or the paperwork, I'd be glad to talk. If you'd rather keep selling it yourself, I understand, and I hope it goes smoothly.

You can reach me at [Agent Phone] or [Agent Email].

[Agent Name]
[Brokerage Name]
```

### First-Touch Email

The same words as the letter, with a Title Case subject that says what it is ("Your Home At 3389 S Example Creek Rd"), never one that pretends to be a reply. 150 words or fewer before the footer. Footer, from `outreach-rules.md` (CAN-SPAM):

```
This is a marketing email from [Agent Name], [Brokerage Name]. To stop these emails, reply UNSUBSCRIBE. [Sender Postal Address]
```

### Call Opener (CALL rows only)

```
When: [the plan's call days], between 8 AM and 8 PM [owner's time zone]. Dialed by hand, live; no recorded or artificial-voice messages.

"Hi, is this [First name]? This is [Agent Name] with [Brokerage Name]. I'm calling because I saw your listing at [street] ended on [weekday]. Are you still thinking about selling?"

If yes: "What matters most to you in the next step?" Listen. Then: "Could I stop by this week and show you how I'd market it?"
If no, or not now: "Thanks for telling me. I won't keep calling." No more calls in this plan.
If they ask not to be called: thank them, end the call, and add the number to your internal do-not-call list today.
Voicemail: "Hi [First name], it's [Agent Name] with [Brokerage Name], about your home on [street]. My number is [Agent Phone]."
```

The FSBO version opens: "I saw your ad for [street]." Never "I have a buyer."

### Door-Knock Note (KNOCK rows only)

```
At the door: "Hi, I'm [Agent Name] with [Brokerage Name]. I [sent you a note / saw your ad] about your home, and wanted to say hello in person and leave my card."
Left at the door if no one answers (never in the mailbox): "Sorry I missed you. I stopped by about your home at [street]. [Agent Name], [Brokerage Name], [Agent Phone]."
```

A No Soliciting or No Trespassing sign means no knock: the letter stands alone. Check the city's door-to-door rules (hours, permits); they were not checked here.

## The Touch Plan

Three or four touches over two to three weeks, starting today, worked out in code and written with weekday and date.

| Touch | When | Letter or email only | With CALL open | With KNOCK open |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Today | Mail the letter, or send the email | Also call, within the window | |
| 2 | Day 2 to 5 | | Second call on a different day | Knock on a weekday afternoon or Saturday daytime |
| 3 | About day 7 | Follow-up note (letter or email) | Third call only if no conversation yet | |
| 4 | About day 14 to 21 | Last note | No calls | |

- Calls: 8 AM to 8 PM in the owner's local time (the house window, inside the federal 8 AM to 9 PM). Utah mode: no calls on a Sunday or a Utah legal holiday (`utah-calling-rules.md`); move them to the next allowed day and say so.
- Stop all touches for an owner who says no, asks not to be contacted, or relists with any brokerage.
- Follow-up note (touch 3), the same for every owner, filled in: "Hi [First name], I wrote last week about your home at [street]. If selling is still on your mind, I'd be glad to talk whenever it suits you. [Agent Name], [Brokerage Name], [Agent Phone]." As an email, it gets the footer.
- Last note (touch 4): "Hi [First name], this is my last note about [street]. If your plans change, I'm at [Agent Phone]. [Agent Name], [Brokerage Name]." As an email, it gets the footer.

# Part 2: Ethics, Fair Housing and Privacy

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Sources at the end. This is not legal advice; ethics and fair housing questions go to the broker.

A first touch to an owner is the agent's own written statement, and a solicitation. Keep it to the property, the listing's own dates and the agent's offer. Never describe the owner, and never explain why the home didn't sell.

## Another Brokerage's Listing (NAR Code of Ethics, Article 16)

The NAR Code of Ethics (2026 edition, effective January 1, 2026) binds REALTORS; many brokerages hold every agent to it.
- **Article 16:** REALTORS shall not take any action inconsistent with exclusive representation or exclusive brokerage relationship agreements that other REALTORS have with clients.
- **Standard of Practice 16-2:** general mailings and canvassing of a whole area are allowed. Article 16 treats two kinds of solicitation as unethical: telephone or personal solicitation of owners identified by a sign, an MLS compilation or another information service as having exclusively listed their property with another REALTOR; and mail or other written solicitation aimed specifically at owners identified that way.
- **Standard of Practice 16-4:** REALTORS shall not solicit a listing that is currently listed exclusively with another broker. If the listing broker, when asked, refuses to disclose the expiration date and the kind of listing, the REALTOR may contact the owner to get that information, and may discuss a future listing or take one that starts when the existing listing expires.
- **Standard of Practice 16-13:** dealings about exclusively listed property are carried on with the client's broker, not the client, unless that broker consents or the client starts them.

What the skill does with that:
- Expired listings (the agreement has ended) may be solicited. Withdrawn, Temporarily Off Market, Active - No Show and similar statuses may still be under an agreement, so they get no drafts. Canceled rows wait for the agent's confirmation (see the status table in Part 1).
- An address that shows up again as Active, Coming Soon or Under Contract has relisted: no drafts.
- A FSBO ad that says it is listed with a flat-fee or limited-service brokerage: no drafts.
- Every expired letter says "If you've already made other plans, or you're working with an agent again, please set this aside," because a listing can relist between the export and the mail.
- Check before sending asks for a fresh MLS search of every address before anything goes out.

## The Prior Agent and Brokerage (Article 15)

- **Article 15:** REALTORS shall not knowingly or recklessly make false or misleading statements about other real estate professionals, their businesses or their business practices. **Standard of Practice 15-2:** that includes not publishing, repeating or republishing false or misleading statements made by others.
- The list never says why a listing expired or why a FSBO hasn't sold. So the skill says nothing at all about the prior agent, the brokerage, the price, the photos, the marketing or the reason. The listing office stays in the agent-only list.
- If the agent asks for that angle ("they were overpriced," "their agent did nothing"), leave it out of every draft and flag it in one line: "Left out: what you said about the prior agents. The list doesn't say why these expired, and the Code of Ethics (Article 15) bars misleading statements about other agents."

## Honest Offers (Article 12)

- **Article 12:** REALTORS shall be honest and truthful in their real estate communications and present a true picture in their advertising, marketing and other representations.
- No draft claims a buyer, a waiting list, a sale "in 30 days" or a price. If the agent asks to say they have buyers, leave it out and flag it: "Left out: a buyer claim. If you have a specific buyer client for a specific home, that's a different conversation, and this skill doesn't write it."
- No price, price change, value or net figure in any draft. The pricing conversation is the agent's (Pricing Conversation Prep, `pricing-conversation-prep`, prepares it once an appointment is booked).

## Fair Housing

- **Federal (Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604):** race, color, religion, sex, handicap (disability), familial status and national origin. Section 3604(c) bars any notice, statement or advertisement about the sale of a dwelling that indicates a preference, limitation or discrimination based on these. Section 3604(e) bars inducing or trying to induce, for profit, anyone to sell or rent a dwelling by representations about the entry or prospective entry into the neighborhood of persons of a particular protected class (blockbusting).
- **Utah (Utah Code 57-21-5):** race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, source of income, disability, sexual orientation and gender identity. Subsection (2) covers written statements and notices that express a preference; subsection (3) covers inducing a sale through representations about who is moving into the neighborhood.
- **Other states and cities** add their own classes. In general mode, treat any remark about a person as off limits.

| The list or the agent's message says | What goes in the drafts |
|---|---|
| A FSBO ad's words about people or the area ("great family neighborhood," "ideal for retirees," "walk to the church," "near the elementary school") | Nothing. Use only the ad's property features ("new carpet, central air, large corner lot") |
| Who lives in the area, who is moving in, schools, safety, crime | Nothing, in any draft or opener. Never a reason to sell |
| The owner's situation (relocating, divorce, death, estate, a trust, tenants, health, money, "motivated," "must sell fast") | Nothing. One generic agent-only flag counts what was left out |
| The owner's name | The greeting and the mail-to line only. Never a guess from a name about anything |

## Privacy and Agent-Only Fields

- **Agent-only MLS fields** (Private Remarks, showing instructions, lockbox and access details, tenant details) never appear in any draft, and are never quoted, even in Flags. One line says they were set aside.
- **Phone numbers:** no draft repeats any phone number, and the agent-only list doesn't either: it points to the row ("row 3"). [Agent Phone] is the agent's own number.
- **Emails:** an owner's email address appears only as the "To" line of that owner's own email draft, never elsewhere.
- **Money:** no loan, equity, mortgage, payoff or net figures, and never ask for, repeat or handle wire, account, routing or Social Security numbers.

## Door-Knocks and Mailboxes

- Federal law makes it an offense to knowingly and willfully put mailable matter with no postage in a mailbox approved by the Postal Service, with intent to avoid postage (18 U.S.C. 1725). So a note left at the door goes on or by the door, never in the mailbox.
- City rules on door-to-door solicitation (hours, permits) vary and were not checked. A No Soliciting or No Trespassing sign means no knock.
