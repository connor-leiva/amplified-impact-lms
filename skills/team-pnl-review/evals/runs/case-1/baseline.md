## Gingersnap Realty Team: First Half 2026 Financial Review

### Summary
Your team generated $725,098 in total income and $38,706 in net income for January through June, a 5.3% net margin. Results were uneven: January lost money, while March and April were strong. April stands out with a 100% gross margin, which suggests the cost of goods sold for April may be missing.

### What Changed
- **April:** Revenue dropped to $64,891, less than half of March, yet net income was the highest of the half at $18,674. April shows $0 in agent commission splits, referral fees and brokerage fees. Since you closed 10 deals in April per Sisu, it looks like April's commission splits were never recorded. If April's splits ran at the roughly 55% to 60% of revenue seen in other months, April's profit is overstated by tens of thousands of dollars, and your true first-half net income is lower than reported.
- **May:** Revenue rebounded to $150,269, but net margin fell to 1.7% as commission costs returned.
- **June:** Online lead programs increased from $9,800 to $12,300.
- Expenses were steady at roughly $44,000 to $47,000 a month, with payroll the largest line.

### Production
- Sisu shows 56 closings and $801,664 in GCI for the half.
- Cost per closing: total costs of $686,391 (COGS plus expenses) divided by 56 closings = $12,257 per closing.
- Your P&L income ($725,098) is about $76,566 lower than Sisu GCI, which could reflect timing or the April issue.

### Recommendations
1. Have your bookkeeper record April's missing commission splits so the P&L reflects true profitability.
2. Watch lead generation spend, which rose 25% in June.
3. Payroll is your biggest expense; consider whether staffing is right-sized for your volume.
4. Aim for a net margin closer to industry norms for real estate teams.
