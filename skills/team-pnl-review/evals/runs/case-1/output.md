# Team P&L Review: Gingersnap Realty Team, January to June 2026

**Bottom line:** The team kept $38,706 in net income for January to June, a 12.6% margin on company dollar, and $6,838 in June (12.9%), but April's books record commission income net of agent splits while every other month is gross, so any ratio on commission income is off until April is restated. The biggest change in June was company dollar, up 6,445.68 from May, while Online Lead Programs rose from 9,800.00 to 12,300.00. The one decision: agree with your bookkeeper and CPA on one way to record commission income, and have April restated to match.
