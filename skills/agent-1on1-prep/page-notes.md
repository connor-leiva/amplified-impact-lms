## One-Line Promise

Attach one agent's monthly numbers, their goals and your notes from last time, and walk into your coaching one-on-one with the pace math written out against their own goal, last time's commitments carried forward, and the questions to ask.

## Who It's For

The team leader, coach or ops leader who runs regular coaching one-on-ones with agents on a real estate team, in any state and at any brokerage. Sisu or not: the numbers can come from Sisu, Follow Up Boss or the team's own tracker.

## What Sisu Already Gives, and What This Adds (Connor's Filter)

Sisu already shows each agent's dashboard and accountability: the Agent Accountability Report, scoped to one agent and a date range, shows Actual To Date against a Personal Pacer and Personal Goal for each measure, with On Pace, Not On Pace or Goal Achieved; the Personal Dashboard shows each step from Conversations on with the goal under it and the conversion between steps; goals are set by year and month (Account > Performance > Goals). Sisu's own help center names the Dashboard and the Agent Accountability Report as the tools teams use to hold agents to their goals and coach in one-on-ones. This skill doesn't rebuild that. The value is the prep: the pace math written out against this agent's own goal (so the leader can show where each number comes from, and so it works when the numbers live in a spreadsheet), what the months since the last one-on-one added beside the agent's own monthly average, last time's commitments carried forward with their status exactly as given, and the questions, focus options, agenda and recap template. Sisu stays the source of record for its own pacer and conversion. ## What to Upload, and Where It Comes From

1. **The agent's numbers by month (required, or at least last time's notes).** Appointments set and met, signed, under contract, closed units, closed volume and GCI, one row per month or months across the top. Any measure that's missing is just left out.
   - **The team's tracker** (CSV or Excel). A tracker with every agent works: the skill keeps only the agent you name and never shows anyone else.
   - **Sisu's Agent Accountability Report:** set Scope to the agent and a date range (Last Month, This Year or custom), then click the download icon at the top. Sisu's article shows the icon but not the file type, so this is not confirmed. Run it for each month to build a month-by-month view, or for This Year to get year-to-date totals (the pace math works from those; what changed month by month needs months). Source above.
   - **Follow Up Boss Reporting:** the Appointment report (appointments that occurred, by user, type and outcome) and closed deals by agent, with the user set to the agent and the time frame to a month, exported to CSV ("Exporting reports to a spreadsheet," https://help.followupboss.com/hc/en-us/articles/8897311199511-Exporting-reports-to-a-spreadsheet , read through search excerpts 2026-11-10). Whether the Appointment report separates set from met is not confirmed.
   - **A PDF printout or a screenshot:** the skill copies the monthly figures into a CSV exactly as printed and lists each one to check.
2. **Their goals or business plan (helpful).** Sisu: Account > Performance > Goals (Yearly Goals and Monthly Goals). Or a business plan doc, or the goals typed into the chat. Without goals, there's no pace math, and the skill says so.
3. **Last time's one-on-one notes (helpful).** Whatever you keep: a doc, a text file, notes pasted from a meeting notes app. The date and the agent's commitments in their own words matter most.
4. **Your notes for this one (helpful).** A file, or typed into the chat.

## Things to Say

1. "Prep my one-on-one with [Agent] on [day] at [time]. Her numbers, her goals and my notes from last time are attached."
2. "Here's our team tracker. Prep my 1:1 with [Agent] tomorrow; we never set her goals in Sisu."
3. "Make the agenda 45 minutes."
4. "Just the questions and the focus options."
5. "Draft the recap from what we agreed: [what you agreed]."

## What Comes Back

One page you can sort, search and print (an HTML file to download, the same layout every time), plus four lines in the chat: the headline, the number that matters, the first thing to ask, and a reminder to check every number against your files. The page holds, in this order:

- Flags: the files read, the date the numbers run to, the meeting, how goals were read and prorated, columns to confirm, anything left out for privacy or pay.
- The bottom line at the top: a two-sentence headline and three findings.
- Numbers against goal: year to date, the prorated goal, pace and the difference, each with the arithmetic ("24 x 10 / 12 = 20.0", "20 / 20.0 = 100.0%"), what the annual goal needs per month left, the month-by-month table and the count ratios. With no goal, the year to date and the line saying why there's no pace math.
- What changed since the last one-on-one: year to date then and now, each month since against the agent's own monthly average, and what your notes report, labeled.
- Last time's commitments, word for word, with the status exactly as your notes give it, or "No status given."
- Five to eight open coaching questions.
- One to three possible focus areas from the numbers, as options for you to choose.
- A suggested agenda with times.
- A recap template to fill in after, with [placeholders] and a Copy Draft button.
- A Check Before Sending list you can tick (the ticks stay in your browser), and beside the page, the file with every sum (one_on_one_data.md).

The page opens with a plain headline and four key numbers, each with a bar toward her prorated goal and the words "At Goal" or "Under Goal". Numbers are drawn where a picture reads faster: progress bars, ratio meters with her plan's mark, small monthly charts, then-and-now rows. Commitments look like pledges with owner and due chips, questions like a numbered deck (Why Ask This shows the fact behind each), focus options like tiles you can pick, the agenda like a run of show with times in MT and CT, and the recap like an email with its blanks highlighted. Tables, the arithmetic and the rest of the questions wait behind Show The Details; Print Or Save As PDF opens everything on Letter paper. Nothing you do on the page changes your numbers or sends anything. A made-up sample: `evals/page/Agent-1on1-Prep-Sample.html` (screenshots, an expanded screenshot and a PDF beside it).

## What It Won't Do

- Invent a number, goal, commitment or status, or estimate a goal when there isn't one.
- Mark a commitment done or not done unless your notes say so.
- Label the agent ("underperforming", "struggling"), predict their year, or compare them with another agent, even when you ask or their numbers are in the same file. A team average only if you supply it.
- Carry health, family, personal, HR or pay details into the prep or the recap. They stay out, with one line saying so.
- Write a performance review, a write-up, a performance plan or a termination document, or suggest changing splits or pay.
- Send anything, or write to Sisu, Follow Up Boss or any CRM.

## Run, Draft, Own

- **Run:** reads the numbers, goals and notes; does every sum, prorated goal, pace and ratio in code with the arithmetic shown; lines up last time's commitments with their status as given.
- **Draft:** the prep (questions, focus options, agenda) and the recap template, for the leader to read first.
- **Own:** the focus and what to raise; the agent's own commitments and status; any judgment about the agent's work; anything about pay, splits, HR or their place on the team (the leader, the broker, HR).

## FAQs

**Sisu already shows pacing. Why this?** Sisu shows each agent's actuals against their pacer and goal, and it stays the source of record. This writes the arithmetic out so you can walk the agent through it, works from a spreadsheet too, puts the months since your last one-on-one beside their own average, carries last time's commitments forward, and gives you the questions and a recap.

**Why doesn't my number match Sisu's Personal Pacer?** Without monthly goals, the skill prorates the annual goal evenly by month (24 a year is 20.0 through October). Sisu may spread goals by month with seasonality. Attach the monthly goals and the skill uses them.

**What if the agent has no goals?** You get their year to date, their own monthly averages and the count ratios, and a line saying there's no pace math without a goal and how to add one. It never makes up a goal.

**Will it tell me if they're underperforming, or how they compare with my other agents?** No. It gives facts against the agent's own goal and their own months. A judgment is yours, made in the conversation. It won't name or compare other agents.

**What happens to personal things in my notes?** Health, family, personal, HR and pay details stay out of the prep and the recap, and a line in Flags says something was left out, so you decide whether and how to raise it.
