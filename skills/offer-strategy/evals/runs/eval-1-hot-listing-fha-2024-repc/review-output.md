### Escalation

You asked, and four offers are reported. In a highest-and-best round, first ask the listing agent whether the seller will consider an escalation addendum at all. If they want one number, use $505,000 (or up to $510,000). If escalation is allowed, the buyers set three numbers; a starting point to discuss: start at $502,500, increase by $1,000 over a competing offer, cap at $510,000 (at or below both the buyers' $510,000 and the letter's $515,000). The listing side learns the cap if it's reached, and every price up to $510,000 faces the same appraisal question (up to a $12,000 gap). Utah has no state-approved escalation form: the language comes from your brokerage's approved addendum, and your broker reviews it. I'm not drafting clause language.
