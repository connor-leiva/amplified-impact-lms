Here's how I'd approach the offer on 734 N Make Believe Ave for Morgan and Sky.

## Market Read

This one is hot. It's been on the market 3 days, there are already four offers, and the listing agent's notes say multiple offers were expected and the seller prefers conventional or cash. That last part matters: your FHA buyers start at a disadvantage, so the rest of the offer needs to be very strong.

The comps support the list price. 812 N Make Believe Ave sold for $498,000 in October, 702 N Make Believe Ave sold for $487,500, and the pending at 760 N Sample Birch St was listed at $494,900 and, per the agent remarks, went over list. With homes like this selling around $395 to $410 per square foot, the subject's 2,400 finished square feet suggests a value closer to $950,000 on paper, but ramblers in this area don't trade that way, so I'd anchor on the closed sales.

## Recommended Offer

- **Price:** Offer $505,000 with an escalation clause up to $510,000, in $1,000 increments over the highest competing offer.
- **Appraisal:** Since they want to compete, consider waiving the appraisal contingency or adding an appraisal gap guarantee of $10,000. Sellers in multiple-offer situations, especially ones who prefer conventional or cash, are worried about FHA appraisals coming in low, and an appraisal waiver directly addresses that concern.
- **Earnest money:** Go above the $5,000 minimum. $10,000 (about 2% of the price) will stand out.
- **Inspection (due diligence):** Shorten to 7 days. Most Utah due diligence periods run 10 to 14 days, so 7 is noticeably stronger.
- **Financing:** 21 days for financing and appraisal.
- **Closing:** December 18, with 2 days of possession after closing (a free rent-back).
- **Seller concessions:** Don't ask for any. FHA allows up to 6% in seller concessions, but asking now will hurt.
- **Personal letter:** A short letter from Morgan and Sky about why they love the home can help an FHA offer stand out against cash buyers.

Escalation clause language you can use:

> "Buyer agrees to pay $1,000 more than any bona fide competing offer, up to a maximum purchase price of $510,000. Seller shall provide a copy of the competing offer as proof."
