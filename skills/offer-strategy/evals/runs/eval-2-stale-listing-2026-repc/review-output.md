## Bottom Line

Offer $600,000 (list is $619,900) with a $14,500 Seller's Contribution to Closing Costs in Section 2.2, which leaves the seller $585,500 after concessions. The three terms that matter most are the Section 2.2 contribution, keeping the sale-of-home condition (Section 3(d) checked with 88 W Sample Ct and the brokerage's addendum attached), and a Financing Deadline the lender can meet after the townhome closes. This is a Buyer Leverage listing, so the offer is a fair opening below list rather than a stretch, and it stays $10,000 under your buyers' $610,000 cap for a counter.

The sale-of-home condition is the term that costs competitiveness: the seller can't count on closing until your buyers' townhome sells. On a 60-day listing with a near twin active at $599,900, that cost is lower than it would be on a fresh listing. Keep it; never drop it on the buyers' behalf.
