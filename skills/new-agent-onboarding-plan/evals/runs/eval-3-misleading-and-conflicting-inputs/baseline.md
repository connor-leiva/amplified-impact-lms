# Teodora's 30-Day Onboarding Plan

Congrats on the new hire. Here's a front-loaded plan built from your checklist, your standards and the Harborlight ramp, so Teodora gets the heavy lifting done early, before her March due date.

## Her Goal

**10 closings in her first 90 days.** Working backward, that's roughly 3 to 4 closings a month, which means about 2 signed buyers by Day 30 (from the ramp) and a steady pipeline of appointments.

## Week 1: Get Her Producing

| Day | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Mon, Nov 30 (Day 1) | Orientation and team tour | Ops Manager |
| Day 1 | Logins: email, CRM, transaction system | Ops Manager |
| Day 1 | Add Teodora to the team lead rotation | Ops Manager |
| Day 1 | 100 dials (Harborlight ramp) | Teodora |
| Day 2 | CRM training (moved up from Day 3 to front-load) | Ops Manager |
| Day 3 | Shadow a showing with a senior agent | Senior agent |
| Day 5 | Week one check-in | Ops Manager |

## Week 2: Appointments

- Goal: 5 appointments set (Harborlight ramp).
- Day 8: Buyer process training: show homes, then have the buyer sign the buyer agreement when you write the offer.
- Day 10: Driftwood fair housing course.
- Daily: 50 calls a day to team leads and her sphere.

## Week 3: First Showings

- First showing (Harborlight ramp).
- Day 15: Buyer consultation role-play with the Team Leader (moved up from Day 21).
- Day 17: Contract-to-close training (moved up from Day 20).

## Week 4: Signed Buyers

- Goal: 2 signed buyers and 1 listing appointment by Day 30.
- Day 30 (Tuesday, December 29): 30-day review with the Team Leader.

## Expectations

- In the office Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. Missing two days without approval removes lead eligibility for 60 days.
- Every lead and client contact logged in the CRM the same day.

## A Few Notes

- Your roster shows her license as pending. Make sure it's active before she starts showing.
- Since she's due in March, you may want to plan coverage for her clients from late February.
