# 30-Day Onboarding Plan: Linden Marchbank

Here's a complete 30-day plan for Linden as a new buyer's agent, built from your notes and filled out with common best practices for new licensees.

## Goals by Day 30

- Complete all systems training (Follow Up Boss, MLS, Dotloop)
- Pass the buyer consultation role-play
- Add 100 sphere contacts to Follow Up Boss
- Make 20 prospecting calls a day
- Set 4 buyer appointments
- Take her first team leads

## Week 1: Foundations

- **Day 1:** Welcome meeting with you. Team overview, expectations and culture.
- **Day 1:** Set up Follow Up Boss, the MLS and Dotloop.
- **Day 2:** Fair housing and NAR Code of Ethics training.
- **Day 2:** Complete brokerage paperwork, W-9 and E&O insurance.
- **Day 3:** Follow Up Boss training: stages, tags, smart lists and action plans.
- **Day 4:** Build her sphere list (start with 50 contacts).
- **Day 5:** Friday 1:1 with you.

## Week 2: Shadowing

- **Day 8:** Shadow a listing appointment.
- **Days 9 to 11:** Shadow two buyer appointments.
- **Day 10:** Script practice: buyer consultation, objection handling.
- **Day 12:** Friday 1:1 with you.
- **Daily:** 20 prospecting calls.

## Week 3: Role-Play and Prospecting

- **Day 15:** Role-play the buyer consult with you. She should pass before taking leads.
- **Day 16:** Open house with a senior agent.
- **Day 17:** Lead follow-up training: respond to every lead within 5 minutes.
- **Day 19:** Friday 1:1 with you.

## Week 4: Leads

- **Day 22:** Start taking team leads.
- **Day 24:** Hold her own open house.
- **Day 26:** Friday 1:1 with you.
- **Day 30:** 30-day review: goals, numbers and what's next.

## Day 60 and Day 90

- Day 60: first contract written
- Day 90: first closing
