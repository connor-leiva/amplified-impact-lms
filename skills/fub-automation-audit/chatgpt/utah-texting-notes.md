# Utah Notes for Automated Texts

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Utah only: read this when the team works in Utah or its leads are in Utah. Outside Utah, skip it and say "State rules were not checked." This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice; questions go to the broker or an attorney.

## Utah's Telephone and Facsimile Solicitation Act

- **Sundays, holidays and evenings.** "A person may not make a telephone solicitation to a residential telephone or cellular telephone without prior express consent" between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. local time, on a Sunday, or on a legal holiday (Utah Code 13-25a-103(3), effective May 4, 2022).
- **What counts as a telephone solicitation.** "The initiation of a telephone call or message for a commercial purpose," including one "encouraging the purchase or rental of, or investment in, property" or "encouraging the person called to sell real or personal property" (13-25a-102(8)). The definition says "call or message" and doesn't name text messages. Whether it reaches texts was not confirmed for this file. Ask your broker.
- **Automated dialing.** Utah defines an "automated telephone dialing system" as equipment that stores or produces numbers, calls them, and connects the call "with a recorded message or artificial voice" (13-25a-102(2)). Using one for a telephone solicitation needs the person's prior express consent to hear from that solicitor, or an established business relationship (13-25a-103(1) and (2)).
- **Established business relationship.** Based on an inquiry, application, purchase or transaction, formed by a voluntary two-way communication, and ended by either side or by 18 months passing since the last inquiry, transaction or two-way communication (13-25a-102(4)).
- **Federal law too.** The same section bars a telephone solicitation that violates the federal TCPA, 47 U.S.C. 227 (13-25a-103(4)). The federal rules are in `outreach-rules.md`.
- **Licensee exemption.** Sections 13-25a-103 and 13-25a-108 "do not apply to a person who holds and acts within the scope of a license" issued by the Utah Division of Real Estate (Utah Code 13-25a-111, effective May 1, 2024). So for a licensed agent acting as one, Utah's own evening, Sunday and holiday limits may not apply. The skill keeps the Sunday and holiday care in this file anyway, as one of the house's conservative defaults (`outreach-rules.md`, Conservative Defaults). The federal TCPA still applies (`outreach-rules.md`), and whether a message is within the license is the broker's call.

## Utah's Legal Holidays

Under Utah Code 63G-1-301 (the version effective May 6, 2026, superseded January 1, 2027): New Year's Day, Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Good Friday, Easter Sunday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Pioneer Day (July 24), Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day (November 11), Thanksgiving Day (the fourth Thursday of November), Christmas, any day of fasting or thanksgiving proclaimed by the president or the governor, and every Sunday. When a fixed-date holiday falls on a Saturday, the Friday before is the holiday; on a Sunday, the Monday after. Check the list again after January 1, 2027.

## What the Audit Does in Utah Mode

- For each automated text, say whether anything in the inputs keeps it from sending on a Sunday or a Utah legal holiday. Follow Up Boss's help center describes only a 9 p.m. to 8 a.m. hold on initial texts, in the assigned agent's time zone (Part 1 of `fub-automation-audit-guide.md`); an outside tool's days are whatever its settings show, or "not shown."
- When a text can send on a Sunday or a holiday to a lead whose consent isn't documented, add one line to its consent result and to Check Before Sending: "Utah's solicitation law limits Sunday and legal holiday solicitations to people who gave prior express consent (Utah Code 13-25a-103(3)), though Utah Code 13-25a-111 exempts a licensee acting within the license, and whether it reaches texts was not confirmed. Ask your broker."
- Never say a text broke Utah law. Say what to check and who answers it.
