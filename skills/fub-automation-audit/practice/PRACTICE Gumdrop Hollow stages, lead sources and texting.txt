Day 0, 1 hour after the tag: "Hi {first name}, thanks for coming to the open house today! It's {agent name} with Gumdrop Hollow Home Team. Any questions about the home?"

Day 2: "Hi {first name}, did the home catch your eye? I can send you similar ones."

Day 6: "Last check-in from me, {first name}! Want new listings like that one? {agent name}, Gumdrop Hollow Home Team, Example Ridge Realty. Reply STOP to opt out."
