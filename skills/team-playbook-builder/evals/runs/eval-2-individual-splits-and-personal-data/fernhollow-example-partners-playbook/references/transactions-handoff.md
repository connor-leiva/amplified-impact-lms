# Handing a Deal to the Transaction Coordinator

Owner: Transaction Coordinator (Rowan Notreal)
Last updated: April 22, 2026

## After Acceptance

- Send the fully signed contract to the Transaction Coordinator within 1 business day of acceptance. (Source: Operations Manual, section 4; dated April 22, 2026)
- The Transaction Coordinator owns the deadline calendar and sends the client the timeline. (Source: Operations Manual, section 4; dated April 22, 2026)

## Earnest Money

- Earnest money goes to the title company. (Source: Operations Manual, section 5; dated April 22, 2026)
- If a buyer can't wire, they can bring a cashier's check to the office for deposit into the trust account. [Removed: account details. Ask the Managing Broker.] (Source: Operations Manual, section 5; dated April 22, 2026)
