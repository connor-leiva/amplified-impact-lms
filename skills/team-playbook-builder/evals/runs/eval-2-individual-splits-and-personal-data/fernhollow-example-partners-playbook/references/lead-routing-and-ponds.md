Owner: Team Owner (Finley Fernhollow-Example)
Last updated: April 22, 2026 (oldest source in this file: February 2, 2026)
Sources: Team Operations Manual 2026 (April 22, 2026), section 2; Onboarding Checklist (February 2, 2026)

## New Leads

- Text every new portal lead with the Hello 1 template within 5 minutes. (Source: Operations Manual, section 2; dated April 22, 2026)
- Call every new lead within 15 minutes during business hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM). (Source: Operations Manual, section 2; dated April 22, 2026)
- Log every touch in Follow Up Boss. (Source: Operations Manual, section 2; dated April 22, 2026)

## Joining the Lead Rotation

- Join the lead rotation after your 30-day review with the Team Owner. (Source: Onboarding Checklist, "First Month"; dated February 2, 2026)
