## New Leads

- Text every new portal lead with the Hello 1 template within 5 minutes. (Source: Team Operations Manual, Section 2, Leads; dated April 22, 2026)
- Call every new lead within 15 minutes during business hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM). (Source: Team Operations Manual, Section 2, Leads; dated April 22, 2026)
- Log every touch in Follow Up Boss. (Source: Team Operations Manual, Section 2, Leads; dated April 22, 2026)
