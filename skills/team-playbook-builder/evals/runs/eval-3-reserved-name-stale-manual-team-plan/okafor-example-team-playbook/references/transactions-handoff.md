Owner: Transaction coordinator
Last updated: September 30, 2026 (oldest source in this file: May 2024)

## After Acceptance

- Every deal goes to the transaction coordinator within 24 hours of acceptance. (Source: Training Call, 00:01:41; dated September 30, 2026)
- Email the fully signed contract to the transaction coordinator within 24 hours. (Source: Policy Manual, section 4; dated May 2024. The September 30, 2026 call confirms the 24-hour rule.)
- The transaction coordinator owns the deadline calendar. (Source: Policy Manual, section 4; dated May 2024. This section is more than 12 months old; confirm it's still current with the Team Leader.)
