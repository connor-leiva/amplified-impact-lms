## Listing Photos

- Listing photos are ordered by the Listing Manager within 2 business days of the signed listing agreement. (Source: Policy Manual, section 3; dated May 2024)
- The team pays for photos up to $250. (Source: Policy Manual, section 3; dated May 2024)

## Social Media

- Every post about a listing names Fable Ridge Realty. (Source: Policy Manual, section 6; dated May 2024)
- No posting a listing before it is active. (Source: Policy Manual, section 6; dated May 2024)
