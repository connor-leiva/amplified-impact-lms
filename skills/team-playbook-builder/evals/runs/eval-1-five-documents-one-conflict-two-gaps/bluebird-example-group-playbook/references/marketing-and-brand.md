# Marketing, Brand and Closing Gifts

Owner: Marketing Coordinator (Robin Pretendson)
Last updated: September 8, 2026 (oldest source in this file: August 20, 2026)
Sources: Marketing and Brand Rules (August 20, 2026); Bluebird Agent Handbook (September 8, 2026), "Closing Gifts"

## Brand

- Every ad and social profile shows Storybook Realty Co. with the team name. (Source: Marketing and Brand Rules, "Brand"; dated August 20, 2026)
- Use the Bluebird templates in Canva. Don't edit the logo. (Source: Marketing and Brand Rules, "Brand"; dated August 20, 2026)
- Send listing marketing to the Marketing Coordinator for approval. Allow 2 business days. (Source: Marketing and Brand Rules, "Brand"; dated August 20, 2026)

## Social Media

- You may repost anything from the team's accounts. (Source: Marketing and Brand Rules, "Social Media"; dated August 20, 2026)
- Don't post a client's photo or name without their written OK. (Source: Marketing and Brand Rules, "Social Media"; dated August 20, 2026)

## CONFLICT: Who Pays for Closing Gifts

- Bluebird Agent Handbook, "Closing Gifts" (September 8, 2026): "The team pays for closing gifts up to $150 per side. Order them through the Marketing Coordinator at least one week before closing."
- Marketing and Brand Rules, "Closing Gifts" (August 20, 2026): "Closing gifts are the agent's own expense. The team does not reimburse closing gifts."
- The team hasn't decided which applies. Give agents both versions and send them to the Team Lead.
