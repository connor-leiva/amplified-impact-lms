# Buyers, Showings and Offers

Owner: Team Lead (Jamie Notarealname)
Last updated: May 14, 2026
Sources: Buyer Process SOP (May 14, 2026)

## Before the First Tour

- Hold a buyer consultation, in person or by video, before the first tour. (Source: Buyer Process SOP, 1; dated May 14, 2026)
- Have the buyer sign the brokerage's written buyer agreement before the first tour, including live virtual tours. Talking with a buyer at an open house doesn't need one. (Source: Buyer Process SOP, 1; dated May 14, 2026)
- Get a preapproval letter or proof of funds before tours. The Team Lead approves any exception. (Source: Buyer Process SOP, 1; dated May 14, 2026)

## Showings

- Book showings through ShowingTime. (Source: Buyer Process SOP, 2; dated May 14, 2026)
- Log showing feedback in Follow Up Boss within 24 hours. (Source: Buyer Process SOP, 2; dated May 14, 2026)

## Writing Offers

- Use the team offer checklist in Google Drive (Bluebird > Buyers > Offer Checklist). (Source: Buyer Process SOP, 3; dated May 14, 2026)
- The Designated Broker reviews any non-standard terms before the offer goes out. (Source: Buyer Process SOP, 3; dated May 14, 2026)
