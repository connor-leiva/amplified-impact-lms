## Steps

- Upload the signed listing agreement to Dotloop within 24 hours of signing (agent). (Source: Listing Launch Checklist, Steps; dated July 1, 2026)
- The Marketing Coordinator schedules professional photos within 3 business days of the signed agreement. (Source: Listing Launch Checklist, Steps; dated July 1, 2026)
- The team pays for photography up to $275 per listing, billed to the team. (Source: Listing Launch Checklist, Steps; dated July 1, 2026)
- Coming soon marketing only as the MLS allows; ask the Designated Broker before any coming soon post. (Source: Listing Launch Checklist, Steps; dated July 1, 2026)
- Go live in the MLS on a Thursday (agent). (Source: Listing Launch Checklist, Steps; dated July 1, 2026)
- Hold the first open house the first weekend after going live (agent). (Source: Listing Launch Checklist, Steps; dated July 1, 2026)
- Listing marketing goes to the Marketing Coordinator for approval; see `marketing-and-brand.md`.
