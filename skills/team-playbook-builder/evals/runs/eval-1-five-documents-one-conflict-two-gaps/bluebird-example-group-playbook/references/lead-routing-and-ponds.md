## First Contact and Logging

- New leads get a first call within 10 minutes during business hours (8:00 AM to 7:00 PM). (Source: Agent Handbook, "Follow Up Boss"; dated September 8, 2026)
- Log every conversation in Follow Up Boss the same day. (Source: Agent Handbook, "Follow Up Boss"; dated September 8, 2026)
- Every new lead gets the Bluebird New Lead action plan. Don't turn it off without asking the Team Lead. (Source: Tech Stack Training Transcript, 2:10; dated October 6, 2026)
