# Meetings and Expectations

Owner: Team Leader (Avery Example)
Last updated: March 2, 2026 (oldest source in this file: January 12, 2026)
Sources: 01 Lantern Lane Policy Manual (March 2, 2026), sections 3 and 4; 02 Lead Routing and Pond SOP (January 12, 2026)

## Meetings

- Weekly team meeting: Tuesdays, 9:30 to 10:30 AM, at the office or on Zoom. Required for every agent in the Lead Rotation. (Source: Policy Manual, 4.1; dated March 2, 2026)
- New agents meet one on one with the Team Leader once a month during their first 90 days. (Source: Policy Manual, 4.3; dated March 2, 2026)

## Follow Up Boss Habits

- Log every call, text and email in Follow Up Boss the same day. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.3; dated March 2, 2026)
- Update Follow Up Boss stages within 24 hours of any change. (Source: Policy Manual, 4.2; dated March 2, 2026)
- Keep fewer than 20 leads in the Attempted Contact stage to stay in the Lead Rotation. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "Staying in the Lead Rotation"; dated January 12, 2026)
