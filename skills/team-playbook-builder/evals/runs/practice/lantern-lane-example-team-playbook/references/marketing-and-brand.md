## Rules

- Use the Lantern Lane templates in Canva for every listing flyer, social post and email. (Source: Policy Manual, 5.1; dated March 2, 2026)
- The Marketing Coordinator approves listing marketing before it is posted, printed or emailed. Allow 1 business day. (Source: Policy Manual, 5.2; dated March 2, 2026)
- Every ad, sign and social profile shows "Make-Believe Realty Group" with the team name. (Source: Policy Manual, 5.3; dated March 2, 2026)
- Don't post a listing on social media before it is active in the MLS. (Source: Policy Manual, 5.4; dated March 2, 2026)
