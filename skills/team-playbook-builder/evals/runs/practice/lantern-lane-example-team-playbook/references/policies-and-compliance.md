# Legal Questions, Client Funds and Fair Housing

Owner: Team Leader (Avery Example)
Last updated: March 2, 2026 (oldest source in this file: February 10, 2026)
Sources: 01 Lantern Lane Policy Manual (March 2, 2026), sections 1, 6 and 7; 04 Training Call (February 10, 2026)

## Who Decides

- If anything in the team's documents conflicts with Make-Believe Realty Group's own policies, the brokerage policy controls and the Managing Broker decides. (Source: Policy Manual, section 1; dated March 2, 2026)
- Questions about contract language, addenda, disclosures or anything legal go to the Managing Broker. Don't give clients legal advice. (Source: Policy Manual, 6.1; dated March 2, 2026)

## Client Funds

- The team never handles client funds. Earnest money goes from the buyer directly to the title company. Wire fraud steps are in `transactions-handoff.md`. (Source: Policy Manual, 6.2; dated March 2, 2026)

## Fair Housing

- Every agent follows fair housing law. Describe homes, not people. Don't answer questions about schools or neighborhood demographics; send clients to official sources. (Source: Policy Manual, 7.1; dated March 2, 2026)

## File Compliance

- After closing, the Transaction Coordinator sends the file to the brokerage for compliance review. (Source: Training Call, 00:03:11; dated February 10, 2026)
