# Lead Routing, Stages and the Team Pond

Owner: ISA Manager (Casey Testwell)
Last updated: March 2, 2026 (oldest source in this file: January 12, 2026)
Sources: 02 Lead Routing and Pond SOP (January 12, 2026); 01 Lantern Lane Policy Manual (March 2, 2026), section 3

## Lead Sources

- Team website leads (our IDX site); portal leads the team pays for; sign calls and open house sign-ins at team listings; sphere and self-generated leads, which are yours (tag them Sphere). (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "Lead Sources"; dated January 12, 2026)

## How a Lead Reaches You

- All team leads come in through Follow Up Boss. The ISA Manager qualifies new leads and assigns them to agents in the Lead Rotation. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.1; dated March 2, 2026)
- A new lead arrives in Follow Up Boss and is assigned to the ISA. The ISA calls within 5 minutes during business hours, then assigns qualified leads to the next agent in the Lead Rotation (round robin) with a warm-transfer note in Follow Up Boss. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "Routing Steps" 1 to 3; dated January 12, 2026)
- Accept the lead in Follow Up Boss within 15 minutes. If you don't, it goes to the next agent. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "Routing Steps" 4; dated January 12, 2026)

## First Contact and Logging

- Call or text a newly assigned lead within 5 minutes during business hours (8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern), and within 1 hour outside business hours. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.2; dated March 2, 2026)
- Log every call, text and email in Follow Up Boss the same day. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.3; dated March 2, 2026)

## Staying in the Lead Rotation

- Attend the Tuesday team meeting, keep fewer than 20 leads in the Attempted Contact stage, and finish the Follow Up Boss training videos. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "Staying in the Lead Rotation"; dated January 12, 2026)

## Follow Up Boss Stages

- The stages are New Lead, Attempted Contact, Nurture, Active Client, Under Contract, Closed, Archive. Move a lead's stage within 24 hours of a change. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "Stages"; dated January 12, 2026. The Policy Manual, 4.2, also says within 24 hours.)

## CONFLICT: How Long Before a Lead Goes to the Pond

- 01 Lantern Lane Policy Manual, 3.4 (March 2, 2026): "a team lead with no logged contact in Follow Up Boss for 10 days returns to the Team Pond, where any agent in the Lead Rotation may claim it."
- 02 Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "The Pond" (January 12, 2026): "If an assigned team lead shows no logged call, text or email for 14 calendar days, a Follow Up Boss automation moves it to the Lantern Lane Pond."
- The team hasn't decided which applies. Give agents both versions and send them to the Team Leader.

## Claiming Pond Leads

- Agents in the Lead Rotation can claim up to 5 pond leads per week. A claimed pond lead is a team-provided lead for compensation. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "The Pond"; dated January 12, 2026)

## Sphere Leads

- Your own sphere and self-generated leads are never moved to the Team Pond. Tag your own contacts Sphere. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.5, dated March 2, 2026; Lead Routing and Pond SOP, "Sphere Leads", dated January 12, 2026)
