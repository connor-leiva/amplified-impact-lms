## After Mutual Acceptance

- Within 24 hours of mutual acceptance, upload the fully signed contract and every addendum to SkySlope, and fill out the Deal Intake form. The link is pinned in the #transactions channel. (Source: Training Call, 00:00:16; dated February 10, 2026)
- The Transaction Coordinator then sends the intro email to your client, the lender, title and the other agent within one business day, and builds the deadline calendar. (Source: Training Call, 00:00:39; dated February 10, 2026)

## Who Owns What, Contract to Close

- The Transaction Coordinator owns the deadline calendar, title and lender coordination, and file compliance. (Source: Policy Manual, section 2; dated March 2, 2026)
- Don't send your own deadline summary to the client; point them to the Transaction Coordinator's. (Source: Training Call, 00:00:39; dated February 10, 2026)
- You schedule inspections and arrange access. Repair negotiations are yours too. Once you and your client decide what to ask for, the Transaction Coordinator drafts the paperwork on the brokerage's form. (Source: Training Call, 00:01:09; dated February 10, 2026)

## Changing a Date

- Any date change needs a signed addendum. Don't just agree by email with the other agent. Send the details to the Transaction Coordinator, who prepares it. Anything about contract wording goes to the Managing Broker. (Source: Training Call, 00:02:02; dated February 10, 2026)

## Earnest Money and Wires

- The team never handles client funds. Earnest money goes from the buyer directly to the title company. (Source: Policy Manual, 6.2, dated March 2, 2026; Training Call, 00:01:30, dated February 10, 2026)
- Every time, remind your buyer to call title at a number they look up themselves before they wire anything. (Source: Training Call, 00:01:30; dated February 10, 2026)
- At the first meeting, tell every client the team will never email wire instructions, and that they should confirm any wire instructions by calling the title company at a number they find on their own. (Source: Policy Manual, 6.3; dated March 2, 2026)

## Closing Gifts

- The team covers up to $100 per transaction. Order through the Marketing Coordinator at least 5 days before closing. (Source: Training Call, 00:02:21; dated February 10, 2026)

## After Closing

- The Transaction Coordinator sends the file to the brokerage for compliance review. You don't need to do anything unless the Transaction Coordinator asks. (Source: Training Call, 00:03:11; dated February 10, 2026)
