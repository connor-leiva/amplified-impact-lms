# Leads, the Lead Rotation and the Pond

Owner: ISA Manager (Casey Testwell)
Last updated: March 2, 2026 (oldest source in this file: January 12, 2026)
Sources: Lantern Lane Policy Manual (March 2, 2026); Lead Routing and Pond SOP (January 12, 2026)

## Lead Sources

- All team leads come in through Follow Up Boss. The ISA Manager qualifies new leads and assigns them to agents in the Lead Rotation. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.1; dated March 2, 2026)
- Lead sources: team website leads (our IDX site), portal leads the team pays for, sign calls and open house sign-ins at team listings, and sphere and self-generated leads (yours; tag them Sphere). (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Lead Sources; dated January 12, 2026)

## Routing and First Contact

- A new lead arrives in Follow Up Boss and is assigned to the ISA. The ISA calls within 5 minutes during business hours. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Routing Steps 1 and 2; dated January 12, 2026)
- The ISA assigns qualified leads to the next agent in the Lead Rotation (round robin) and leaves a warm-transfer note in Follow Up Boss. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Routing Step 3; dated January 12, 2026)
- Accept the lead in Follow Up Boss within 15 minutes. If you don't, it goes to the next agent. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Routing Step 4; dated January 12, 2026)
- First contact: call or text a newly assigned lead within 5 minutes during business hours (8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern), and within 1 hour outside business hours. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.2; dated March 2, 2026)
- Log every call, text and email in Follow Up Boss the same day. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.3; dated March 2, 2026)

## Staying in the Lead Rotation

- Attend the Tuesday team meeting. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Staying in the Lead Rotation; dated January 12, 2026)
- Keep fewer than 20 leads in the Attempted Contact stage. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Staying in the Lead Rotation; dated January 12, 2026)
- Finish the Follow Up Boss training videos. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Staying in the Lead Rotation; dated January 12, 2026)

## Stages

- We use these Follow Up Boss stages: New Lead, Attempted Contact, Nurture, Active Client, Under Contract, Closed, Archive. Move a lead's stage within 24 hours of a change. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Stages; dated January 12, 2026)

## CONFLICT: How Long Before a Lead Goes to the Pond

- Policy Manual, 3.4 (March 2, 2026): "Pond rule: a team lead with no logged contact in Follow Up Boss for 10 days returns to the Team Pond, where any agent in the Lead Rotation may claim it."
- Lead Routing and Pond SOP, The Pond (January 12, 2026): "If an assigned team lead shows no logged call, text or email for 14 calendar days, a Follow Up Boss automation moves it to the Lantern Lane Pond."
- The team hasn't decided which applies. Give agents both versions and send them to the Team Leader.

## Claiming Pond Leads

- Agents in the Lead Rotation can claim up to 5 pond leads per week. A claimed pond lead is a team-provided lead for compensation. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, The Pond; dated January 12, 2026)

## Sphere Leads

- Your own sphere and self-generated leads are never moved to the Team Pond. (Source: Policy Manual, 3.5; dated March 2, 2026)
- Tag your own contacts Sphere. Sphere leads are never moved to the pond. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, Sphere Leads; dated January 12, 2026)
