# Splits, Fees and the Team Cap

Owner: Team Leader (Avery Example)
Last updated: January 1, 2026 (oldest source in this file: January 1, 2026)
Sources: Compensation and Fee Schedule 2026 (effective January 1, 2026); Lead Routing and Pond SOP (January 12, 2026)

The Team Leader confirmed on November 8, 2026 that this schedule applies to every agent on the team.

## Splits

- Splits apply to the agent's commission after Make-Believe Realty Group's brokerage split. (Source: Compensation and Fee Schedule 2026, Section 1; effective January 1, 2026)
- Team-provided leads (Lead Rotation, the Team Pond, sign calls on team listings): 50% agent, 50% team. (Source: Compensation and Fee Schedule 2026, Section 1; effective January 1, 2026)
- Self-generated and sphere leads: 70% agent, 30% team. (Source: Compensation and Fee Schedule 2026, Section 1; effective January 1, 2026)
- A claimed pond lead is a team-provided lead for compensation. (Source: Lead Routing and Pond SOP, The Pond; dated January 12, 2026)

## Fees

- Monthly team fee: $85, charged on the 1st of each month. It covers your Follow Up Boss seat, Canva Pro and the team website. (Source: Compensation and Fee Schedule 2026, Section 2; effective January 1, 2026)
- Transaction coordination fee: $350 per closed transaction, paid from the agent's side at closing. (Source: Compensation and Fee Schedule 2026, Section 2; effective January 1, 2026)

## Team Cap

- Once the team's share of your commissions reaches $18,000 in a calendar year, your split on all leads becomes 85% agent and 15% team for the rest of that year. (Source: Compensation and Fee Schedule 2026, Section 3; effective January 1, 2026)
