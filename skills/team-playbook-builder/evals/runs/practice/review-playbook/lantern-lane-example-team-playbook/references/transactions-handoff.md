## After Mutual Acceptance

- Within 24 hours of mutual acceptance, upload the fully signed contract and every addendum to SkySlope, and fill out the Deal Intake form. The link is pinned in the #transactions channel. (Source: Training Call, 00:00:16; dated February 10, 2026)
- The Transaction Coordinator sends the intro email to your client, the lender, title and the other agent within one business day, and builds the deadline calendar. (Source: Training Call, 00:00:39; dated February 10, 2026)
- The Transaction Coordinator owns the calendar. Don't send your own deadline summary to the client; point them to the Transaction Coordinator's. (Source: Training Call, 00:00:39; dated February 10, 2026)

## What Stays With You

- Scheduling inspections and getting access are on the agent. Repair negotiations are on you too. Once you and your client decide what to ask for, the Transaction Coordinator drafts the paperwork on the brokerage's form. (Source: Training Call, 00:01:09; dated February 10, 2026)
- Any date change needs a signed addendum. Send the Transaction Coordinator the details to prepare it. Contract wording goes to the Managing Broker. (Source: Training Call, 00:02:02; dated February 10, 2026)

## Earnest Money

- We never handle client funds. Earnest money goes from the buyer directly to the title company. (Source: Policy Manual, 6.2; dated March 2, 2026)
- Every time, remind your buyer to call title at a number they look up themselves before they wire anything. (Source: Training Call, 00:01:30; dated February 10, 2026)

## Closing Gifts

- The team covers closing gifts up to $100 per transaction. Order through the Marketing Coordinator at least 5 days before closing. (Source: Training Call, 00:02:21; dated February 10, 2026)

## After Closing

- The Transaction Coordinator sends the file to the brokerage for compliance review. You don't need to do anything unless the Transaction Coordinator asks you for something. (Source: Training Call, 00:03:11; dated February 10, 2026)
