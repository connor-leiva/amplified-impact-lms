# Contracts, Client Funds and Fair Housing

Owner: Team Leader (Avery Example)
Last updated: March 2, 2026 (oldest source in this file: March 2, 2026)
Sources: Lantern Lane Policy Manual (March 2, 2026)

## Brokerage Policy

- If anything in the team's manual conflicts with Make-Believe Realty Group's own policies, the brokerage policy controls and the Managing Broker decides. (Source: Policy Manual, Section 1; dated March 2, 2026)

## Contracts and Legal Questions

- Questions about contract language, addenda, disclosures or anything legal go to the Managing Broker. Don't give clients legal advice. (Source: Policy Manual, 6.1; dated March 2, 2026)

## Client Funds and Wire Fraud

- We never handle client funds. Earnest money goes from the buyer directly to the title company. (Source: Policy Manual, 6.2; dated March 2, 2026)
- At the first meeting, tell every client that we will never email wire instructions, and that they should confirm any wire instructions by calling the title company at a number they find on their own. (Source: Policy Manual, 6.3; dated March 2, 2026)

## Fair Housing

- Every agent follows fair housing law. Describe homes, not people. Don't answer questions about schools or neighborhood demographics; send clients to official sources. (Source: Policy Manual, 7.1; dated March 2, 2026)
