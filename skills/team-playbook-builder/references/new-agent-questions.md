# Questions New Agents Ask

Check the team's documents against this list to find gaps. It's a working list of what a new agent on a real estate team needs answered, not a statement of what any team must have. A question counts as answered only when a document answers it, not when a document mentions the topic. "Likely owner" is a suggestion for the gaps list: use the role names the team's documents use.

Items marked **[First month]** are the ones a new agent hits in their first 30 days. Put unanswered first-month items at the top of the gaps list, in this order.

## Pay and Fees
- **[First month]** When and how do I get paid after a closing, and who sends it? (Team leader or ops)
- How are splits figured for team leads versus my own leads? (Team leader)
- What fees do I pay the team each month or per deal, and what do they cover? (Team leader)
- Is there a team cap, and what happens after I reach it? (Team leader)
- How are referral fees and lead source fees handled? (Team leader)

## Leads
- **[First month]** How are leads assigned to me, and what do I have to do to stay in the rotation? (ISA or lead manager)
- **[First month]** How fast do I have to make first contact, and what counts as contact? (ISA or lead manager)
- **[First month]** When does a lead get taken back or moved to a shared pond, and can I claim pond leads? (ISA or ops)
- Which CRM stages and tags do we use, and when do I change them? (Ops)
- Are my own sphere leads treated differently from team leads? (Team leader)

## Buyers and Showings
- **[First month]** What do I do before the first showing (consultation, written buyer agreement, preapproval)? (Team leader or broker)
- Which buyer agreement form do we use, and who reviews it? (Broker)
- How do I schedule showings and give feedback? (Ops)
- How do open houses work, and what's the safety policy? (Team leader)

## Listings
- What's the process from listing appointment to live listing? (Listing manager or ops)
- Who orders photos, signs and marketing, and who pays? (Marketing or ops)
- Who approves the list price conversation materials and listing marketing? (Team leader or marketing)

## Contract to Close
- **[First month]** What do I hand to the transactions team after acceptance, and by when? (Transactions)
- What does the transactions team own, and what stays with me? (Transactions)
- Who answers contract or addendum questions? (Broker)
- How are earnest money and wire instructions handled, and what do I tell clients about wire fraud? (Transactions or broker)
- Who orders closing gifts, and who pays? (Ops or marketing)

## Marketing and Brand
- What has to be approved before I post or print? (Marketing)
- How must the team name and brokerage name appear in ads and on social? (Marketing or broker)
- Can I run my own social accounts and ads, and what are the rules? (Marketing)

## Tech and Systems
- **[First month]** How do I get logins for the CRM, MLS, transaction system and lockbox access? (Ops)
- Where do templates, scripts and forms live? (Ops)
- Who do I ask when something in the tech stack breaks? (Ops)

## Meetings and Expectations
- **[First month]** Which meetings are required, and when are they? (Team leader)
- What activity or CRM logging is expected each week? (Team leader)
- Who is my mentor or point person, and for how long? (Team leader)

## Time Off and Coverage
- **[First month]** Who covers my clients when I'm away, and how do I arrange it? (Team leader)
- What hours am I expected to be reachable for leads? (Team leader or ISA)

## Leaving the Team
- What happens to my leads, pipeline and pending deals if I leave? (Team leader)
- What do I owe or keep when I leave? (Team leader)

## Compliance
- Who answers legal, fair housing or license law questions? (Broker)
- What's the rule for texting or calling leads, and how is consent recorded? (Broker or ops)
- Where do I report a complaint or a mistake on a deal? (Broker or team leader)

## How to Write the Gaps List

- One line per unanswered question, in the agent's words, with the likely owner.
- Mark a question "partly answered" when a document touches it but leaves out the part agents need (a fee schedule with no word on when pay arrives, for example), and say which part is missing.
- Don't list a question as a gap when the answer is in a document that wasn't uploaded but is named; list that document under "Documents referenced but not uploaded" instead.
- Never suggest what the policy should be. The leader writes it.
