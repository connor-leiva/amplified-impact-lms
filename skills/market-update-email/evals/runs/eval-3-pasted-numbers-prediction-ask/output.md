## Flags

- **Left out on purpose: "a great time to buy before rates drop."** It predicts rates and tells people when to buy, and nothing in these numbers can support that. Rate questions belong with a lender. A sentence the numbers do support, now in both drafts: "More homes sold than a year earlier, at a higher median price."
- **Left out on purpose: the schools line.** Fair housing rules keep school quality out of market advertising. If a client asks about schools, point them to the school district's own information, not an opinion.
- **Placeholders:** your message names no MLS or board, and "this month" doesn't say which month (monthly figures cover a finished month). The drafts use [MLS or Board Name], [Month Year], [Start Date] and [End Date]. Fill them from the board's report.
- **Read as:** "712k" as $712,000 and "689k" as $689,000.
- **Not supplied, so not used:** active listings (inventory), months of supply, a list-to-sale ratio, and last year's days on market. Average days on market (29) appears with no comparison.
- **Utah mode:** the brokerage name goes in both drafts (Utah's advertising rule). If the board's figures come from UtahRealEstate.com, its required source line is "Based on information from UtahRealEstate.com for the period [date] through [date]."
