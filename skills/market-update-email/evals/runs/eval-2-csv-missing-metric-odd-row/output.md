## Flags

- **Odd row: October 2025.** It shows 1,262 closed sales, against 96 to 141 in each of the other 12 months, and a $4,710,000 median sale price, against $452,000 to $486,500. That median is also more than twice the row's own $498,200 average sale price, which can't happen. Both figures are about ten times the other months, which usually means an extra digit in the export. I didn't use any figure from this row, and nothing is estimated in its place. Ask Fakeland Regional MLS for the corrected October 2025 row.
- **No comparison with last October yet.** You asked for one, but it needs that row. The drafts compare October 2026 with September 2026 and say so. Once the MLS confirms October 2025, send it over and the year-over-year lines can go in.
- **No inventory in this export.** It has no active listings column, so there's no inventory or months-of-supply figure anywhere here. Get "active listings at month end" for October 2026 from the same MLS's stats tool or its monthly report. With it, months of supply would be that number ÷ October's 126 closed sales. The email holds a flagged [placeholder] line for it; delete the line if you don't add the number.
- **List-to-sale ratio is computed.** The export has no ratio, so it's the average sale price ÷ the average list price of the homes that sold (N14, N15). It can differ a little from a ratio the MLS publishes.
- **Utah mode:** the brokerage name goes in both drafts (Utah's advertising rule). Fakeland Regional MLS's own source-line rule was not checked; the drafts use the standard "Based on information from..." form.
