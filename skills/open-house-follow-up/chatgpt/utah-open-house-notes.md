# Utah Notes for Open House Follow-Ups

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Utah only: read this when the property is in Utah. Outside Utah, skip it and say "State rules were not checked." Not legal advice; questions go to the broker.

## A Visitor Who Has an Agent

- Utah's real estate licensing rules say an individual licensee may not "enter or attempt to enter into a concurrent agency representation when the licensee knows or should know that the principal has an existing agency representation agreement with another licensee" (Utah Admin. Code R162-2f-401b(1)(k)).
- An open house visitor who says they have an agent is a reason to know. So a represented visitor's follow-up is a thank-you only, with no offer to represent them, and questions about the home go through their agent.

## Texts and Calls on Sundays, Holidays and Evenings

- Utah's Telephone and Facsimile Solicitation Act says that, without the person's prior express consent, a telephone solicitation may not be made to a residential or cell phone between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. local time, on a Sunday, or on a legal holiday (Utah Code 13-25a-103(3), effective May 4, 2022).
- The section speaks of telephone calls and automated dialing. Whether it reaches text messages was not confirmed for this file. Ask your broker.
- What the skill does: it drafts texts only for visitors who consented, and tells the agent to send between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. When the open house was on a Sunday or a legal holiday, Check before sending carries one line: Utah's solicitation law limits Sunday and holiday solicitation calls to people who consented; these texts go only to visitors who consented; a call to anyone else waits for a weekday.
- The same section also requires following the federal TCPA (13-25a-103(4)). The federal rules are in `outreach-rules.md`.

## Fair Housing

Utah's protected classes add source of income, sexual orientation and gender identity to the federal list (Utah Code 57-21-5). See Part 2 of `open-house-follow-up-guide.md`.
