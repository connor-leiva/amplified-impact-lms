# Utah Notes for Open House Follow-Ups

Last verified: 2026-11-10. Utah only: read this when the property is in Utah. Outside Utah, skip it and say "State rules were not checked." Not legal advice; questions go to the broker.

## A Visitor Who Has an Agent

- Utah's real estate licensing rules say an individual licensee may not "enter or attempt to enter into a concurrent agency representation when the licensee knows or should know that the principal has an existing agency representation agreement with another licensee" (Utah Admin. Code R162-2f-401b(1)(k)).
- An open house visitor who says they have an agent is a reason to know. So a represented visitor's follow-up is a thank-you only, with no offer to represent them, and questions about the home go through their agent.

## Texts and Calls on Sundays, Holidays and Evenings

- Utah's Telephone and Facsimile Solicitation Act says that, without the person's prior express consent, a telephone solicitation may not be made to a residential or cell phone between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. local time, on a Sunday, or on a legal holiday (Utah Code 13-25a-103(3), effective May 4, 2022).
- The section speaks of telephone calls and automated dialing. Whether it reaches text messages was not confirmed for this file. Ask your broker.
- What the skill does: it drafts texts only for visitors who consented, and tells the agent to send between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. Texts and emails keep their day, Sundays and holidays included.
- No call or voicemail on a Sunday or a Utah legal holiday (the list below), even with consent and even though a licensee is exempt (Utah Code 13-25a-111). This is a conservative default, stricter than Utah law for a licensee. A call that would land on one moves to the next day that is neither, with a one-line why.
- When the open house was on a Sunday or a legal holiday, or a legal holiday falls in the next few days, Check before sending carries one line: no calls or voicemails on Sundays or Utah legal holidays (a conservative default: Utah Code 13-25a-103(3) limits solicitation calls on those days, and 13-25a-111 exempts a licensee); these texts go only to visitors who consented; a call moves to the next day that is neither, named with its date.
- The same section also requires following the federal TCPA (13-25a-103(4)). The federal rules are in `outreach-rules.md`.
- **Licensee exemption.** Sections 13-25a-103 and 13-25a-108 "do not apply to a person who holds and acts within the scope of a license" issued by the Utah Division of Real Estate (Utah Code 13-25a-111, effective May 1, 2024). So for a licensed agent acting as one, Utah's own evening, Sunday and holiday limits may not apply. The skill keeps its Sunday and holiday rule for calls anyway, as a conservative default. The federal TCPA still applies (`outreach-rules.md`), and whether a message is within the license is the broker's call.

## Utah Legal Holidays

Utah Code 63G-1-301 (the version effective May 6, 2026, and the one effective January 1, 2027):
- Every Sunday.
- New Year's Day (January 1), Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Day (third Monday of January), Presidents' Day (third Monday of February), Good Friday, Easter Sunday, Memorial Day (last Monday of May), Juneteenth (June 19), Independence Day (July 4), Pioneer Day (July 24), Labor Day (first Monday of September), Columbus Day (second Monday of October), Veterans Day (November 11), Thanksgiving Day (fourth Thursday of November), Christmas (December 25), and any day of fasting or thanksgiving the president or the governor proclaims.
- A fixed-date holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed the Friday before, and one on a Sunday the Monday after. Skip both the date and the day it is observed.
- The next ones: Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (Veterans Day); Thursday, November 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving); Friday, December 25, 2026; Friday, January 1, 2027; Monday, January 18, 2027; Monday, February 15, 2027.

## Fair Housing

Utah's protected classes add source of income, sexual orientation and gender identity to the federal list (Utah Code 57-21-5). See `fair-housing-and-representation.md`.
