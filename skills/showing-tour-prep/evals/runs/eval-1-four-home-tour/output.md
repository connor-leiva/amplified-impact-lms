## Home by Home (public fields only)

### 1. 220 E Sample Station Way #305, Draper

**Snapshot**
- Condo, mid-rise, 3rd floor end unit, elevator building. Built 2008. Mountain view.
- Price history: listed 10/15/2026 at $399,000; reduced 11/03/2026 to $389,000 ($399,000 minus $389,000 = $10,000).
- Parking: 1 assigned covered stall and 1 open stall. Garage: none.
- HOA: $285 monthly. Covers water, sewer, trash, snow removal, exterior insurance, clubhouse and fitness center. Reinvestment fee: 0.5% of sale price. Rental restrictions: Yes.
- Heat: electric heat pump; central air. In-unit laundry.
- Taxes: $2,104 (2025). Terms: Cash, Conventional, FHA, VA.

**Look at in person**
- What the HOA maintains versus the owner, and what "exterior insurance" leaves to the owner (HOA covers exterior insurance per the sheet; the buyer's insurance agent advises on the rest).
- The assigned covered stall and where the open stall is (no garage on the sheet).
- The heat pump and air handler: look for date labels (age not on the sheet).
- The in-unit laundry space and the end unit's shared walls.

**Red flags in the remarks**
- "Rental cap in place, and owners must occupy for 12 months before renting; see CC&Rs." For buyers who want to rent later, this limits that plan. Under Utah law a buyer doesn't inherit a seller's grandfathered rental status. Ask for the CC&Rs and the current rental count against the cap.
- Reduced $10,000 on 11/03/2026. Ask what feedback the seller has had.
- Reinvestment fee of 0.5% of sale price. Who pays it is a checkbox in the REPC; get the amount from the HOA and the title company.

**Fit**
- 3+ bedrooms: Doesn't (2 bedrooms).
- Under $550,000: Meets ($389,000).
- A garage: Doesn't (assigned covered stall, no garage).
- Rent out part or all later: Doesn't as stated (rental cap and a 12-month owner-occupancy rule in the public remarks).

**Look at in person**
- Built 1958 (before 1978): look at painted surfaces for peeling or chipping, and ask for the federal lead-based paint disclosure and pamphlet.
- Boiler and radiant heat: see the boiler and ask its age and service records (not on the sheet).
- Evaporative cooler, no central air on the sheet: ask its age and whether it runs.
- Roof age: not on the sheet; ask.
- Older home: the buyers may want a sewer scope during due diligence.
- Radon: ask whether there is a test result or a mitigation system; plan to test.
- The basement: what's finished (40%) and what isn't.

**Red flags in the remarks**
- "Trust sale." The trustee signing may never have lived there, so disclosures may be thin. Ask who signs and whether any court approval is needed (title confirms).
- "Sold strictly as-is; seller will make no repairs or provide any warranties." The seller won't fix things, but under the Utah REPC's as-is clause (Section 10.2 of the December 2024 form, 13.1 of the November 2026 form) the buyer still has due diligence to inspect and evaluate.
- Terms: Cash, Conventional only. Fine for a conventional loan.
- Two price cuts totaling $34,000; 66 days on MLS. Ask about feedback and any contract that fell through.

**Fit**
- 3+ bedrooms: Meets (3, all on the main floor).
- Under $550,000: Meets ($515,000).
- A garage: Meets (1 car attached).
- Rent out part or all later: Can't tell from the sheet. No HOA is shown; renting a basement unit depends on city rules and permits. The basement has a three-quarter bath but no bedroom.

### 3. 1350 N Sample Canal Rd, Mapleton

**Snapshot**
- Single family two-story, built 1987, 0.52 acres. Garage: 2 car attached. RV parking: yes.
- Price history: listed 10/27/2026 at $549,900; no changes.
- Above grade: Floor 2 1,010 sq ft plus Floor 1 1,050 sq ft = 2,060 sq ft. Basement: 1,020 sq ft, 50% finished, with a bedroom and a three-quarter bath.
- Water: culinary from Mapleton City. Irrigation: secondary, flood irrigation (open ditch, turn schedule). Pressurized irrigation: No. Water shares: 2, Example Ditch Irrigation Company.
- Lot: fruit trees, garden space, ditch along the rear lot line.
- HOA: none. Taxes: $3,320 (2025). Terms: Cash, Conventional, FHA, VA.

**Look at in person**
- The ditch along the rear lot line and where the irrigation turn comes onto the lot; how the yard is watered between turns.
- Ditch along the lot: point the buyers to FEMA's flood maps; don't say whether the lot is in a flood zone. Ask whether the irrigation company has an easement along the ditch (title confirms).
- Basement bedroom: look for an escape window.
- Roof, furnace and air conditioner ages: not on the sheet; look for labels.
- Radon: ask for any test result; plan to test.
- RV parking: see the access.

**Red flags in the remarks**
- "Includes 2 shares of Example Ditch Irrigation Company for flood irrigation on the company's turn schedule." Water shares are stock in the water company and move by that company's own transfer steps; the company sets fees and assessments. Ask how they convey, the transfer fee, the annual assessment, how much water a share delivers and the turn schedule.
- No pressurized irrigation. Watering depends on the ditch schedule.

**Fit**
- 3+ bedrooms: Meets (4: 3 up, 1 down).
- Under $550,000: Meets ($549,900, $100 under).
- A garage: Meets (2 car attached).
- Rent out part or all later: Can't tell from the sheet. No HOA shown; a basement unit depends on city rules and permits.
