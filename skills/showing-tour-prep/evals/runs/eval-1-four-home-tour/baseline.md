## Property Notes

**1. Draper condo, $389,000**
- 2 bed, 2 bath third-floor end unit with mountain views and an elevator. HOA covers water, sewer, trash, snow, exterior insurance, clubhouse and gym.
- Price dropped from $399,000 on 11/3.
- Concern: the HOA has a rental cap that's currently full (20 units, 4 owners on the waitlist), and owners have to live there 12 months before renting. Since your buyers want to rent it out eventually, this is likely a dealbreaker.
- Seller is offering $3,000 toward closing costs.
- Only 2 bedrooms and no garage, so it misses two of their criteria.
- Showing: vacant, go and show; combo lockbox at the unit door.

**2. Holladay rambler, $515,000**
- 3 bed, 2 bath brick rambler on 0.27 acres. Good value: it has come down $34,000 from $549,000.
- Trust sale, sold strictly as-is. The trustee has never lived there and will only give a limited disclosure.
- Red flag: a neighbor reported a sewer backup in 2025. Definitely get a sewer scope.
- Built in 1958, so expect older systems: boiler heat, swamp cooler, likely original electrical and plumbing. The buyers should budget for updates.
- Hardwood under the carpet could be a nice bonus.
- Showing: vacant, lockbox on front door.

**3. Mapleton two-story, $549,900**
- 4 bed, 4 bath on a half acre with RV parking, fruit trees and garden space. Right at the top of their budget.
- Comes with 2 shares of Example Ditch Irrigation Company for flood irrigation. Water shares are a big plus in Utah County and can add real value; the buyer pays a $150 transfer fee.
- Seller wants to close after 12/15.
- Basement is half finished with a bedroom and 3/4 bath, which could work as a rental space down the road.
- Showing: 2-hour notice through Aligned Showings, SentriLock on the front door.

**4. Riverton two-story, $534,500**
- 5 bed, 4 bath with a fully finished basement, backing to the trail. New furnace and AC in 2024.
- Price reduced $15,000 on 11/4.
- Seller is offering $5,000 toward closing costs or a rate buydown, and highest and best is due Tuesday 11/17 at noon, so be ready to move fast.
- Basement with its own bedroom, full bath and family room is a strong setup for renting part of the home later.
- Showing: SentriLock on the side garage door, code 4590. Alarm code 8812. Two cats, keep doors closed.

## My Take

Riverton is the strongest fit: it hits every criterion, has the best basement setup for their rental plans, and the seller is offering $5,000. Mapleton is a great option if they want land and water. Holladay is the value play if they're handy, and Draper is probably a pass given the rental cap.

## Questions for Listing Agents

- Draper: Can you confirm the rental cap count and waitlist? Any special assessments?
- Holladay: Any documentation on the sewer backup? Will the trustee accept a longer due diligence period?
- Mapleton: What's the annual assessment on the water shares? Is the seller flexible on closing date?
- Riverton: How many offers do you have so far?
