# Showing Tour Prep

Turn the MLS sheets for tomorrow's showings into what the buyer's agent needs in the car: a clean snapshot of each home, what to look at while inside, what to ask each listing agent, and a one-page tour sheet the buyer can hold. The agent runs the tour; this skill does the reading and sorting.

Why it matters: an agent-detail sheet mixes public facts with fields only MLS members may see (agent remarks, showing instructions, lockbox and alarm codes, occupancy), and one forwarded printout can leak them. The things that change a buyer's mind, such as a rental cap, an as-is sale or water shares that may not convey, often sit in one line of remarks.

**Runs on its own:** reading every sheet, sorting public fields from agent-only ones, the snapshot math (price change totals, monthly HOA equivalents) and the fit check. **Drafts for the agent to review:** the buyer tour sheet and the questions for each listing agent. **Stays with the agent:** tour order and scheduling (the showing service), what to tell the buyer about anything agent-only, offer decisions (offer-strategy), compensation, fair housing judgment calls, and any payment or banking details, which this skill never handles.

## 1. Gather the Sheets and Pick the Mode

- **Required:** an MLS sheet for each home, one PDF per home or one PDF with all of them. The agent version (with agent remarks and showing instructions) is best. A client or public version works; say the agent-only page will be thin. No sheet at all: ask for them and stop.
- **Optional:** the buyer's criteria from the agent's message, a pasted Follow Up Boss note or an uploaded note; the tour date; the buyer's loan type if mentioned. Use what's there. Don't ask for more.
- A home the agent names without a sheet: list it under Flags and prep the rest. Never ask the agent to type what a sheet contains. A missing tour date becomes [Tour Date], flagged at the top.
- **Utah mode** (Utah addresses): read `references/utah-tour-notes.md`, then the sections of `references/utah-property-notes.md` the sheets raise (water rights and shares, HOA rental restrictions, reinvestment fees, radon, public infrastructure districts).
- **General mode** (any other state): skip every utah-* file. Say near the top: "State rules were not checked; this follows the MLS sheets only."
- Read `references/buyer-agreements.md`. Unless the message or note says a written buyer agreement is signed, flag: "Confirm a written buyer agreement is signed before the tour (in person or live virtual)." Never state its terms or a fee.

## 2. Sort Every Field: Public or Agent-Only

Read `references/tour-guide.md` first. Before writing anything, split each sheet:
- **Agent-only:** agent, private or confidential remarks; showing instructions; lockbox, gate, alarm and door codes and lockbox location; occupancy and occupant or owner names and phone numbers; appointment and listing agent contact lines; offer instructions; any field labeled agent, private, confidential, member or broker only.
- Anything taken from an agent-only field goes only on the Agent-Only Page. Tour at a Glance, Home by Home and the buyer tour sheet use public fields only. Flags and the bottom line may point to the Agent-Only Page ("one showing needs notice today; see the Agent-Only Page") but never spell out what's there.
- Never repeat a lockbox, gate, alarm or door code anywhere, the Agent-Only Page included. Write "code is on the MLS sheet; not repeated here."
- Public remarks that describe who a home suits ("perfect for a young family," "walk to church"): don't repeat them. Describe the feature instead.

## 3. Snapshot Each Home

From the sheet only, with the line it came from:
- Status (flag anything other than Active), list price, original price and each price change with its date; the total change with the arithmetic shown
- Beds and baths split above grade and below grade; above-grade and basement square feet shown separately, with basement finish. Never give total square feet alone. Write each kind of bath as the sheet counts it; never add them into a decimal.
- Lot, year built, style, garage; HOA dues, how often, a monthly equivalent when not monthly (math shown) and what the dues cover; taxes and tax year; days on market as labeled (both counts when two are shown)
- Utah: culinary water, irrigation or secondary water, and water shares, as the sheet states them
- A field that's missing says "not on the sheet." Never fill it from memory or an estimate.

## 4. What to Look At in Person

Use the triggers in `references/tour-guide.md`. Three to six items per home, each tied to what on the sheet raised it (the year built, a remark, a feature). Name what to notice or ask for; don't diagnose, estimate a cost or guess a system's age. The inspector evaluates later.

## 5. Red Flags in the Remarks

Read the public remarks for the Home by Home section and the agent-only remarks for the Agent-Only Page. Use the red-flag table in `references/tour-guide.md` (as-is, cash only, tenant-occupied, short sale, bank-owned, estate or trust sale, recent price cuts, back on market, rental caps, special assessments, water shares, public infrastructure districts). For each: quote the words, say what they can mean for this buyer, and give the question they raise. Never guess at a seller's motives or finances.

## 6. Fit Against the Buyer's Stated Criteria

- Only criteria the agent or the note states. Rate each per home: Meets, Doesn't or Can't tell from the sheet (and what to check on the tour). No criteria given: skip fit and say so.
- Never add criteria, and never rank or drop homes by anything else, compensation included.
- Translate needs into property features: "main-level bedroom and full bath, step-free entry," never the person or condition behind them.
- Criteria about people or places' reputations (school quality, safety or crime, who lives nearby, a place of worship): not assessed. On the Agent-Only Page, say a request was left out for fair housing without repeating it, and put the official-sources line on the tour sheet.
- Commute or distance: can't tell from the sheet. Suggest the buyer check the drive. Don't compute distances.

## 7. Questions for Each Listing Agent

From the question bank in `references/tour-guide.md`, pick up to six per home that the sheet raises: offer activity and any deadline, the seller's timing, concessions, disclosures and HOA documents, rental rules and the cap count, special assessments, water rights and shares, what the remarks leave unclear. Neutral wording. Never ask about occupants as people or the seller's personal life. If the agent's buyer agreement depends on it, one question on whether the seller will contribute toward the buyer's brokerage, on the Agent-Only Page only.

## 8. Write the Tour Sheet and the Agent-Only Page

Read `references/tour-sheet-writing.md` before writing.
- **Buyer tour sheet:** one page, homes in the order the agent gave (never reorder; the agent and the showing service set the route). Public facts, what to look for, fit, notes and a rating line, and the official-sources line. No agent-only content, listing agent questions, price opinions or offer advice.
- **Agent-Only Page:** before-the-tour checks (including any showing notice that must go out before the tour, counted back from the tour time), then per home the access and showing notes (no codes), what the agent-only remarks say and mean, and the questions for the listing agent. When an agent-only field holds something that may matter to the buyer (a special assessment, a past backup, a tenant's lease), say so and suggest asking the listing agent for the document and checking with the broker on how to share it.
- Follow the user's voice profile when one is in the conversation (a Project's instructions such as the "My Voice" project from amplifiedimpact.io/voice-profile, their preferences, or a voice skill). It changes tone, not content. Without one: warm, plain, direct.

## 9. Guardrails

- Every fact, number, date and name comes from the sheets or the agent's message. No invented comps, values, school or crime data, distances, utility costs or repair costs. Unclear or cut off: say so.
- Agent-only fields never reach the tour sheet. Codes are never repeated anywhere.
- Fair housing: describe homes, never people. No comments on schools, safety, demographics or who a home suits; point to official sources (`references/tour-guide.md`). Don't repeat protected-class wording from remarks or the buyer's note.
- Stay in lane: not the inspector, appraiser, lender, title officer or attorney. No price opinions ("overpriced," "a steal") and no offer advice; that's offer-strategy. No guarantees.
- Don't rebuild the MLS or the showing service: no routing, scheduling or confirming appointments.
- Privacy: leave out seller, occupant and owner names and numbers, the buyer's loan and financial details beyond the loan type and the budget they gave, and any wire, account or routing details.
- Drafts only. Never send, post or write to a CRM. With a Gmail connector and the agent's ask, save a draft.

## 10. Output, In This Order

1. **Flags**, only when they apply: buyer agreement not confirmed, a status other than Active, a showing notice due before the tour, financing terms that leave out the buyer's loan type, a sheet printed before today, missing sheets, client-version sheets, placeholders, general mode
2. **Bottom line for the agent** (three sentences max): how many homes, the one or two things to handle before the tour, and which homes meet the most stated criteria
3. **Tour at a Glance:** one table, a row per home (status, price, beds and baths split, above-grade and basement square feet, year, HOA per month, days on market, price change)
4. **Home by Home** (public fields only): the rest of the snapshot, what to look at in person, red flags in the remarks, fit
5. **Buyer Tour Sheet (Share This Page)**
6. **Agent-Only Page: Do Not Share or Forward**
7. **Check before sending:** every price, date, size, fee and quoted remark on the tour sheet and in the listing agent questions, with the sheet and field it came from, plus open placeholders and anything a sheet left unclear
