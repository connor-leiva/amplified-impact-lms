## Fair Housing: Ten Classes in Utah

The Utah Fair Housing Act makes it a discriminatory housing practice to make, print, circulate, publish or post "any notice, statement, or advertisement" for the sale or rental of a dwelling "that directly or indirectly expresses any preference, limitation, or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, source of income, disability, sexual orientation, or gender identity" (Utah Code 57-21-5(2), effective May 6, 2026). That is the federal list (42 U.S.C. 3604(c)) plus source of income, sexual orientation and gender identity.

## Advertising: The Brokerage Name

It is grounds for discipline, "at the time of placing an advertisement," to advertise real estate or a licensee's services "in a false, misleading, or deceptive manner," or to leave out "the brokerage name with which a person who is licensed is affiliated or, if the advertisement is placed online or in a digital format, a link to a website or media platform that identifies the brokerage name within one click" (Utah Code 61-2f-401(12), the version effective October 1, 2026).

## Calls and Texts: Federal Rules Apply; Utah's Hour Limits Do Not Reach Licensees

- Utah Code 13-25a-103(3) bars a telephone solicitation without prior express consent between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. local time, on a Sunday, or on a legal holiday (effective May 4, 2022).
- But Utah Code 13-25a-111 says Sections 13-25a-103 and 13-25a-108 (the state no-call list) "do not apply to a person who holds and acts within the scope of a license or registration ... issued by the Division of Real Estate" (effective May 1, 2024). So for a licensed agent working within the license, those Utah limits don't apply.
- The federal TCPA (47 U.S.C. 227 and the FCC's rules at 47 CFR 64.1200) still applies to every agent in Utah.
- **House practice, not Utah law:** no marketing calls or texts on Sundays or Utah legal holidays, and none before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m. in the recipient's time zone. Write it in the compliance block as house practice.

## Generative AI: Utah's Disclosure Law

- "It is not a defense to the violation of any statute administered and enforced by the division under Section 13-2-102" (Utah's Division of Consumer Protection) "that generative artificial intelligence" made the statement, took the act or was used in it (Utah Code 13-77-102, effective May 6, 2026).
- A supplier that "uses generative artificial intelligence to interact with an individual in connection with a consumer transaction shall disclose" that the person is dealing with AI and not a human, "if the individual asks or otherwise prompts" with "a clear and unambiguous request" (Utah Code 13-77-103(1), effective May 7, 2025).
- For the brain: every draft is the agent's own message once sent, and if a client ever clearly asks whether they're dealing with AI, the answer is the truth. Whether 13-77-103(2) (regulated occupations and "high-risk" interactions) reaches a licensee's AI drafts was not checked. Ask the broker.
