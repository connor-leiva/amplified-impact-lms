## One-Line Promise

Answer a few questions about your business, and get your Claude setup back ready to paste: your instructions, a business file, the facts to remember, and exactly where each one goes.

## Who It's For

Any agent, team leader or ops lead who has Claude (or ChatGPT) and has never told it about their business, or isn't sure what goes in project instructions, project files and memory. It's the first skill to run: every other skill works better once Claude knows the market, the team, the CRM and the rules. Utah gets Utah's rules in the compliance section; anywhere else, the skill says state rules weren't checked.

## Connor's Filter: What It Adds

Claude's memory builds itself slowly as you chat, and Claude's help center explains each place (instructions, projects, project knowledge, memory) one article at a time. Nothing in Claude interviews you about your business and hands back the setup. This does, with a compliance block every brain carries, and it keeps client records in Follow Up Boss instead of copying them into Claude. No manual entry beyond answering the questions; anyone with a voice profile pastes its QUICK CARD.

## What to Bring, and Where It Comes From

1. **Your answers (required).** Nothing to download: the skill asks 17 short questions in five rounds, or you paste or attach answers you already wrote. Skip anything; it becomes a [placeholder].
2. **Your voice profile QUICK CARD (optional, recommended).** From the free guide at amplifiedimpact.io/voice-profile. Its last step gives you a profile between START OF PROFILE and END OF PROFILE lines; the QUICK CARD is its first section, starting "Write as me." Copy that section.
3. **Your brokerage's or team's policy document (optional).** If you have the advertising or compliance rules as a PDF or Word file, attach it, and the skill takes only the rules it states, with the page.
4. **Never bring:** passwords, logins, account, routing, wire or Social Security numbers, or any client's name or contact details. If you paste one by mistake, the skill tells you to remove it and never repeats it.

## What Comes Back

- Flags first: anything left out on purpose (a pasted login, a client's details, a team member's personal detail), any wording about people rewritten for fair housing, no QUICK CARD yet, general mode outside Utah, plan notes, and how many placeholders are left.
- A three-sentence bottom line.
- Where Everything Goes: a table with each piece, its exact place in Claude, the clicks to get there, and what your plan allows.
- Block 1, How I Like to Work: for Settings, Your Claude, Instructions, so every chat reads it. Your QUICK CARD sits at the end, word for word.
- Block 2, Project Instructions: for a new project named after you ("Juniper's AI Brain"), with a compliance section that always carries fair housing, texting consent, drafts only and stay in lane, your brokerage's rules and your own limits as you stated them, and Utah's rules in Utah.
- Block 3, About My Business: a plain text file for the project's knowledge: you, your brokerage and team by role, your market, what you're known for, your tools and CRM, where your business comes from, your rules, your goals exactly as you said them.
- Block 4, Facts to Remember: five to ten lines to paste into a new chat so Claude's memory has them in every project. Nothing about clients.
- Your Voice, Still To Fill In (every placeholder with its question), and Check Before Pasting (every fact with the answer it came from).

## What It Won't Do

- Change a setting, create the project, save a memory or log in to anything. You paste every block yourself.
- Ask for, repeat or keep a password, login, or account, routing, wire or Social Security number.
- Save anything about a client, even when asked. Client records stay in Follow Up Boss (or your CRM).
- Invent anything: no market statistics, no example goals, no sample team. What you didn't say stays a [placeholder].
- Rule on what the law or your brokerage requires. Your rules go in as you state them; questions go to your broker.
- Audit a setup you already have (that's AI Brain Tuneup), build the brain your whole team shares (Team AI Brain), or turn your SOPs into a playbook skill (Team Playbook Builder).

## Run, Draft, Own

- **Run:** asks the questions a round at a time, screens every answer, sorts each one into the right block, picks Utah or general mode, and labels where each block goes.
- **Draft:** the four blocks, for you to read, change and paste.
- **Own (you):** pasting every block, what Claude remembers, confirming your brokerage's and your state's rules with your broker, keeping passwords and client details out of Claude, your plan and settings.

## FAQs

**Why four places? Isn't one enough?** Each place reaches a different set of chats. Instructions reach every chat, so they hold how you like to work and your voice. A project's instructions and files reach only that project's chats, so they hold the longer business detail and the compliance section. Memory reaches every chat and grows on its own; the skill gives it a clean start. Claude's help center describes each one; the table in your setup says which is which.

**Why won't it remember my clients?** Client records belong in your CRM, where your team, your consent records and your history live. A client's number in Claude's memory would show up in chats it has nothing to do with, and a text still needs that client's documented consent. So the brain points to Follow Up Boss and keeps clients out.

**I pasted my password by accident. Now what?** The skill won't repeat it or use it. Copy your setup, delete that chat, check Settings, Your Claude, Manage next to Your memory and delete anything that mentions it, and change the password. Claude's help center says deleting a chat doesn't remove memories made from it.

**I don't have a voice profile yet.** The setup still works, with a plain default voice line. Make one free at amplifiedimpact.io/voice-profile, then paste its QUICK CARD at the end of block 1.

**Does it work outside Utah?** Yes. Outside Utah the compliance section carries the federal house lines and a line saying your state's rules weren't checked, with a placeholder to add them after you ask your broker.
