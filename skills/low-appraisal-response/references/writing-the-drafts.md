# Writing the Drafts

Every draft here is read by someone who can say no: a lender's underwriter, a nervous client, the other agent. Write so each can act without a phone call. Facts, page numbers and MLS numbers; no adjectives about anyone.

## The Reconsideration of Value Request (Buyer's Side)

From the buyer (the borrower) to the lender, prepared by the buyer's agent. Addressed to the loan officer, never the appraiser. If the lender has its own ROV form, put the same content on it.

```
Subject: Request For Reconsideration Of Value, [Property Address]

To: [Loan Officer Name], [Lender]
From: [Buyer Names] (borrower)
Date of request: [date sent]
Property address: [from the report]
Appraisal effective date: [from the report]
Appraiser: [name from the certification page]
Loan program: [Conventional / FHA / VA, from the documents]

We're asking for a reconsideration of value based on the facts and sales below.
[Conventional only: We understand one borrower-initiated request is permitted per appraisal.]

1. Items in the report that differ from the property records
   [Item]: the report shows [X] (page [n], [section]). [Source] shows [Y].
   [Attach or name the source: MLS #, the report's own sketch or photo page, permit.]

2. Additional closed sales for consideration (closed on or before [effective date])
   [MLS #], [address], [distance], closed [date], [price], concessions [amount],
   above grade [sq ft], basement [sq ft] / [finished sq ft], [beds/baths above + below],
   [garage], [lot], built [year]. Why it applies: [one sentence: closer, same
   subdivision, same style and size, more recent than Comparable [n]].
   (Up to five.)

3. Why this supports another look
   [Two or three sentences, facts only.]

Thank you for reviewing this. Please let us know the expected turn time; our
contract's appraisal deadline is [date and time]. [Buyer Names]
```

Rules:
- No target value, no "the value should be," no request to "come in at" a number.
- No adjectives about the appraiser or the report ("careless," "wrong," "biased"). Name the difference and the source.
- No loan number, Social Security number, income or asset details. The lender has them.
- Don't include the report's-own-rates arithmetic from the agent analysis. The appraiser does the math.
- Mention the contract deadline once, as a date, so the lender knows the clock.
- **FHA:** same content. Add one line: "We understand the FHA underwriter decides whether to request a reconsideration of value."
- **VA after the Notice of Value:** address it "To the VA Regional Loan Center, through [Lender]" (or send it to the lender for submission), and give each sale the Tidewater fields: street address, sales price, date of sale, gross living area, and the listing if it was listed.
- **VA before the report is final (a Tidewater call from the lender):** reply to the lender within the two business days with sales data only, in the same fields. No comment on value.

## The Data Package (Listing Side)

The seller can't file an ROV. The listing agent sends facts and sales to the buyer's agent, "for your buyer to share with their lender if they choose." Same sections 1 and 2 as above, a one-line cover note, nothing addressed to the appraiser, and nothing about the seller's bottom line, timing or finances.

## The Client Email

- Subject line in Title Case: "Appraisal Results And Next Steps For [Address]".
- Open with a bolded bottom line: the appraised value, the gap, and the decision needed by when.
- **What The Appraisal Says:** the value and the gap in one or two sentences.
- **What We Found:** the facts or sales that matter, plainly. If the report is well supported, say so.
- **Your Options:** numbered, each with its tradeoff. Buyer: request a reconsideration, renegotiate, cover the gap, or cancel under the appraisal condition by the deadline. Seller: support a reconsideration with data, reduce, meet in the middle, or hold. The client picks.
- **What Happens Next:** dates.
- Under 300 words. Short paragraphs. Warm, calm, specific. No promise the value will change.
- Sign with the agent's name, or [Agent Name].

Money language:
- Show the gap as arithmetic: "$640,000 contract price minus $612,000 appraised value = $28,000."
- When the buyer would cover the gap, say the lender bases the loan on the lower of price and appraised value, so the extra cash is at least the gap; the lender confirms the exact figure. Never compute a loan amount or down payment.
- For VA: if the buyer proceeds above the reasonable value, the difference comes from their own cash, not borrowed.
- Splits: show the arithmetic ("meeting halfway would be $640,000 minus $14,000 = $626,000"). The client decides; the skill never recommends a number.

## The Note to the Other Agent

Two to five sentences. Factual and friendly: what you're doing (requesting a reconsideration through the lender, or sharing sales for the buyer's lender), what you need (a complete copy of the report, the seller's position, an extension signed before the deadline), and by when. Never include your client's priorities, their maximum, their finances or how much of the gap they'd cover.

## Check Before Sending

List every date, time, dollar amount, square footage, page reference, MLS number and quoted phrase in the drafts, each with its source document and page, plus any open [placeholder].
