## Options for the Buyers

1. **Request a reconsideration of value through Example Lending Group, and ask for an extension.** Strongest grounds you'll see. Tradeoff: under the lender's conventional process, the buyers get one borrower-initiated request per appraisal, and it may not finish by Tuesday, so it only protects them alongside a signed extension of the Appraisal Deadline. Whether to extend is the seller's call; the cooperation language in Section 12.2(a) and the seller's own interest in a higher value both help the ask.
2. **Renegotiate.** A price change or seller credit only happens on a signed addendum, and the lender gets a copy. Tradeoff: any number between $538,000 and $575,000 is the buyers' and seller's decision. Meeting halfway would be $575,000 minus $18,500 = $556,500. Any credit is subject to what the lender allows at the appraised value.
3. **Cover the gap.** The buyers keep the price and bring more cash. Tradeoff: the lender bases the loan on the lower of price and appraised value, so the extra cash is at least the $37,000 gap; Example Lending Group confirms the exact figure.
4. **Cancel under the Appraisal Condition.** Written notice to the seller with a complete copy of the appraisal by 5:00 PM Tuesday, November 10. The $8,000 earnest money is released to the buyers. Tradeoff: they lose the house.

If the extension isn't signed by Tuesday at 5:00 PM, the buyers have to choose between options 2 to 4 by then, even with the request pending.
