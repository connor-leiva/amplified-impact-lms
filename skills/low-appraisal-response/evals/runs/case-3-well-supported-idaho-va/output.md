## Options for the Buyers

1. **Renegotiate.** Ask the seller to lower the price, in writing (Section 7 allows "a new Purchase Price or other terms"). The price at the VA value would be $441,000; halfway would be $455,000 minus $7,000 = $448,000, with the buyers paying the remaining $7,000 from their own cash. Tradeoff: the seller may say no; any change goes to the lender, who confirms how the $5,000 seller-paid closing costs fit.
2. **Cover the gap.** Keep $455,000 and pay at least $14,000 from their own cash, not borrowed (VA rule). Tradeoff: cash out of pocket on a price above the VA value.
3. **Cancel.** Written notice to the seller by 5:00 PM local time, Wednesday, November 18, under Section 7, and the $2,500 earnest money is returned. The VA escape clause separately says they won't forfeit earnest money or have to buy while the price exceeds the VA value. Tradeoff: they lose the house. Have your broker confirm the notice.
4. **Request a reconsideration anyway.** VA lets the Veteran ask in writing after the NOV (below). Tradeoff: it's unlikely to change the value, and VA's review takes up to 5 business days (up to 20 if VA does a field review), which can run past November 18. If they do it, decide on an extension with the seller first.

If the buyers ask about a new appraisal or a different lender: VA's handbook says that after the NOV is issued, "no duplicate appraisals may be requested for the same Veteran on the same property during the validity period of the NOV." Send any follow-up questions to the loan officer.

## What a Reconsideration Request Would Need

There are no grounds in these files, so no request is drafted. A VA request would need at least one of these:
- a fact the report got wrong about the subject, with a source (the MLS, a county record, a permit), or
- a closed sale, on or before 10/28/2026, that is closer or more similar than Comparables 1 to 4 and wasn't already considered.

**VA's process** (VA Pamphlet 26-7, Chapter 10, Topic 22): after the NOV, the Veteran "may request reconsideration of value in writing by contacting the RLC of jurisdiction" (the VA Regional Loan Center), or the request can go through the lender. Market data is "encouraged, but not required," and "a market data grid is not required." VA staff review within 5 business days; a field review, if used, within 20 business days. If an increase is supported, VA issues an amended NOV.

If you find a qualifying sale, the request would carry, for each sale: [street address], [sales price], [date of sale], [gross living area], [a copy of the listing], and why it's closer or more similar than the comparable it would replace. It goes from the buyers to the VA Regional Loan Center through Sample Federal Home Lending, never to the appraiser.
