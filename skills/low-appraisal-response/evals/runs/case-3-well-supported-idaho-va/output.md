## Options for the Buyers

1. **Renegotiate.** Ask the seller to lower the price to $441,000, or meet partway (halfway would be $455,000 minus $7,000 = $448,000), in a signed written agreement that goes to the lender. Tradeoff: the seller may say no, and the well-supported report is the buyers' strongest point in that conversation: the next VA or conventional buyer's appraisal would lean on the same sales.
2. **Cover the gap.** Keep the price and pay the difference. Under VA rules, when the price exceeds the reasonable value, the difference "must be made in cash from the borrower's own resources," so at least $14,000 of the buyers' own money, not borrowed. Tradeoff: cash out of pocket; Sample Federal Home Lending confirms the exact cash to close and how the $5,000 seller-paid closing costs still apply.
3. **Cancel.** Written notice to the seller by 5:00 PM local time on November 18 under the appraisal contingency, with the $2,500 earnest money returned; the VA escape clause also protects the earnest money when the price exceeds the reasonable value. Tradeoff: they lose the house.
4. **Request a VA reconsideration anyway.** Tradeoff: time and hope against a report the data supports. If they insist, see the next section, and keep the November 18 deadline in view.

The buyers decide. Don't present option 4 as likely to work.

## What a Reconsideration Would Need

There are no grounds in these files, so there's no request to draft. A VA request would need either a fact the report got wrong or a closed sale, on or before 10/28/2026, that's closer or more similar than the four used and wasn't already considered.

If you find one:
- **Who and how:** after the Notice of Value, the Veteran "may request reconsideration of value in writing by contacting the RLC of jurisdiction" (the VA Regional Loan Center), or through Sample Federal Home Lending. VA staff review within 5 business days; a field review, when used, within 20 business days. If an increase is supported, VA issues an amended Notice of Value.
- **What to include for each sale:** street address, sales price, date of sale, gross living area, and the listing if it was listed. Market data is "encouraged, but not required," and no grid is required.
- **Fields:** Chris Example and Robin Example; 3318 Placeholder Pine Ct, Exampleburg, ID 83000; effective date 10/28/2026; appraiser Jesse Imaginary, Notreal Appraisal LLC; [date of request]; [the new fact or sale, with its MLS number and why it's closer or more similar].
- Nothing goes to the appraiser directly.
