# Drafting Replies, Forwards and Follow-Ups

Every draft is for the leader to read, change and send. Nothing is sent by this skill.

## Rules for Every Draft

- **One message per person.** When two replies or notes go to the same person, combine them into one draft. A forward stays one per forwarded email, with a short note on top. Show To, Cc (only when needed), Subject, then the body.
- **Subjects:** a reply stays in its thread, so its subject is the thread's ("Reply in thread: [subject as it appears]"). Forwards and new emails get a new subject in Title Case, every word capitalized, naming the property or topic: "Lender Conditions For 77 Example Orchard Ln," "Holiday Party Date Is Set."
- **The point first.** The first sentence says what the reader needs to know or do.
- **Short.** Two to five short paragraphs, usually under 150 words. A forward note is one to three lines on top of the original.
- **Facts only from the inputs.** Dates, times, amounts, addresses and names come from the threads, transcripts or roster. Anything missing is a [placeholder] in square brackets: [decision], [time and place], [attach: listing presentation template], [policy answer], [new date].
- **Dates, not day counts.** Write the date ("Monday, November 9"), not a count you worked out ("four days before"). A wrong count in a money or deadline email does real damage.
- **No em dashes.** Use a comma, a colon or a period.
- **Plain words.** None of "stunning," "dream home," "don't hesitate to reach out," "I hope this email finds you well," "circle back," "touch base."
- **Voice:** the user's voice profile when one is in the conversation. Without one: warm, plain and direct. Sign with the leader's first name, or [Your Name].
- **No guarantees** of price, timing, approval or outcome.
- **Keep private things private.** Never mention an agent's health, family, pay, performance or personal situation in anything that goes to a client, a vendor or another agent.

## Reply

Answer the question, or give the decision with a [decision] placeholder when it's the leader's call. One ask, if any, with a date.

## Forward to a Teammate

```
To: [teammate]
Subject: [Title Case subject naming the property or topic]

[Name], [what this is] for [property or client]. [What you need them to do] by [date from the email, or a rule date].
[Anything they need to know that isn't in the email below, from the inputs only.]
Thanks,
[Leader first name]

(original email below)
```

A forward carries the whole original email. Forward only when everything in it can go to that person. When the original holds something that must not travel (a lead's remark about who they want as neighbors, an agent's private matter), write a new email instead, with "(new note, not a forward: [reason])" above it, and give the recipient what they need from the inputs.

For a lead: the lead's name, how they reached us, and their property criteria, timing, price range and financing as stated, plus what they asked for (a showing, a call). Never what they said about who they want as neighbors. If the lead's own message includes such a remark, the note is a new email with the lead's email and phone as given, not a forward.

For a lender condition or contract email: name the file and say the details are in the email below. Don't restate amounts, balances or account details in the note.

## Meeting Recap (One Per Team Meeting)

```
To: [the meeting's team attendees]
Subject: [Meeting Name] Recap: Who Owns What

Thanks, everyone. Here's what we each took on:
- [Owner]: [commitment], by [day, date]
- ...
Waiting on: [outside person], [what], by [date]. [Team member] is following up.
[Leader first name]
```

Leave out every sensitive item and anything said "between us." Leave out commitments made only to the leader in private. Transactions items that involve contract dates say "confirm dates with [transactions team member]."

## Follow-Up (Only When Overdue)

```
Subject: [Title Case subject] (or reply in thread)

Hi [Name], checking on [the specific thing] you mentioned [when]. [Why it matters now, from the inputs.] Could you send it by [date]?
Thanks,
[Leader first name]
```

Don't draft a follow-up for anything not yet due. List it instead.

## Delivering an Unkept Promise

- When the inputs hold the thing promised (a date someone confirmed, a file someone else sent), deliver it: "Here's [the thing]." Add an [attach: file name] line for any attachment from another email.
- When they don't, write a short note that owns the delay in one clause and gives a firm [new date] or a [placeholder] for the content: "You're right, I owe you the template. [attach: listing presentation template]" or "I didn't get to your CMA yet. You'll have my notes by [new date]."
- Never invent the deliverable, the names of vendors, a number or a document.

## Wire Fraud Alert (Internal)

```
To: [transactions team], [agent on the file]
Subject: Possible Wire Fraud Email On [Property]: Do Not Forward

Team, an email came in [day and time] claiming to change the wiring instructions for [property]. Red flags: [list]. Please don't forward it, reply to it or use any number or link in it.
[Agent], please call [client] today and remind them not to wire based on any email. Before any money moves, verify by phone with title at a number you already have.
[Leader first name]
```

No account numbers, routing numbers, phone numbers or links from the suspicious email, anywhere.

## Client Heads-Up (Optional, After a Wire Fraud Flag)

Two or three sentences: our team and the title company will never change wiring instructions by email; before sending any money, call the title company at the number in your closing paperwork. No numbers, no links, no attachment.

## Creating Drafts in Gmail or Outlook

Only when the user asks and the connector allows drafts. Create replies inside their thread, one draft per thread, and list each draft with its link. A message that combines two replies to one person goes in the thread of its first item; say which. New notes (alerts, recaps, lead notes) are new drafts. Never send, even if asked in the same breath: tell them the drafts are ready to review. If the connector can only read, say so and leave the drafts in the chat.

## Check Before Sending

List, for each draft: every date and time, every dollar amount, every name and email address it goes to, every attachment the leader needs to add, and every [placeholder] to fill. Add "Confirm the recipient" for every forward, and, when there was a high-risk email, "Confirm nothing from the flagged email was forwarded."
