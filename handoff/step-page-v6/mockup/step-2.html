How a week runs
  1. Before the live build, by Tuesday night, your operator runs that week’s skill and the owner reads what it finds.
  2. Wednesday is the live build, 90 minutes, 12:00 to 1:30 PM MT: the question, live reads, the hire lab and the commit.
  3. After the build, your hire’s first run lands and the owner signs its seat card.
  4. Office hours follow the live build, 60 minutes, to get any setup unstuck.
  5. The checkpoint closes the week: the signed seat card, a screenshot of the first run and the minutes the hire took over.

Reference · How a week runs

Every week has the same rhythm

One skill a week, one live build on Wednesday, and one hire that starts working the next morning.

Your operator runs that week’s skill before the live build, by Tuesday night, and the owner reads what it finds. You don’t need to understand every line yet. The goal is that both of you show up Wednesday with the findings open. Week 1’s skill is Map Your Spend, and you can see what it reads before you run it.

TipBlock 30 minutes on Tuesday afternoon for the owner’s read. It’s the part that makes Wednesday fast.

Heads upHires read and draft only. Nothing sends, and nothing changes your books or your CRM.

Doing it yourself?If the owner is also the operator, do both reads in one sitting.

The live build, step by step

  1. The questionWhat this week’s hire should take off someone’s desk.
  2. Live readsWe run the skill together and read what it finds.
  3. The hire labYour operator builds the hire while we watch.
  4. The commitThe owner signs the seat card.
“A hire is a skill that runs on a schedule and leaves a draft on one person’s desk.”The definition we use all course

Do

  • Run the skill before Wednesday
  • Bring the findings to the build
  • Sign the seat card the same day

Don’t

  • Give a hire send or edit access
  • Skip the owner’s read
  • Change the seat card after it’s signed
The checkpoint closes the week: signed seat card, first-run screenshot, minutes taken over.
