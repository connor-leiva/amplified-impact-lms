# The $37 skill buyer inside the LMS

**Status:** approved by Connor on Nov 24, 2026 ("See the AI Accelerator is fine.", "Show them now", "Light only is fine") and built in PR #252 (D-241). This folder is the design, and the build matches it (see "Parity").

## The base mockup

Connor's `lms-mockup.zip` (`index.html`, `ai-motion.css`, the Geist fonts and the academy logo) is already in the repo. It is byte for byte `handoff/lms-v3/mockup/`: `index.html` sha256 `ab62643b…d9d1d`, the approved LMS v3 mockup, revision v7, D-225. So it is not copied a second time. This folder extends it, and leaves every file of it as it is.

- **Shell and components.** The same sidebar, top bar, account trigger, hero, lists, cards, chips, segmented control, breadcrumbs, icons and motion.
- **Stylesheets.** The mockup's two stylesheets, linked as they are: `handoff/lms-v3/src/lms.tokens.css` and `lms.css`, generated from `index.html`. The app already ships these two files byte for byte.
- **What the buyer pages add.** `buyer.css` is built only from the mockup's own tokens (`--sp-*`) and classes, with no new colour, font, radius or motion value. The app ships it byte for byte (`apps/web/src/routes/learn/buyer/buyer.css`; a test holds the checksum).
- **Light only,** as the v3 member pages are (D-226).
- **Data.** `buyer-data.js` is generated by `tools/make-data.ts` from two sources, so the mockup and the build say the same words:
  - the real catalog, the library's lead copy and the guided setups;
  - the build's own buyer content module (`apps/web/src/routes/learn/buyer/content.ts`).

## Open it

```bash
npx http-server . -p 4455
```

Run that from the repo root, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4455/handoff/lms-mockup/buyer.html>. The black MOCKUP bar switches what was bought (an owner skill, an agent skill, or two skills) and which of the six pages is shown.

```bash
pnpm --filter @ai/api exec tsx ../../handoff/lms-mockup/tools/make-data.ts
```

That rebuilds `buyer-data.js`. It needs `apps/web/dist-private/skill-guides.json`, which `pnpm --filter @ai/web build:fixture` writes.

## The pages

| View | Address in the LMS | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `#home` | `/learn` | Their skill first, as the hero: Open the Setup Guide, with Claude and ChatGPT downloads side by side. Then "Also in the Academy", every locked area with what it is and which plan opens it. The one upgrade card. Three skills from their own set to open next. |
| `#library` | `/learn/skills` | "Your Skill" on top, with its lead (the problem, what it does, who it's for), the Setup Guide and both downloads. Then the upgrade band, with a count of what it opens. Then every skill by set: yours, free, and locked to preview, five per set until Show All. |
| `#skill-<id>` | `/learn/skills/:slug` (locked) | The skill's lead and its real setup steps, Claude and ChatGPT as equal tabs. The hero offers the skill's Insiders plan first, and "Get This Skill, $37" as the second choice beside it. |
| `#training` | `/learn/training` | Weekly Live Training, locked: Upgrade to the Agent Plan, $117/Month. The 4-week rotation with its real skills, and what the Agent Plan includes. |
| `#office-hours` | `/learn/office-hours` | Office Hours, locked: Thursdays, 12:00 PM MT, 60 minutes, recorded. The buyer's Insiders plan. |
| `#accelerator`, `#week-<n>` | `/learn/week/:n` | The AI Accelerator, locked: Week 0's eight steps, then a hire a week from the Accelerator's own page, and "See the AI Accelerator". |

The sidebar is the members' sidebar.
- **Plan lines.** Where a member's sidebar shows the program and cohort, it shows "Amplified Skill" and "1 skill bought".
- **Nav.** Home, Skill library, then Office hours and Weekly training with the weeks' lock, then the AI Accelerator's nine weeks, locked.
- **Top bar.** It keeps their skill one click away ("Your skill · Team P&L Review · Open the Setup Guide"), hidden on Home, whose hero is that skill.
- **Account menu.** The members' menu (Account, Theme, Sign out). Account has Manage billing and the skills bought.
- **Several skills.** A buyer of two or more sees the latest purchase first.

## Parity

```bash
(cd apps/web && VITE_DATA_SOURCE=http npx vite build --mode parity --outDir dist-buyer --emptyOutDir)
```

```bash
pnpm --filter @ai/api exec tsx ../../handoff/lms-mockup/tools/pairs.ts --out <dir> [--b64] [--diff]
```

The first command builds the production app (http data, both learner flags unset) into `apps/web/dist-buyer`.

The second captures each page of the mockup beside the same page of that build, at 1280 and 390. It answers every `/api` call with the mockup's buyer, the real catalog and the guides' real steps, and reports the share of pixels that differ. The pairs and that table are in `docs/pr-review/252/`.

`tools/capture.mjs` captures the mockup alone.

## Rules

- **Utah Life.** Nothing changes. An access-group member never sees these pages; their `/skill-library` stays as it is, with no upsell or buy button.
- **Agent Plan, Team Plan, Insiders and Accelerator members.** No change.
- **Who sees these pages.** Only a learner with at least one $37 skill, no plan and no access group (`skillOnlyBuyer`).
- **Copy.**
  - Headings and buttons are Title Case. There are no em dashes. Times say MT.
  - **Numbers.** Every number is computed from the catalog (48 locked, 33 for Agent Insiders) or quoted from the pricing page ("7 AI employees built with you", "8 live builds, 90 minutes, Wednesdays", "With at least 5 running by Feb 3").
  - **Hires.** They read and draft: "Every hire reads and drafts. Nothing is sent without you."
- **Claude and ChatGPT.** Their buttons and setup tabs are the same component, side by side. No page here shows a prompt.
- **Links.** No link is in code. Each upgrade button's href is its cart's `PAYMENT_LINK_*` from `GET /api/offer`:

  | Cart | Variable |
  |---|---|
  | `insiders-monthly` | `PAYMENT_LINK_INSIDERS_MONTHLY` |
  | `insiders-agent-monthly` | `PAYMENT_LINK_INSIDERS_AGENT_MONTHLY` |
  | `agent-monthly` | `PAYMENT_LINK_AGENT_MONTHLY` |

  While a link is unset, its button draws disabled. "Get This Skill, $37" is today's in-app checkout.
